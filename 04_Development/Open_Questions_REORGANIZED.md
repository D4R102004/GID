# ✅ RESOLVED QUESTIONS

*Archive. Answers are final unless explicitly revisited.*

---

**[✅ RESOLVED — 2025-01-29] Does Gallan survive Arc 1?**
→ YES. Gallan survives Arc 1. He makes a deal with Diominiciel (Pristania) to keep the kids safe and leaves the island powering a teleporter. The kids believe he was captured — dramatic irony. He is alive and free at Arc 1's end.

---

**[✅ RESOLVED — 2025-01-29] How does Glayne discover the island?**
→ He always knew. Gallan told him, or Glayne simply knew his brother had retreated there. For 15 years, Glayne considered the island Gallan's self-imposed exile and found satisfaction in it. He never needed to "find" it.

---

**[✅ RESOLVED — 2025-01-29] What triggers Glayne to act after 15 years?**
→ Glayne learns Gallan has children and is living with some degree of happiness. To Glayne, Gallan's exile was meant to be suffering — not redemption. Learning Gallan found joy through Arjan and Lopney breaks his tolerance. He sends Yurisha to kill the kids, intending to destroy everything Gallan rebuilt.

---

**[✅ RESOLVED — 2025-01-29] Who placed Arjan and Lopney in the cave? What was the beam of light?**
→ Nobody placed them. The beam of light IS the reincarnation event. When Meiji killed Kami 18 years prior, his power fragmented — 30% became Arjan (positive), 30% became Lopney (negative), 40% remained in the corpse. The cave and island were not chosen. Gallan simply happened to be there. No hidden protector, no grand design — pure consequence of a god's death.

---

**[✅ RESOLVED — 2025-01-29] Does Perlereina sanction Gallan's capture?**
→ YES. Perlereina and Glayne are the only two people in Pristania who know Gallan is being held. Her motivation is ideological: Gallan is a deserter and betrayer who abandoned his role as Hirohiko's Apostle. To Perlereina, who considers betrayal unforgivable, this is principled justice — not personal. Key tension: Glayne's motivation is personal revenge. Perlereina's is ideological principle. Same action, two entirely different justifications. Neither fully knows the other's real reason.
