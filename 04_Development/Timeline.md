## THE ANCIENT ERA - RYAKUJIN PROJECTS

### **Centuries Before Present**
- **Kiryuuin Creation** - Ryakujin create the Kiryuuin family as perfect soldiers
- Kiryuuin are abandoned, form assassin dynasty over centuries
- Origins forgotten/concealed by descendants

### **Many Years Before Present**
- **Bakuzoku Creation** - Ryakujin create Bakuzoku as biological bomb weapons

## KAMI'S BIRTH - 73 YEARS BEFORE ARC 1

### **The Ryakujin Facility**
- **Three prototypes created:**
  - **Zenshin (善神)** / Magnus Dei - "The Positive God" (Kami)
  - **Jashin (邪神)** / Opus Dei - "The Negative God"
  - **Megami (女神)** / Mei Dei - "The Neutral Force"
- Design: Positive and negative clash to grow stronger, neutral mediates
- The three develop emotional bonds
- Romantic tension between Jashin and Megami
- Zenshin becomes jealous

### **The Betrayal**
- Chief scientist **Hide** manipulates Zenshin
- Promises to eliminate Jashin
- Sets up the Three Cities Test

## THE FACILITY MASSACRE - 55 YEARS BEFORE ARC 1

**Kami's Age:** 18 years old

### **The Three Cities Test**

**Jashin's City:**
- Ordered to kill target, complies gleefully
- Ambushed by Ryakujin agents (Hide's assassination attempt)
- Kills agents in self-defense
- Feels betrayed, destroys entire city in rage
- Returns to facility, begins massacring scientists

**Megami's City:**
- Ordered to kill, **cannot** (pacifism hardcoded)
- Ryakujin deem her a failure, abandon her
- Shot in the head by police, **dies**

**Zenshin's City:**
- Refuses orders, destroys transmission device
- Rushes to save Megami (arrives too late)

### **The Facility Confrontation**
- Zenshin finds Jashin killing Hide
- Hide confesses the conspiracy
- Zenshin and Jashin clash over Megami's corpse
- Zenshin losing badly

### **The Sealing**
- Hide reveals sealing technique using Zenshin's power
- Zenshin seals Jashin into coma
- **Seal condition:** Works as long as Zenshin's power exists

### **Hide's Final Lie**
- Reveals Resurrection ability
- **THE LIE:** "After 2 resurrections, you cease to exist"
- **THE TRUTH:** After 2 resurrections, the POWER ceases (user survives)
- Zenshin kills Hide in fury

### **The Choice**
- Zenshin uses **1st resurrection** on Megami (1 use remaining)
- Lies to her: "Ryakujin killed Jashin, we destroyed facility together"
- They bury Jashin's comatose body
- Escape together

## THE DUEL - 18 YEARS BEFORE ARC 1

**Kami's Age:** 55 years old (dies this day)

### **Setup**
- **Meiji (age 17)** founds **Free**
- Ideological opposition to Pristania
- Publicly challenges Kami to a duel

### **The Kamigami's Ages During Duel**
- **Diominiciel:** 27 years old
- **Hirohiko:** 24 years old (has recruited Apostle **Gallan Byronstrike**)
- **Perlereina:** 18 years old

### **The Duel**
- Location: Stadium (public to factions, hidden from civilians)
- **Opening:** Kami dominates with experience
- **Turning Point:** Meiji's Ryakujin contingency activates (he doesn't know this)
  - Massive strength/speed boost
  - Hidden programming triggers against god-tier powers
- **Reversal:** Meiji gains upper hand
- **Climax:** Kami grows desperate, fights ruthlessly, loses composure
- **Outcome:** Kami dies at age 55

### **Immediate Aftermath**
- **Kami's power fragments:**
  - 30% → **Arjan** (positive reincarnation, born in cave)
  - 30% → **Lopney** (negative reincarnation, born in cave)
  - 40% → Remains in Kami's corpse (preserved by Kamigami)
- **Jashin's status:** Still sealed in coma (seal holds because Kami's power exists in fragments)
- **Rajin's reaction:**
  - Revenge achieved, feels emptiness
  - Mourns for the Kamigami (now fatherless)
  - Remains in Free but becomes wandering, emotionally absent
- **Angel Velasco** (Kami's secret chosen successor and lover, about 25) was to be presented to Pristania **three days later**. He never is: cut off from Pristania, beaten at its gate, he ends up in debt and on the street (see ANGEL_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

## THE PEACE ERA - 15 YEARS BEFORE ARC 1 TO PRESENT

### **15 Years Before Arc 1 - The Treaty**
- **Peace treaty signed**
- Jitsumoto serves as neutral mediator (Shogun Shigeru)
- Fragile peace established
- Cold war tension persists

### **Immediately After Treaty - Hirohiko vs Siblings Conflict**
- **Hirohiko vs. Diominiciel and Perlereina conflict** (specific events TBD)
- Hirohiko loses memories
- Imprisoned by Diominiciel and Perlereina
- Later escapes, wanders with amnesia

### **Gallan's Defection**
- Shortly after treaty is signed
- Disappears to isolated island seeking redemption
- Uses **Mental Erasure** to hide

### **Gallan Discovers Arjan & Lopney**
- Shortly after arriving on island
- Finds them in a cave (babies, ~3 months old)
- Beam of light had come from cave
- Recognizes Arjan has abilities (supernatural)
- Takes them in, raises them as his own
- Never reveals his past

### **10 Years Before Arc 1 - Jashin Possesses Hirohiko**
- **Jashin's consciousness** (40% of his power) escapes comatose body
- **Possesses Hirohiko** while imprisoned at Pristania
- Hirohiko moved to **Kamino**
- 60% of Jashin remains with comatose body in Pristania chamber
- Hirohiko unaware of possession
- **Jashin is the mastermind** pulling strings (plot twist for Arc 6)

### **5 Years Before Arc 1 - Free Relocates**
- **Pristania attacks Free's original headquarters**
- **Gabriella** (Tairin's daughter) captured
- Free presumes her dead
- Gabriella actually held captive at Pristania HQ for several months, then escapes with Uragaeshi's help and hides in Vista Alegre
- **Gabriella arrives hurt at the Vista Alegre hospital and is treated by Claudia Whannell** (then a nursing trainee/aide, ~16, not yet in Pristania). They become best friends ("Misty" and "Metal"). Claudia joins Pristania afterward
- **Free builds new headquarters** in the island cliffs (Gallan's island)
- Hidden from Gallan (doesn't know it's there)
- Camouflaged within cliff system

### **About 4-5 Years Before Arc 1 - Perlereina Finds Angel**
- Perlereina notices a handkerchief on Kami's body (the only memento of Angel's mother, which he gave Kami), traces Angel's mother to her death at the orphanage, then Angel himself, and makes him her secret spy "to honor her father"
- Author's timing: **12-13 years after Kami's death, after Gabriella escapes and before the protagonists reach Vista Alegre**. Gabriella escaped about 4.5 years before Arc 1 and Kami died 18 years before, so the gap works out to about 13-14 years
- She assigns him to tail **Derek Guzman**, a Vista Alegre police commander. Only Perlereina and Diominiciel know Angel exists

### **Peace Period Developments**
- Pristania declared illegal by Vista Alegre
- Both factions operate in hiding
- Free membership reduces during peacetime
- Perlereina shifts policy: Hunt defectors (unlike Kami's lenience)
- Hirohiko (as Dr. Araki/Gyrus) lives peacefully with amnesia in Kamino

## AGE CALCULATION FORMULA

**To find current age (Arc 1):**
- Character born X years before Arc 1 = X years old in Arc 1
- Examples:
  - Meiji born ~35 years before Arc 1 = ~35 years old in Arc 1
  - Diominiciel born 45 years before Arc 1 = 45 years old in Arc 1
  - Perlereina born 36 years before Arc 1 = 36 years old in Arc 1

**To find age during past events:**
- Years before Arc 1 that character was born - Years before Arc 1 of event = Age during event
- Example: Perlereina in Arc 1 is 36 (born 36 years before Arc 1)
  - The Duel was 18 years before Arc 1
  - 36 - 18 = 18 years old during the Duel ✓
