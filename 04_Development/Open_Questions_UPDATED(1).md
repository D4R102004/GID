---

**[✅ RESOLVED - 2025-01-29] Does Perlereina know Glayne captured Gallan? Did she authorize it?**
→ **YES on both counts.** Perlereina authorized the capture. She and Glayne are the ONLY two people in Pristania who know Gallan is being held.
→ **Her motivation:** Gallan is a deserter and betrayer in her eyes — he abandoned his role as Hirohiko's Apostle and defected during the war. To Perlereina, who values loyalty above all and considers betrayal unforgivable, this is not personal — it is ideological justice.
→ **Key tension:** Glayne captures Gallan for *personal* revenge. Perlereina authorizes it on *principle*. Same action, two completely different justifications. Neither of them knows the other's real reason is different from their own.
*(Source: user decision, consistent with PERLEREINA_DEI_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md — "betrayal = death, no exceptions")*

[✅ RESOLVED] How does Glayne discover the island?
→ He always knew. Gallan told him or Glayne simply knew his brother had retreated there. For 15 years, Glayne considered the island Gallan's self-imposed exile and found satisfaction in it. He never needed to "find" it.

[✅ RESOLVED] What triggers Glayne to act after 15 years?
→ Glayne learns Gallan has children and is living with some degree of happiness on the island. This breaks his tolerance. To Glayne, Gallan's exile was supposed to be suffering — not redemption. Learning Gallan found joy through Arjan and Lopney triggers his rage. He sends Yurisha to kill the kids, intending to destroy everything Gallan rebuilt for himself.

[✅ RESOLVED] Who placed Arjan and Lopney in the cave? What was the beam of light?
→ Nobody placed them. The beam of light IS the reincarnation event. When Meiji killed Kami 18 years prior, his power fragmented — 30% became Arjan (positive), 30% became Lopney (negative), 40% remained in the corpse. The cave and island were not chosen. Gallan simply happened to be there. No hidden protector, no grand design. Pure consequence of a god's death.
