## **OVERVIEW**

**Arc Title:** 5 Souls to the Rescue
**Arc Boss:** Mauricio Fangio, mayor of Kamino and God's Flag champion (MAURICIO_FANGIO_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
**Antagonists on page:** Perlereina, Glayne, Sea (S34); Goro (Fukusei) at the close
**Setting:** The sea route to Vista Alegre → **Kamino** (town founded by Kami) and the invisible Ryakujin facility outside it → the road to Vista Alegre
**Timeline:** Departure on Yurisha's ship (end of Arc 2) → shipwreck → days in Kamino → the God's Flag → the road

### **Core Premise**

The five sail for Vista Alegre: the twins to save Gallan, the trio (Yonoa, Lyzander, Ichiro) to save Meiji. Pristania ambushes them at sea; Perlereina's attack wrecks the ship and diverts them to **Kamino**, a closed town that worships Kami and hates Free and Vista Alegre. Yonoa is near death. With no way out, they must **win the God's Flag**, the race Kami created, whose winner gets one wish from the mayor: a ride to Vista Alegre.

**The arc's tension:** a race arc in enemy territory. Kamino is not Pristania (Pristania never showed itself there after Kami's death) but believes in everything Pristania claims to stand for. It ends with Kamino falling under Pristania.

### **Arc Structure (author's rule, see Quick Ref)**
- **Opens** with the arc boss flashback: **Masaki Oku** (Mauricio's girlfriend, Kami's first chosen successor).
- **After the boss's defeat** (the twins win the God's Flag): **Interlude 3 of Zenshin, Megami and Jashin — the three cities test.**
- Closes with the Kamigami and Meiji in Pristania.

### **PART 1 — AT SEA**

**1. The Ship**
- The five sail toward Vista Alegre on Yurisha's ship. Getting to know them:
  - **Yonoa** is the de facto leader, bossy; **cat-and-mouse with Ichiro** (he means no harm: it's how he is).
  - **Lyzander** is hit by depression but glad to see Yonoa again after all these years; **his feelings for her show**.
  - **Ichiro** has feelings for **Lopney** and starts seeking out **Arjan** to get close to his sister; the two interact a lot.
  - Bonds already formed: Arjan, Ichiro and Lyzander (planning the rescue in Arc 2); Lopney and Yonoa (prison).

**2. S34 (Sea)**
- **The plan (revealed later):** S34, Perlereina's beloved robot, carried her there. (a) S34 attacks to distract and hold the heroes at sea, then leaves; (b) Perlereina teleports one of Free inside S34, which takes the prisoner to Pristania; (c) Perlereina finishes the rest and blows up the ship. **Goal: capture Lyzander, Meiji's son**, and torture him before Meiji so Meiji "deactivates" Protos's sphere.
- A flying armored robot attacks. On deck: Yonoa, Lyzander, Lopney, Ichiro. **Lopney deduces** Pristania would watch the route; **Yonoa flies off** to face it and drags it away.
- **S34 disobeys:** wanting to show Perlereina how much it loves her, it waits for Yonoa to chase it and leads her where it thinks it can win.
- **Yonoa vs. S34:** it provokes her as a proud Pristania robot. A savage fight; Yonoa takes the lead, wrecks its side cannons; no missile or turbine stops the Logia. **Last defiance:** it has Perlereina, while Yonoa has no father, was betrayed by her best friend, and will die alone. Yonoa answers with tornado after tornado until only the core remains, and smashes that too. **S34 dies** (first of Perlereina's losses).
- Destroying it, Yonoa finds **Lyzander unconscious inside**. She carries him back underwater.

**3. Perlereina on the Ship (parallel to beat 2)**
- Lopney, Ichiro and Lyzander debate what to do; **Perlereina walks slowly out of a portal**. Lyzander recognizes her and warns the others; **she teleports him away** (into S34).
- Lopney's hunch: attack now. A portal sends her back to where she started; Perlereina comes through behind her with a crushing blow; **Ichiro takes it**, smashing through the deck wall, out of sight.
- Lopney counters; Perlereina stops her easily. "I don't recognize you." Lopney: "Where does Pristania have my father?" Perlereina confirms they have him; she'll take her to him, "not my problem if you drown on the way."
- **Arjan** attacks from behind; one blow almost goes through his chest. **Perlereina is surprised how resilient he is.** Lopney, enraged, attacks and is thrown back.
- Ichiro, near dying, speaks through an illusion and helps: he makes Perlereina believe **Lopney's Nouryoku is invisibility**. Her experience wins again and again; Arjan joins and is beaten.
- **Lopney's best technique**, a falling kick hidden by illusions: Perlereina blocks with her arms, **not a scratch**. Lopney freezes in shock; a punch to the stomach sends her flying, taking Arjan with her and destroying the ship's rear half.
- **Playing dead:** Ichiro tells the twins they're lost; fake death. They hold their breath; Ichiro spends his last strength so she can't hear their hearts. She decides to stab their hearts with a splinter anyway — **an alarm sounds on her clothes** (S34 in danger). She drops a **bomb** among the wreckage (**the reader sees it; no character does**) and leaves through a portal.

**4. The Explosion and the Raft**
- On her way back Yonoa sees Perlereina heading for S34's remains and fears the worst. She finds the ship half destroyed, her friends half dead.
- They recover; Ichiro climbs to the deck; they tell each other what happened. At the part where Perlereina leaves, **Yonoa senses something is off** — the ship explodes. Her tornadoes shield the others; **she is gravely wounded**.
- On floating planks: **Ichiro** alters her pain perception (anesthetic); **Lopney** gives first aid; **Arjan** lifts the plank with spheres into a raft; **Lyzander** propels it with his blue fire.
- **Perlereina** finds S34's remains, punches the island, leaves a huge **crater** (her gesture: "damn it"). She takes the **core, a sphere with the kanji 海**, and wears it as a **necklace** from now on. Back at the ship she finds only Yonoa's blood on the planks and **laughs**, believing them dead.
- They sight land, crash ashore and land sprawled on the beach, Yonoa still gravely wounded.

### **PART 2 — KAMINO**

**5. Arrival: Dr. Hero**
- **Dr. Hero** (Gyrus/Hirohiko, amnesiac) finds them on the beach (Jashin sensed Lopney and planted the urge) and takes them to his clinic. They distrust him but recognize he's a doctor. "Welcome to **Kamino**, the city founded by Kami."
- Bad luck: they know Kamino is Kami's town; instead of Vista Alegre they're trapped there with Yonoa half dead. The twins know Gallan is held by Pristania.
- **Goals:** stay hidden; find a way to Vista Alegre.

**6. The Plan: the God's Flag**
- Kamino is self-centered, has no way out (least of all to Vista Alegre), believes in Kami's ideology, **hates Free and Vista Alegre**.
- **The God's Flag:** a vehicle race, no Nouryoku, an ordinary race (like the Indianapolis 500). **Created by Kami, its first winner.** It represents human virtue born of sacrifice, so **the winner gets one wish from the mayor's office**. Arjan is a huge fan (Ch. 2: Gallan brought him photos).
- **Mauricio** is mayor and champion many times running; **Glayne** has lost to him for ~10 years, racing without anyone knowing he's Pristania.
- They have no car, and Arjan, who knows the race best, can't enter (Nouryoku). **Ichiro steals a car; Lopney will drive; Arjan rides hidden in the trunk** giving instructions.

### **PART 3 — LIFE IN KAMINO** *(order not decided)*

**7. The Ryakujin Facility (twins & Hirohiko)**
- They need a place to practice. **Hirohiko** takes the twins to the outskirts: "Do you see anything?" They do: **the destroyed Ryakujin facility**, **invisible and impassable** except to Kami's power. They'll practice there undetected.
- **Hirohiko reveals his past here:** he woke up here with no memory; no one else can see this place; his hunch about Lopney, a "connection" with the twins. A bond forms.
- On one visit, metal pieces **react to Arjan's face** and open a door to **monitors (the facility still monitors in real time)**:
  - **Subject # M-493+ — Zenshin** — Age: Kami's age at death — **Status: Dead**
  - **Subject # M-493− — Jashin** — Age: Jashin's current age — **Status: Sealed**
  - **Subject M-151 — Mei** — Age: Mei's — **Status: Stable**
  - **Subject M-150 — [blurred]** — Age: Meiji's — **Status: Stable**
- **Hide** appears: an abomination, face decomposed, wrapped like a zombie. The facility's chief, killed by Zenshin; before dying he injected **a substance derived from Kami and Jashin** he thought would save him; it turned him into a monster in lethargy. **He wakes sensing the twins** and repeats only **"Zenshin"** (never "Kami"). Shock: the monitor says Zenshin is dead.
- **Hide has Kami's powers**, but his beam fires **from him forward** (Kami's descends from the sky; Jashin's rises from the ground). He knocks out the twins and Hirohiko.
- As he moves to finish the twins, **Jashin possesses Hirohiko** and defeats Hide (saving himself too, and settling his grudge against Hide). Hirohiko is in a trance; he remembers only passing out. **No one knows how the monster fell.**
- The twins wake; **Hirohiko heals them** (healing spears) and explains. **Hide is dead, definitively.**

**8. The Clinic (Lyzander, Hirohiko, Yonoa)**
- Lyzander and Hirohiko care for Yonoa. **The children come to see "Dr. Hero."**
- **Kamino's history:** built on the **ruins of the city Jashin destroyed**, rebuilt by Kami; how he founded it, its first inhabitants, the cult, Kami's ideals, daily life. Lyzander begins to see **Kami may not have been such a bad person**, and understands a little of his father's pain as his killer. He learns Kamino's **visceral hatred of Meiji**. Hirohiko knows Lyzander is from Free and doesn't care.
- **Pristania as myth:** Kami built his family into children's stories. The kids argue about them like superheroes, by **hero names only** (no real names, no surnames), and only figures from Kami's lifetime:
  - **Kamigami:** Paru, Dyoni, Gyrus
  - **Apostles:** Elipse (with Paru), Disappear (with Gyrus)
  - **Emperors:** Kami, Fénix, Protos, Bellsaw, Aoi, Fukusei
- Public knowledge: one of the three Kamigami "isn't around." Lyzander maps Paru and Dyoni to faces; Gyrus and Disappear sound like old legend.
- **Lyzander reacts to "Protos" and "Fénix"** (his grandfather, dead days ago; Yonoa's father in the next bed), idolized as heroes of Kami. He already knew Kensuke was an Emperor: the blow is emotional. **This reaction is the misdirection** that lets "Disappear" and "Bellsaw" pass unmarked.
- **Hirohiko hears "Gyrus" and just smiles.**
- For anyone in Kamino, seeing an Apostle or Emperor would be a dream come true.

**9. Glayne & Perlereina at the Beach**
- On **the same Kamino beach where the heroes washed up**, Glayne invites Perlereina. The villains in another light.
- Glayne helps her bear the loss of S34 (necklace 海). Both believe the heroes drowned. She is frustrated they can't kill Meiji (the capture plan failed with Sea). Her human side with Glayne: what unites them, why the reader invests in them. She knows his whole vendetta (they're a couple; they tell each other everything).

**10. Lyon & Fukusei**
- **Lyon is in Kamino with Marsella** (the reader's shock: alive, well, with the traitor, free). Marsella wants him to see a city, and Perlereina, Glayne and the others are there; **Lyon wants to see the race**. **Goro is also there for the race.**
- Lyon notices a **Fukusei clone** tailing him and follows it to an abandoned house. Goro dissolves that clone with a snap ("You embarrass me of myself"). Lyon: "You just killed your own brother!" Goro dissolves all the clones: energy clones, all made by him. "You can call me… **Fukusei**."
- A fake "real Goro" vanishes; the clones beat Lyon ("I'm the real one!"). **"You have a lot of bodies, huh?" — "So?" — "That means more corpses."** A bomb barrage destroys the clones and brings down the roof; the real Goro falls on his backside. "Nice try." — "For you, right?" More clones from floor and ceiling nearly smother him; Lyon levels the place. Out of the rubble: **a dagger at his throat. Lyon recognizes it** (from Ichiro): **"You're a Kiryuin."**
- Goro senses Lyon isn't sure he belongs in Pristania; asks how things are with **Dyoni**, Perlereina and the rest. Lyon doesn't answer. Goro leaves him **his card**. **Lyon tells Marsella and gives her the card.**

**11. Ichiro at Mauricio's House**
- Ichiro wants to **sabotage** Mauricio; he enters hidden by illusions. He doesn't know **the car is alive**: he manipulates Mauricio's senses but not the car's. **The car gives him away** before he sabotages anything.
- An exchange showing **Mauricio's ideals** and **Ichiro's mentalist techniques** (he avoids arrest). Mauricio thinks he came to kill him; he sees a resemblance to **Goro** and guesses **Kiryuin** (an assassin with Nouryoku is almost surely one). Learning Ichiro is interested in the race, Mauricio decides **anyone can race in the race Kami designed** and lets him go.
- We see the bond between Mauricio and his car. **The car learns Ichiro's scent** (pays off in beat 13).

**12. Yonoa**
- No event: gravely wounded, she recovers little by little.

### **PART 4 — THE GOD'S FLAG (CLIMAX)**

**13. The Race**
- **Only 3 drivers** this time: no one in Kamino thinks they can beat Mauricio.
- Entry requires proving you're not a Nouryokusha: **the Selector**, an artifact that belonged to Kami (put your finger on it). **Glayne cheats** with a tiny invisible ellipse on his fingertip (ellipse encapsulation nullifies the Selector).
- Race names: **"Lost"** (Lopney, stolen car, Arjan in the trunk), **"Elipse"** (Glayne, motorized skates built by Bellsaw: the crowd is thrilled by the superhero name, hiding in plain sight), **"MasakiMan"** (Mauricio, his car). **Glayne doesn't recognize Lopney at first.**
- In the stands: **Marsella, Perlereina, Lyon** on one side; **Ichiro and Gyrus** on the other. Lyzander stays at the clinic with Yonoa. Goro is in the stadium.
- A very tense race; everyone plays their cards. **The twins win** (the boss is defeated).

**→ INTERLUDE 3: ZENSHIN, MEGAMI AND JASHIN — THE THREE CITIES TEST** *(right after the twins win: the boss is defeated)*

- Mauricio asks their wish: **"To go to Vista Alegre."** Glayne connects the dots, recognizes the twins and does the unthinkable: declares himself **Glayne Byronstrike, Apostle of Pristania**, proves it with his power, and tells the crowd these kids want to save Meiji. The crowd turns on them. (The twins: a man with **their surname**, from Pristania.)
- As the crowd is about to lynch them, **Mauricio lends them his car**. The twins escape the stadium. **Glayne and the crowd seize Mauricio.**

**14. The Clinic Standoff**
- During the race Ichiro spotted the Pristania group and left. **Lyon recognized him and followed him to the clinic.**
- **Lyon and Lyzander meet**, stunned. Lyon starts to explain; **Ichiro ambushes him with a knife**: he saw Lyon with Pristania. "I can explain. I didn't expect to see you here."
- **Perlereina and Marsella arrive.** Perlereina grabs Lyzander, happy to finally have her target. Marsella, devastated to see her son at a Kiryuin's knife, thanks Lyon for leading them to the enemy; Lyon says it wasn't like that.
- Ichiro: "Let Lyzander go or I kill Lyon." Perlereina: "He's Meiji's son. He's mine." **Marsella, with authority: "Let him go."** No fighting in the middle of Kamino. Perlereina obeys grudgingly. Ichiro tries to keep Lyon as hostage; **Perlereina reveals Lyon is a member of Pristania** (Lyzander didn't know). Lyon tries to deny it.
- **Yonoa comes out, recovered.** She sees **Marsella** and goes for her; **Perlereina** steps between them: before her stands the killer of her best friend (Yonoa was the only one she didn't see on the ship). A three-way stare-down.
- **Mauricio's car arrives with Arjan and Lopney** (it tracked **Ichiro's scent**), picks up Ichiro, Lyzander and Yonoa, and they're gone.

**15. The Chase**
- Marsella tends the cut on Lyon's neck. **Perlereina chases them through Kamino** with her portals. From the balconies people **connect the portals with "Paru"**, and knowing about the race, try to stop the heroes. They escape the city.
- Perlereina punches another **crater** and a curtain of smoke rises. Marsella arrives with Lyon; a heated argument.
- **Glayne** calms them and points out that Kamino is watching. He tells the people she is **Kami's daughter**, and **from now on Pristania will be with them**. The four leave.

### **PART 5 — THE ROAD**

**16. The Card**
- **Marsella gives Perlereina the card** and recommends **Goro**. Pristania hires Fukusei to kill the protagonists (deliberately not telling him Ichiro is a target: a loyalty test; GORO_KIRYUIN_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md).

**17. Fathers**
- On the long road to Vista Alegre the group stops and talks about **each one's father** (Gallan captured; Meiji captured; Shigeru dead; Goro alive). They tell Ichiro he should maybe be glad his father is alive and safe; Ichiro isn't in the mood.

**18. Goro vs. Ichiro**
- A clone finds the group while **Ichiro is on guard**; the clone, recognizing his master's son, approaches almost with joy: "Young master Ichiro! Come, I'll take you to your father." First meeting in three years. Goro's first reaction is happiness; Ichiro refuses; they fight.
- **Goro wins easily**, but Ichiro's determination stops him from finishing it. To a defeated Ichiro: **he'll be watching; he'll wait for him at his mansion in Vista Alegre.**

**19. Mauricio's Death**
- **Public execution by Glayne**, as punishment for helping Free; his own town condemns him as a traitor. **Last words: to Masaki.** (The car, far away with the heroes, learns of it in Vista Alegre: see Transition to Arc 4.)
- **The staging of a new power in Kamino:** the town now tied to Pristania and its bloody will.

**20. The Kamigami and Meiji**
- The Kamigami receive the news: **Goro found nothing** (he lied; he has never missed a contract before).
- The scene of the **Kamigami with Meiji** in Pristania. **Perlereina sees Meiji face to face for the first time since his capture** (she hadn't before): **"I have you now. HAHAHAHAHA"**.

**21. End of Arc**
- The protagonists drive on toward **Vista Alegre**.

## **CHARACTER ARCS**

**Arjan:** The fan reaches the race he only knew from photos, without Gallan, to save Gallan. Almost killed by Perlereina; wins the God's Flag from the trunk. His face opens the facility's door. Escapes a lynch mob thanks to the man who dies for granting him his wish.

**Lopney:** Deduces the ambush; her best technique leaves Perlereina without a scratch, a trauma that strikes at what she is (pure skill). Drives and wins the God's Flag as "Lost". The facility, Hide and Hirohiko's intrigue (her eyes like his) tie her to the mystery of her origin.

**Yonoa:** Destroys S34, the arc's first face of her rage (father, betrayal, solitude). Gravely wounded saving everyone; recovers off-stage. Faces Marsella and Perlereina at once.

**Lyzander:** Perlereina's capture target twice (S34, the clinic). Cares for Yonoa; through Kamino's eyes he begins to understand Kami and his father's pain. Hears his grandfather celebrated as a hero of Kami. Discovers Lyon is in Pristania.

**Ichiro:** The team's trickster: feelings for Lopney, cat and mouse with Yonoa. Nearly killed shielding Lopney. Fails to sabotage Mauricio; holds Lyon at knifepoint; loses to his father and is told to come home.

**Mauricio (arc boss):** The clean version of Kami's ideal. Grants the wish to his enemies because it's tradition, and dies for it at the hands of Kami's own organization, without his car.

**Perlereina:** The arc's real antagonist force. Loses Sea (first of her losses), wears its core. Her human side with Glayne. Her frustration with Meiji grows; brings Pristania into Kamino.

**Glayne:** Ten years losing to Mauricio. The tender partner at the beach; the Apostle who unmasks himself before a town and executes its mayor.

**Lyon:** Alive and with Marsella, finding his way in Pristania, not sure he belongs (Goro sees it). Fights Fukusei with a cold head. Unwittingly passes on the card that sends Goro after his old friends.

**Hirohiko (Dr. Hero):** Helps without knowing why; drawn to Lopney; possessed by Jashin without knowing it; swept away by the crowd's hatred.

## **THEMES**

1. **Kami's ideal without Pristania:** Kamino and Mauricio show what Kami's teachings look like lived honestly; the arc ends with Pristania taking it over.
2. **Myth vs. reality:** the town that dreamed of seeing an Apostle sees two, and both bring violence.
3. **Rules and cheating:** three cheats in a race without powers; the only one who plays fair loses everything.
4. **Fathers:** Gallan and Meiji captured, Shigeru dead, Goro alive and dangerous, Kami as father of a town; Lyzander inherits his father's guilt.
5. **Grief:** Mauricio chose no hatred after losing Masaki; Perlereina punches the earth; Zenshin destroys a city (Interlude 3).
