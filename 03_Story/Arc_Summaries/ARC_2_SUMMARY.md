## **OVERVIEW**

**Arc Title:** The Land of the Free
**Arc Boss:** Light (Raydel Linares), Free Elite
**Hidden Villain:** Shiro (as "Zafiro", Diominiciel's Apostle), possessing Kensuke/Protos
**Setting:** Free HQ, built into the island's northeastern cliffs
**Timeline:** Night of Gallan's departure → following days → the Jitsumoto/Pristania attack

### **Core Premise**

Arjan and Lopney follow the hooded stranger (Shiro in Kensuke's body) into Free HQ and are separated. Free is on alert for a Pristania attack that "Kensuke" announced, and it is so traumatized by the attack five years ago that anyone unknown is treated as a Pristania agent. Arjan, Lopney, Ichiro and Yonoa are all accused in turn. Shiro uses that paranoia as his weapon: he frames Yonoa, keeps Meiji unconscious, and provokes a frontal clash between Free and Jitsumoto so Pristania can strike both.

**The real enemy of the arc is Free's fear of itself.** The Elite are antagonists because they do their job well.

### **Arc Structure (author's rule, see Quick Ref)**
- **Opens** with the arc boss flashback (Light).
- **After the boss's defeat** (Arjan beats Light): an event from the past of Zenshin, Megami and Kami. **[Content TBD]**
- Arc 3 opens with Mauricio's flashback, after the boat departs.

## **STORY BEATS**

### **0. ARC BOSS FLASHBACK — LIGHT (5 years before Arc 1)**
- Raydel Linares, a child prodigy (~14), lives in Free's old HQ.
- Introduces Free's Elite from the war against Pristania: **Uragaeshi, Elysia and Terror**.
- Ends with Raydel wanting to be like them, and an explosion behind him: Pristania's attack on the old HQ.

### **PART 1 — NIGHT ONE**

**1. Arjan & Cristopher**
- Arjan and Lopney enter Free HQ following Kensuke (Shiro).
- In the dark corridors, Cristopher silently knocks Arjan out and takes him.
- Arjan wakes locked in Cristopher's office. Cristopher accuses him of being a Pristania agent; Arjan says he only came to find his father.
- They fight; Arjan loses. Cristopher, consumed by hatred of Pristania, keeps beating him.
- Tairin intervenes, scolds Cristopher (history of violence against children) and takes Arjan to the infirmary.

**2. Lopney, Ichiro & Tased**
- Searching for Arjan, Lopney finds a door into Ichiro's shop. Ichiro explains where she is, warns her of the Pristania alert, and suggests asking Terror (bar owner, knows everyone).
- Terror's bar is empty. Tased intercepts her; she says she is new and looking for Terror. Tased sends her "to Tairin": end of the corridor, door on the right.
- It is Tased's office: a trap. His plants bind her. When he pulls some plants away to examine her, she kicks him and breaks free (**pure skill, no Nouryoku**: hers awakens in Arc 5).
- Tased identifies himself as Security and demands her name. "Lopney **Byronstrike**" (adopted surname): he believes she is the daughter of his parents' killer and attacks.
- Tased is winning when Ichiro arrives. Tased, who discriminates against him as a Kiryuin, accuses him of being an undercover Pristania agent.
- Lopney and Ichiro defeat Tased, but the noise draws attention; they must flee for assaulting a Security member. **(Hard cut.)**

**3. Lyon**
- Lyon finds Tased unconscious, suspects Pristania, carries him to the infirmary (Tairin and Arjan are there).
- Tairin goes to raise the alarm and orders Lyon to hide: she will not lose another of her children, and she fears his **Kyousen**.
- At the bar, Terror is there (he had only stepped out). He tells Lyon to look after himself and Lyzander: he is fond of them as his "dead" daughter's best friends.
- **Short flashback:** Lyon, Gabriella and Lyzander playing in the bar (the trio's golden days).
- Lyon descends to a restricted, unguarded zone: **the secret hideout only he and Lyzander know**, a room with a large hole open to the sea.
- Lyzander is there, drinking. He is glad of the attack: at last he can ask Pristania about Gabriella.
- Lyon: Gabriella has been dead five years. Lyzander: that's a lie. Lyon leaves angry ("you're all I have left"); Lyzander answers that his duty is to try to save her.

**4. Flashback — The Attack 5 Years Ago (Lyzander's memory)**
- Free and Jitsumoto are about to seal an alliance after Meiji and Yonoa reunite.
- At the meeting: **Free** — Kensuke, Meiji, Rajin, Lyzander, Tairin. **Jitsumoto** — Shigeru, Yonoa, Umeda, Marsella. Everyone else guards the periphery.
- In the bar, Lyon, Lyzander and Terror discuss the alliance. Terror distrusts Shigeru, Kami's former disciple.
- Light appears briefly to reprimand Lyon (past clashes over the Kyousen).
- Gabriella (15, already Security) comes to fetch Lyzander for the meeting. Alone with Lyon, she tells him the opening is his chance to look for his origins.
- Marsella passes them, **recognizes Lyon instantly**, says nothing but "be careful, the meeting is about to start".
- Lyon and Gabriella imagine that together, Free and Jitsumoto might beat Pristania.
- **Air** (Android Unit 41R) attacks with missiles. Gabriella's metal saves them both, but Lyon goes into **Kyousen**; Gabriella is wounded trying to hold him back.
- Lyzander arrives: Pristania is attacking, evacuate. Gabriella goes to protect Meiji (her duty) and leaves Lyon to Lyzander.
- Lyzander is knocked out by a Kyousen bomb. Terror carries both boys out. In the distance, Shigeru's phoenix repels Pristania. Cut to white.
- Two days later Lyzander wakes with Meiji, Tairin and Terror. Gabriella was captured and must be presumed dead: in the war ten years earlier Pristania took no prisoners (Tased's father as example).
- Lyzander denies it, screaming. He demands Meiji wipe Pristania off the map. Meiji takes the blame but will not risk everyone in open war again: Free will hide again.
- Lyon comes in and hugs Lyzander. End of flashback.

**5. Yonoa & Elysia**
- Through the hole in the hideout comes Yonoa. Joyful reunion with Lyzander. She wants to see Meiji; he says wait until dawn (Pristania alert). She agrees, but asks how to contact Jitsumoto; he tells her where the phone room is and repeats: wait.
- They part (Yonoa out through the hole, Lyzander to bed). Then "Lyzander" climbs back through the hole: it is Yonoa wearing **Taeko's necklace**, an **artifact** (like the teleporter) that copies the appearance of someone touched in the last 10 minutes.
- In the phone room, Elysia (on guard) greets "Lyzander" warmly. Yonoa uses a device to message **Marsella** that she has arrived.
- Elysia detects the transmission and activates her power: she traps the space around a person in an empty alternate dimension, where she must stay with her victim, and **takes the form of the person the victim is thinking about most**.
- She becomes **Meiji**. She almost holds back, then realizes this is not Lyzander: with Lyzander she always becomes **Gabriella**. She attacks anyway, presenting herself as Meiji.
- Yonoa runs to hug "Meiji"; Elysia: who are you trying to fool? They fight. Yonoa realizes this is not Meiji either: he doesn't recognize a gift Meiji gave her in their childhood friendship in Vista Alegre.
- At the climax the necklace breaks; Yonoa is revealed. Elysia recognizes her as **Meiji's best friend**, apologizes, warns her the night is dangerous, tells her not to sleep outside, and sends her to Lyzander's room while she guards the zone.

**6. Close of the Night (montage)**
- Yonoa and Lyzander sleep together in his room (non-sexual, comfort).
- Lopney and Ichiro return to the shop, battered. She wants to keep searching; Ichiro will go to work tomorrow and find out what happened and whether there is news of her brother.

### **PART 2 — THE FRAME-UP**

**7. The Video**
- Next morning at the bar, Terror tells Ichiro that Pristania attacked, Tased was knocked out, and shows him a video: **"Yonoa" destroying Free's electrical installations**. Free is now cut off from outside.
- Terror's two hypotheses: Yonoa works with Pristania, or someone from Pristania infiltrated wearing her face.
- **Truth (author):** the video is a **montage prepared in advance by Pristania**, using footage of Yonoa supplied by **Marsella** from Jitsumoto. The damage itself is real: **Shiro destroyed the electrical installations himself** that night, unseen. The montage ties the two traitors together.

**8. Yonoa, Meiji & Kensuke**
- Lyzander wakes; Yonoa apologizes for stealing his appearance and for not waiting (she had to tell her friend she'd arrived). She'll wait for Meiji on a nearby island until things calm down, and leaves through the window.
- She is chased by a flying figure in combat armor and mask: **Meiji**. He doesn't believe his best friend attacked them, but demands to know why. He attacks; she responds with her **tornado** power. She convinces him; in tears he removes his mask. Warm reunion; they catch up and hug.
- **Kensuke** arrives with a **plasma weapon** (not his scepter-claw: he came prepared, because bullets don't affect Yonoa). He accuses her of being Pristania's accomplice. Meiji defends her: the impostor must be someone else. Kensuke raises the possibility that she acts under Shigeru (**Kensuke distrusts Shigeru**: he saw the treaty Jitsumoto mediated as compromise dressed as victory).
- Kensuke fires; **Meiji takes the shot** and falls unconscious. Yonoa reproaches him for hurting his own son; Kensuke knocks her out with his shield.

**9. The Elite Hunt**
- Tairin, remembering Protos's suggestion that "Yonoa" may have had an accomplice, summons the **Elite: Light, Suilan, Policia** (introduced as a group). Mission: check on Tased and find anyone left from last night's attack.
- At the hospital Light wakes the unconscious Tased with electricity. The shock is brutal; Tased only manages: "Ichiro".
- At the bar Terror (whom Light dislikes) lies that Ichiro isn't working today. Ichiro sees trouble coming and leaves invisible with his illusions.
- Suspecting the illusions, Policia goes to the shop; Light and Suilan search the bar.
- Policia finds Lopney hiding and arrests her for the attack on Tased. Policia gains the upper hand; Ichiro saves Lopney with assassin tactics and incapacitates Policia. He has no news of her brother; she must go, he'll contact her.
- Light and Suilan intercept them. Lopney flees; Light corners her in a dead-end room. She explains Tased attacked her by mistake; Light doesn't believe her. They fight; **Light wins** and leaves her unconscious.
- Ichiro tries to convince Suilan (his stage partner) and fails; under orders to arrest them, they fight. **Ichiro wins**, badly hurt. Leaving to find Lopney he runs into Light and barely escapes.
- Light captures Lopney and takes his two companions to the hospital.

**10. Arjan at the Bar**
- (During the Elite fights) Arjan wakes, is nursed by Tairin, and is sent to Terror's bar while she deals with the "Yonoa" damage.
- Ichiro arrives badly hurt; Terror hides him in his room behind the bar. Light's electricity is still in his body: **Arjan removes it with his spheres** (he realized against Yurisha that what his spheres enclose loses the Nouryoku acting on it).
- **Kensuke** arrives. Terror explains to Arjan that Kensuke is Free's President. Kensuke is worried about Ichiro (**it is Shiro's worry: Ichiro is his brother**); Terror says he isn't here.
- Kensuke looks at Arjan; Terror says he's a new kid Cristopher beat up last night. **Shiro recognizes him as Gallan's child and says nothing** (it would be too suspicious).
- Kensuke announces Yonoa's capture. Meiji is unconscious and "won't get up for a while".
- Tairin and Cristopher arrive; Cristopher's very forced apology. As punishment Tairin assigns Arjan to him (both hate it). Terror also takes charge of Arjan, out of fondness; Cristopher accepts out of respect for Tairin and Terror. **Arjan's mentors: Cristopher and Terror.**

**11. Lyzander Realizes**
- Lyzander goes to his grandfather Kensuke: it's a misunderstanding. Kensuke shows him the footage.
- **Elysia** comes in, hugs Lyzander, and apologizes for having let "Yonoa" stay with him that night; she's glad he's fine. Lyzander realizes something is terribly wrong: Yonoa was asleep in his room when the video was supposedly taken.
- Kensuke adds that Lyzander's "uncle" Ichiro (nickname: Ichiro is Meiji's close friend) is a fugitive for helping the Pristania agent just captured, Lopney.
- At the bar, Terror shows Lyzander where Ichiro is, with Arjan. Arjan learns from Lyzander that his sister was captured, but hides that he's her brother for fear of suspicion.
- **The three decide to free Yonoa and Lopney.**

### **PART 3 — LIFE IN FREE (character & setting development)**

*Ichiro recovers. Goal set: rescue Yonoa and Lopney. Order of scenes not decided.*

- **Kensuke interrogates and tortures Yonoa** for Jitsumoto intelligence. Key question: "Does anyone else know you're here?" She says no; he says she's lying. **Hint: he knows about Marsella** (Shiro coordinates with her).
- **Elysia asks to use her power on Yonoa** to settle the question. Kensuke refuses: (1) too dangerous, locked alone in another dimension with a tornado Logia suspected of working for Pristania; (2) inconclusive, "any spy who studied Free would think of Meiji". Elysia is left with a doubt. Second time Kensuke blocks the truth.
- **Meiji mysteriously doesn't wake.** Presented as a mystery; in truth Kensuke keeps him under on purpose so he won't free Yonoa (Shiro's plan is to draw Jitsumoto into a frontal battle), and **wakes him on purpose at the moment of the attack** so Pristania can capture him.
- **Cristopher visits the unconscious Meiji** (his boyfriend): shows Meiji's place in Free while he's absent, and Cristopher beyond violence; ideally with "Kensuke" in the room.
- **Yonoa and Lopney in adjacent cells** get to know each other. Both love **chess** (they can play by calling moves cell to cell). Conversations about **Marsella** (loads her later betrayal) and about Yonoa and Meiji's friendship.
- **Arjan asks about Gallan**: no one in Free has seen anyone like him; Arjan decides to rescue his sister first.
- **Arjan trains** with Cristopher (and Tased, in recovery); grows stronger; interacts with Light, Elysia, Suilan. Lives at Terror's bar.
- **Arjan & Ichiro** in the back room: Ichiro, waking, notices Arjan matches Lopney's description of her brother. Arjan learns Ichiro risked everything for his sister. This is how Ichiro, Lyzander and Arjan coordinate. (Seeds Ichiro's feelings for Lopney.)
- **Lyzander investigates** the damage (looking for the impostor) and the location of the cells. He works alone and tells Lyon nothing, to keep his friend out of it: he suspects they'll have to face the Elite, and believes they'll be forgiven because Meiji will understand, and Ichiro is Meiji's friend. **The Lyon–Lyzander friendship keeps deteriorating.**
- **Lyon and Marsella's communication device**: Lyon tries to repair it (his only clue to his origins) and notices it is sometimes moved. (Shiro steals it at night, fixes it, uses it, breaks it again and returns it.)
- **Terror and Tairin** cross paths over Arjan: ex-spouses who avoid each other; Gabriella's absence felt without being stated.
- **The Elite portrait:** Suilan sings at Terror's bar in front of Arjan and Lyzander; Policia repairs the energy failure (fits her mentality); **Light with Tairin** (mother and son: his hardness is hers, taken further).
- **Suilan watching over Tased** in the hospital.
- **Gallan:** a cut to Gallan preparing the teleporter (no explanation of why).
- The Elite keep hunting Ichiro, who stays hidden.

### **PART 4 — CLIMAX**

**12. The Rescue**
- Lyzander learns Kensuke has redistributed security so that the cells are guarded **only by Suilan**. **This is deliberate (author):** Kensuke knows who Arjan is and his bond with Lopney, guesses the rescue attempt, and lets it happen so the protagonists exhaust the Elite right before the attack. The protagonists' victory is part of Shiro's plan. Plan: Lyzander distracts Suilan ("I need to show you something"); Ichiro and Arjan free the prisoners.
- Just before, **Lyon confronts Lyzander**: he's tired, can't stand what their friendship has become. Heated argument; **Lyon announces he's leaving Free**, Lyzander reproaches him. In low spirits, Lyzander starts the operation.
- **Policia** has deduced the plan. Lyzander is already with Suilan; Policia confronts Arjan and Ichiro. Ichiro uses illusions so Arjan can go on. **Policia beats Ichiro.**
- Lyzander fails to convince Suilan; determined to free the prisoners, he fights her.
- **Lyon**, preparing to leave, is knocked out when the attack begins; **Marsella finds him and carries him onto Pristania's ship** (place this beat in the climax).
- Arjan arrives to find **Light waiting**: he left his post at an entrance to back up his companions.
- **Arjan vs. Light — the hardest fight of the arc.** Arjan wins by encapsulating Light's hands in spheres so he can't generate electricity.

**→ [EVENT FROM THE PAST OF ZENSHIN, MEGAMI AND KAMI — content TBD]**

- Suilan and Lyzander at a stalemate, Suilan gaining the edge, when a huge explosion hits.
- **Meiji appears** (awake): Jitsumoto is attacking Free. He orders Suilan to gather the rest of the Elite. **Arjan frees Lopney; Lyzander frees Yonoa.**

**13. Shigeru, Marsella & Zafiro**
- Meiji crosses the wreckage of the fighting, intending to end the conflict by talking to Shigeru.
- Shigeru is talking with Protos; after a brief clash, Meiji arrives. Protos leaves "to check on the others". Meiji tells Shigeru Yonoa is free; Shigeru demands they pay for imprisoning his daughter.
- **Pristania's troops appear** and start destroying the place. Smoke fills the battlefield; fights all over the base are interrupted by the arrival of the **Emperors: Shigo and Claudia** (Bellsaw is not present: he stays with the Vista Alegre police).
- **Diominiciel** enters where Meiji, Shigeru, Lyzander and Yonoa are. Shigeru, wounded, recognizes the Kamigami.
- **Marsella** enters, glances at Yonoa, goes to tend Shigeru. He hugs her and tells her to escape, he'll hold Pristania back. She says she's glad she arrived in time and **stabs Shigeru in front of Yonoa**.
- Yonoa, frozen, tries to attack, but she and Lyzander are trapped in a **Protos sphere**.
- Diominiciel greets Protos and throws a body at his feet: "Long time no see." Protos laughs, drives his arm through his own chest and collapses. **The body on the floor rises: Kensuke was possessed by "Zafiro", Diominiciel's Apostle** (Shiro, returning to Reiji's body).
- Zafiro seizes the wounded Meiji and moves to capture Lyzander and Yonoa, but **Kensuke's spheres protect them** (Kensuke, conscious through all five years, acts in his last moments).
- **Shigeru transforms into a giant phoenix**, says goodbye to his daughter, and destroys the ship Pristania came in. Pristania retreats.
- **Protos gives Meiji a sphere** and says goodbye, but Meiji is already captured and is carried off by Pristania. (The sphere outliving its owner foreshadows Kami's power persisting in his corpse.)

**14. Aftermath**
- **Light's death is shown on page (author):** already beaten by Arjan, he still rises to fight the Emperors and dies as Elite.
- **Dead:** Light, Suilan and Tased (fighting the Emperors; **Shigo kills Tased, then Suilan**, who fought him after seeing Tased dead; he takes their DNA), Shigeru, Kensuke/Protos.
- **Captured:** Meiji.
- Arjan and Lopney find **their father's tie** on the battlefield and assume he was captured too.
- **Rajin**, absent for a long period, returns too late; becomes acting Commander of Free.
- Pristania's plan is revealed: Marsella's involvement, Kensuke/Shiro's key role. **How Pristania arrived so fast remains a mystery** (to the characters and the reader; the teleporter and Gallan's role are revealed later).
- Cristopher, Elysia and Terror badly wounded. Policia and Tairin tend the wounded.

**15. Departure**
- An advance party of the **five protagonists** (Ichiro, Lopney, Arjan, Lyzander, Yonoa) will rescue Meiji and Gallan, using **Yurisha's ship**.
- Ichiro asks where they got it. Hearing about Yurisha (a Kiryuin family friend he saw as an uncle), he falls sad and resentful, but finds the determination to go on.
- **End of arc:** the five sail for **Vista Alegre**, where rumor says they can find the whereabouts of Pristania's base.

## **CHARACTER ARCS**

**Arjan:** Beaten and accused on arrival → nursed, mentored by Cristopher and Terror, grows stronger → hides his identity to survive → beats Light, the arc's hardest fight. Discovers his spheres strip Nouryoku from what they enclose. Carries the guilt that weakening the Elite contributed to their deaths.

**Lopney:** Hunted from the first night; wins against Tased with Ichiro, loses to Policia and Light (the Elite are meant to be overwhelming). Imprisoned; bonds with Yonoa over chess. Still no Nouryoku (awakens Arc 5).

**Lyzander:** From drunk, grieving recluse → investigator acting alone → rescuer. Loses his grandfather, his father (captured) and his best friend (Lyon leaves). Falls for Yonoa.

**Yonoa:** Reunion with Lyzander and Meiji; framed by video; tortured; watches Marsella stab her father, who then dies for her. Joins the five.

**Ichiro:** Marginalized Kiryuin who protects Lopney at the cost of becoming a fugitive; fights his own stage partner; ends grieving Yurisha. His brother Shiro was beside him all arc.

**Light (arc boss):** The child who wanted to be like the war Elite dies as Elite. Not a villain: a perfect enforcer, hard like Tairin and harder. Defeated by Arjan, then killed fighting the Emperors.

**Lyon:** Guilt over Gabriella (his Kyousen wounded her before her capture) drives him to accept her death. The friendship with Lyzander breaks; he leaves Free (Arc 2+: with Marsella).

**Kensuke/Protos:** Conscious prisoner in his own body for five years; his last acts are a sphere for Lyzander and Yonoa and one for Meiji.

## **THEMES**

1. **Trauma turned into paranoia:** Free devours its own; Shiro weaponizes it.
2. **The cost of winning:** the protagonists' victory over the Elite leaves Free weakened at the worst moment.
3. **Denial vs. acceptance:** Lyzander (Gabriella is alive) vs. Lyon (she's dead). Arc 4 proves Lyzander right in the worst way.
4. **Betrayal from inside:** Kensuke and Marsella, the two most trusted adults in each faction.
5. **Found family under strain:** Tairin's children, Terror's fondness, the trio's memory.

## **WRITING NOTES**

- **Name the pattern:** four characters are accused of being Pristania. Have someone say it aloud so it reads as intent ("in Free, everyone is Pristania until proven otherwise"). Justification: Kensuke announced an attack that night.
- **Clues against Kensuke must accumulate silently:** the look at Arjan, the worry for Ichiro, Meiji not waking, "does anyone else know?", the refusal to Elysia, the cell guarded by Suilan alone.
- **Lopney's escape from Tased is skill, never nullification.**
- **Shigo must visibly take DNA** from Tased and Suilan.
- **Light must stay sympathetic:** hard, efficient, never cruel for its own sake.
- **Do not explain** the fast arrival of Pristania; let the tie speak.
- Rajin is called **Rajin** on page (Mei/Megami are the same person; not revealed until Arc 5).
