# **HIDE (秀) - COMPLETE CHARACTER PROFILE**

---
ROLE: Interlude antagonist (Interludes 1-4); Arc 3 monster at the Ryakujin facility
FACTION: Ryakujin (chief of the laboratory)
STATUS: Killed by Zenshin (~55 years before Arc 1); survives as a monster; killed for good by Jashin through Hirohiko (Arc 3)
AGE_ARC_1: N/A (a monster in lethargy). In life: somewhat older
---

## **BASIC INFO**

**Name:** Hide (秀)
**Species:** **Ryakujin** (the ancient civilization, not human)
**Occupation:** **Chief scientist and head of the laboratory** at the Ryakujin facility
**Creations:** Zenshin (Kami), Jashin, Megami; started **Meiji** as a contingency plan against the gods
**Nouryoku:** None in life. As a monster: the gods' power (see Powers)
**Status:** Dead (Arc 3)

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

### **In life:**
- An **ordinary scientist**, **somewhat older**
- **Heavy-set, ordinary build** (author: "como obeso, más bien normal")
- **Lab coat**
- **Ryakujin traits:**
  - **Pupil-less eyes** (species marker for all Ryakujin; provisional, author may revisit)
  - **Moth wings** on his back (his individual otherworldly trait)
- **Visual reference:** shared by the author (an older man, balding with grey hair at the sides, broad face, piercing look, checked blazer). Inspiration only: the character is "based on" the reference, not a copy of it

### **As a monster (Arc 3):**
- An **abomination**: **decomposed face**, **wrapped like a zombie**
- The pupil-less eyes and wings, if visible at all, read as part of the monstrosity (nobody on page knows what they mean)

---

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
- **Ambitious, manipulative scientist**
- **Genuinely kind and paternal with Zenshin**: his deepest relationship
- Sees his creations as the fruit of his research: Zenshin above all

### **What Drives Him:**
- The **success of the research**: the gods as **great power**, the ability to **revive the living**, and an **asset for the Ryakujin civilization**
- Zenshin as **the** fruit of the research: he always backed Zenshin as the experiment's outcome

### **What Terrifies Him:**
- **Death**. Anyone wants not to die: it is why he injected himself with the substance

### **What He Despises:**
- **Indiscipline and rebellion**: the reason he never looked kindly on Jashin

### **Fatal Flaws:**
- **Ambition** and **manipulation**: even the person he truly cared for, he used

---

## **VOICE & DIALOGUE**

- With Zenshin: **loving**, as with his creation; he **consoles** him. No nickname for him
- The face of the caring father figure, which is real, and the manipulator, which is also real
- As a monster: repeats only **"Zenshin"** (never "Kami")

### **Known lines (Master Lore):**
> "You can revive the dead twice, after which you will cease to exist." *(true for the originals)*
> "Revive me, we'll make more Megamis, call the experiment a success."

---

## **WHY HE WANTED JASHIN DEAD**

1. He saw **Zenshin's jealousy** over Mei and believed he was **doing him a favor**
2. The point of two gods is to improve each other through conflict, but **only one is really needed**: they are **expendable**
3. Jashin had a **problematic personality**: the most rebellious and undisciplined in the experiments

He told Zenshin **he would take care of Jashin**, that everything was fine. Zenshin believed the plan was his own idea (KAMI profile).

---

## **BACKSTORY**

### **The Facility:**
- Chief of the laboratory; creator of **Zenshin, Jashin and Megami**
- Began **Meiji** with the others as a **contingency plan against the gods**. Meiji was a biological experiment that took years to complete; Hide never met him (Meiji was born ~34 years before Arc 1, long after Hide's death)
- **The facility runs on its own**: it kept developing Meiji in secret and still monitors in real time

### **The Conspiracy and the Three Cities Test:**
- Manipulated Zenshin through his jealousy; promised to eliminate Jashin
- **Sent the three gods to the cities** (the Three Cities Test)
- **Hired the agents** who ambushed Jashin, triggering Jashin's destruction of his city and the massacre of the scientists

### **Death (Interlude 4):**
- **Stabbed by Jashin**
- Zenshin arrives; **Zenshin and Jashin fight**
- Hide **confesses** the conspiracy, reveals the **sealing technique** (Zenshin seals Jashin) and the **resurrection rule** (true for the originals)
- **"Revive me, we'll make more Megamis"**: Zenshin **kills him** in fury and revives **Megami**
- **Before dying** he injected **a substance derived from Kami and Jashin**, believing it would save him [TBD: exact moment in Interlude 4]
- It turned him into a **monster in lethargy** inside the facility

### **55 Years of Lethargy:**
- Kami returned once to collect Jashin's buried body, but **never came near Hide**, so he never reacted
- The twins walk the facility with Hirohiko all the way to the monitor room: **he wakes sensing them**

---

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **In life:**
- None. His weapons were science and manipulation

### **As a monster:**
- **The gods' power**: Kami's and Jashin's are identical, and he has it
- **Difference: his beam fires from him forward** (Kami's descends from the sky; Jashin's rises from the ground)
- **Aesthetic: decomposed, a travesty** of the gods' power; in essence the same
- Knocks out the twins and Hirohiko
- *Editor's reading:* the horizontal beam sits between the descending (Zenshin) and the rising (Jashin), as his substance came from both

---

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Zenshin (Kami):**
- **His deepest relationship.** A **father figure**, genuinely kind; he consoled him
- Also **his best experiment**: he always wanted Zenshin to be the fruit of the research
- And in the end he **manipulated** him
- Kami saw him as a genuinely caring friend; learned of the manipulation at the confession, felt deeply betrayed, killed him, and **regrets ever trusting him** (KAMI profile)
- As a monster: **betrayal** (Zenshin was like his son and killed him); the twins' power brings back all his resentment

### **Jashin:**
- **Never looked kindly on him**: always rebelling, undisciplined
- Ordered the ambush that turned him; Jashin stabs him in Interlude 4, and through Hirohiko **finishes him in Arc 3** (saving himself and settling his grudge)

### **Megami:**
- **Liked her**, but saw her more as a **tool** than as a person

### **Meiji:**
- **Never met him.** Hide started the project; the facility completed it on its own

### **The Kamigami:**
- Kami told his children **everything about Hide and the conspiracy** (KAMI profile). Hirohiko once knew, and lost it to amnesia

### **Arjan & Lopney:**
- Senses Kami's power in them and wakes; to him they are **"Zenshin"**

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Interludes (Zenshin, Megami and Jashin):**
- **Interlude 1 (after Yurisha):** introduced at the birth of Zenshin, Jashin and Megami
- **Interlude 2 (after Light):** his relationship with Zenshin deepens; his friction with Jashin
- **Interlude 3 (after Mauricio):** he sends the gods to the cities
- **Interlude 4 (after Marjenis):** stabbed by Jashin; the brothers fight; his dialogue with Zenshin; Zenshin kills him and revives Megami

### **Arc 3 - "5 Souls to the Rescue":**
- The facility outside Kamino; Arjan's face opens the monitor room
- **Wakes sensing the twins**, repeats only "Zenshin"; knocks out the twins and Hirohiko
- **Jashin possesses Hirohiko** and kills him. **Hide is dead, definitively.** No one knows how the monster fell

*Note on reading order:* the reader meets Hide as a monster in Arc 3, before Interlude 4 shows how he became one.
