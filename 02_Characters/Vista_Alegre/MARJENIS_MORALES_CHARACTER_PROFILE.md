## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Marjenis Morales
**Nouryoku name:** **Devirtud** (given by Kami; see Backstory)
**Age:** 65 (Arc 1). Chief of Police for ~34 years
**Gender:** Male
**Occupation:** **Chief of Police**, Vista Alegre Police Department
**Family:** **Kouji Morales** (son), lieutenant under Commander Derek Guzman. His parents were murdered when he was a child. Kouji's mother plays no role in the story
**Nouryoku:** **Yes, in secret.** Nobody knows until his public reveal in Arc 4 (Kami knew)
**Status:** Arc 4 boss; dies at Diominiciel's hands

### **The name "Devirtud":**
- On the surface, **"de virtud"**: virtuous, positive
- Underneath, **"Devil"**
- The thesis of the character in one word: a devil who believes he is virtuous

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- **Tall, heavy, robust.** Bald. Mulatto
- **Deep, grave voice**
- Built to inspire **fear and authority**: people shrink in his presence
- At 65 he still has the body and presence of a much younger man (editor's proposal, accepted by the author: his Nouryoku keeps him imposing; nobody knows why)
- **Visual reference:** Mongul (DC), for presence and menace only. He is not Mongul; the reference is inspiration, not a design to copy

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
- **Believes he is doing right by his people.** He sees himself as the **hero of his city**
- **Military, hard, patriotic to the extreme.** Punishes with brutality
- **Sees his police as a family**
- **Intelligent:** reads the situation in his war against the organizations well
- His ideas are **close to Kami's**, with something of **Light Yagami** (Death Note): justice imposed from above, by any means
- Under his command the department adopted the policy that an officer may **close a case however is necessary**
- **Can be a confused man.** This side shows when he talks with Kami

### **What Drives Him:**
- To make the world better **his way**
- His final goal is **the development of Vista Alegre**. To get there he must rid the city of the organizations, **Free, Pristania and Jitsumoto**, which he sees as obstacles
- The **3-Year War** scarred much of the region around his city. That feeds his twisted sense of justice

### **Hatred of Nouryokusha:**
- **A mask of self-loathing.** He believes that if he had not had a Nouryoku, his parents would never have met the fake doctor and might have lived (see Backstory)
- Over Arc 4 he changes: he stops hating Nouryokusha and **reveals in public that he is one**

### **Kami and the Kamigami:**
- **Follows Kami.** In Kami he found an equal
- **Despises Kami's children.** Like Shigeru, he does not sympathize with the Kamigami or with what they did: after Kami's death they unleashed a bloody war against Free, and Marjenis witnessed it. To him they are **impure**, Pristania is **no longer what Kami founded**, and they **threaten his city**

### **Fatal Flaw:**
- **The worst kind of evil: the kind that does not know it is evil**

### **"Devil" (moral, not visual):**
- There is no monstrous form. "Devil" is Kami's judgment of who he is: a man who **reached the top of hell**, a **fallen from grace**
- The irony: as in modern religion, **God allows the Devil to exist**

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Childhood and the Origin Crime (~57-56 years before Arc 1). The Arc 4 opening flashback:**
- A boy from Vista Alegre. His Nouryoku awakens at **8**. He keeps it secret
- It activates **without his meaning to**: Sellos appear on whatever he touches, with no Vice. A table floats; a friend he touches starts laughing for no reason. His classmates see him as a weirdo
- After months, tired of his condition, he tells his parents. **His mother is terrified**; **his father** says it may be a condition, knowing his son's life cannot be normal. The family goes to a **doctor**
- The doctor refers them to a **shadowy figure** who promises to solve the problem
- Coming home one night, the boy finds the house **in flames**, his **parents dead** and everything they owned **stolen**
- Traumatized, he uses his Nouryoku to **enhance his hearing** and overhears a **corrupt police officer** talking with the "doctor" who had promised to help
- The "doctor" is a **Nouryokusha**. When the parents opened the door, he killed them both, robbed everything, and burned the house with his Nouryoku to erase the evidence. He has a deal with the police that keeps him clean
- The boy follows the fake doctor home and finds he lives very well on what he has stolen over the years. He waits for him and **kills him**
- The corrupt officer hears the struggle and arrives; they fight, and **the boy kills him too**. Before dying, the officer **laughs at him**: he may play the vigilante, but he will not get far
- **The flashback ends here.** The rest of his past is revealed across Arc 4

### **Rise to Chief (~34 years before Arc 1):**
- He sets out to wipe out **police corruption** and **criminals** in his city
- He climbs the ranks, digging up the corruption as he goes, until he reaches its heart: **the former Chief of Police**
- He uses his Sellos to humiliate the former Chief in public (absurd acts, e.g., picking his nose in public) and exposes all the corruption he had uncovered. **The man went mad on his own**
- Marjenis (~31) becomes **Chief**. He turns policy toward **police brutality** and **the elimination of criminals**, all in the name of justice. He institutes the **"however is necessary"** policy

### **Kami (~30-18 years before Arc 1):**
- **~32 years before Arc 1:** **Bellsaw**, a police officer disillusioned by the new brutality, meets Kami and joins Pristania as an informant inside the department (BELLSAW profile)
- **~30 years before Arc 1:** Kami is preaching in Vista Alegre. **With Bellsaw's information** he investigates Marjenis' past and **assaults the police department alone**, knocking out every officer except Marjenis
- Face to face, Kami tells him **he knows his past and understands him**, and that there is a way to eliminate evil in the world, **but it is not Marjenis' way**
- Kami gives him his Nouryoku name:
  > **KAMI:** "You really are Devil. No... you will be Devirtud. And I, as Kami, will allow you to exist."
- Something begins to change in Marjenis. **He sees an equal in Kami**
- **The pact:** Kami may preach in Vista Alegre with no interference from the police; in exchange, **Kami keeps his secret**
- **Right after the assault, Kami names Bellsaw Emperor** (~31)
- For ~12 years they are **secret pseudo-allies**: Marjenis attends Kami's speeches and they **collaborate in secret**
- **18 years before Arc 1:** Kami dies. **Marjenis feels alone**

### **The 3-Year War (18-15 years before Arc 1):**
- Already Chief, he witnesses the atrocities of the war between Pristania and Free
- It hardens his position against **Nouryokusha** and **the organizations**

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Devirtud (Sellos)**

The author's term is **Sellos**. They are distinct from Kami's **Sealing** (Power_System.md), so this profile always writes them as *Sellos*.

**Core ability:**
- He creates **Sellos**: small **postage stamps**, the kind stuck on envelopes and letters
- Each Sello carries an **effect**. Stuck on a person or object, it imposes that effect:
  - **On himself:** enhance a sense (hearing, sight, smell...), more strength, more speed
  - **On a person:** e.g., "blind" (the person goes blind), "you cannot attack me", make the body fly, make them laugh for no reason
  - **On an object:** e.g., "flies off to the left", float

**The Vice:**
- To activate a Sello he first materializes the **Vice**, a handheld device **the size of his fist**, like a card reader
- He creates the Sello in his hand, **swipes it through the Vice**, and then uses it
- He materializes and dematerializes the Vice at will
- The Vice developed later so he would have **more control**. As a child, Sellos manifested unconsciously, without the Vice
- **Visual reference:** a Digivice (Digimon), for shape only. Design an original device

**Rules:**
- **Physical effects only, never the will.** He cannot write "obey", "confess", "forget" or "from now on you are my slave". He can write "you cannot attack me" (an effect on the body)
- **Cannot revive the dead**
- **Cannot make anyone tell him what they do not know**
- **Contact:** he must **touch** the target. Throwing a Sello is possible, but it almost never sticks
- **Skin or object:** a Sello on a person's **skin** affects the person. On their **clothes**, it affects the clothes
- **Visible and tangible:** the target can see the Sello and feel it on the skin
- **Duration:** the effect lasts while the Sello stays on. **Removing it ends the effect**
- **Disappearance:** a Sello vanishes **10 minutes** after the target has removed it
- **No limit** on active Sellos
- **No cost** to use them
- *Comparable to:* Kishibe Rohan's Heaven's Door (JoJo), as a power that can influence people

**Why nobody has traced it:**
- He places his Sellos in **subtle spots**
- Nobody would connect a stamp on their body with what happened to them, let alone with Marjenis
- Removed Sellos vanish after 10 minutes

### **Combat Style:**
- **Brutal close-quarters fighter.** A video-game **tank** that **hits like a truck**
- **Extremely durable**
- Stamps himself for **strength and speed**; stamps his opponents to impose effects
- **Intelligent and cautious** in a fight. A formidable enemy
- *Editor's note:* the counterplay is built in. He must touch you, and you can rip the Sello off. Against a tank who hits like a truck, getting close is exactly what you don't want: fights against him are about evading and tearing off Sellos

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Kouji Morales (son):**
- Lieutenant under **Commander Derek Guzman**
- A **happy childhood**: Kouji is a daddy's boy. His mother died when he was small
- Marjenis **shields him from the department's brutality** and **wonders whether he should have let his son become a police officer**
- **Complicated relationship.** Kouji is more "liberal": a policeman on the side of justice and doing things right. Marjenis shares those ideals in his own way, but they have clashed over them
- **Does not know** his father is a Nouryokusha (until the public reveal)
- **Kouji is the one who changes him** over Arc 4: a father-son conversation stops him from executing Lyzander in public
- **Survives Arc 4**; becomes **Chief of Police** just before Arc 6. Profile: KOUJI_MORALES_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md

### **His Commanders (Derek Guzman, Mariana, Bellsaw):**
- He **appreciates all of them** and sees them as **loyal followers**
- **Bellsaw** resents him in secret (brutality, hatred of Nouryokusha, innocents killed "in the name of justice") and is Kami's spy and an Emperor. Marjenis never suspects. The irony: the commander he trusts was the source Kami used to reach him
- **Derek Guzman** runs Arjan as a private informant without Marjenis knowing, and is himself a secret Nouryokusha; **neither knows the other's secret**. Derek is excited by Marjenis' reveal. Profile: DEREK_GUZMAN_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md
- **Mariana** is secretly the leader of **Karauma** and infiltrated the department to work for peace in Vista Alegre. Exposed by Space in Arc 4

### **Kami:**
- Kami found him through Bellsaw, knocked out his entire department and spared only him
- **He saw an equal in Kami.** Secret pseudo-allies for ~12 years: he attended Kami's speeches and they collaborated in secret
- With Kami, the confused man shows through
- When Kami died, **Marjenis felt alone**

### **The Kamigami:**
- **Despises them** as impure and vengeful
- **Never deals with any of them directly**, except when **Diominiciel kills him**

### **Shigeru Houzu:**
- **Never met.** They share an antipathy for the Kamigami

### **Lyzander Sugimori:**
- The protagonist he **clashes with most** in Arc 4
- **Captures him** (after Diominiciel's ambush) and first means to **execute him in public**. Kouji changes his mind
- Lyzander is still his captive when Free and Pristania assault the coalition

### **Ichiro Kiryuin and Shiro (as Zafiro):**
- His opponents in the **Arc 4 climax**. He **defeats both**

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 4 - "Into the City of the Misbegotten" (arc boss):**
1. **Opening flashback:** the origin crime, ending as the corrupt officer laughs and dies
2. **The ambush:** Lyzander goes out walking with Angel. **Diominiciel (acting as Dragoon)** ambushes and subdues him. Marjenis arrives; Diominiciel has to flee, and the **Vista Alegre police capture Lyzander**. (After Perlereina's meeting, when Diominiciel takes Dragoon's position: Timeline.md)
3. **Karin becomes Space:** Bellsaw, loyal to Pristania, kidnaps his own lieutenant **Karin** for Pristania's experiments. **Marsella and Diominiciel** inject her with Mei's cells: she becomes **Biological Unit 5P4C3 "Space"**
4. **Bellsaw dies:** Bellsaw and Space confront Arjan, Lopney and Yonoa. Space goes rogue and deserts Pristania; Yonoa kills Bellsaw. **Only now does Marjenis learn of Free and the protagonists**, and wonder whether Free was involved
5. **Father and son:** Marjenis plans to **execute Lyzander in public**. A conversation with Kouji leaves him shaken and he changes his mind
6. **Mariana exposed:** Space arrives and exposes Mariana as the leader of **Karauma**. Mariana explains she infiltrated because Marjenis is not doing what is right, and she wants peace for Vista Alegre
7. **Change of heart:** still turning Kouji's words over, Marjenis goes public. He **reveals he is a Nouryokusha**, says he will no longer fight out of hatred but for a better future, and names **the organizations as the real enemy**. The speech **unites the police and Karauma**. The city recognizes him as a **hero** and pledges itself to him: **Vista Alegre united against Free and Pristania**
8. **The assault (climax):** Free and Pristania attack the coalition, which holds Lyzander captive. Marjenis fights **Ichiro and Shiro (as Zafiro)** and defeats both. As he is about to kill Zafiro, **Diominiciel intervenes**; they fight and **Diominiciel kills him**
9. **Aftermath:** the coalition is shattered; Vista Alegre is left without a strong leader, its defenders gravely wounded. Then **Angel's mecha partially destroys the city**, and **Pristania engineers Kamino into an army of fanatics** and **conquers Vista Alegre** in a pitched battle: the last act of the Vista Alegre story. Arc 4 closes with Free preparing the assault on Pristania HQ
