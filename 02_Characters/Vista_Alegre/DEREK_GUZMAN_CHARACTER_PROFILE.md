## **BACKSTORY**

### **His Parents (during the 3-Year War, ~18-15 years before Arc 1; Derek ~12-15):**
- Derek and his parents lived on the **outskirts of a town**
- **Two Pristania soldiers** broke into the house looking for food to steal, not realizing it was occupied
- The family tried to slip out unnoticed. When they were discovered, **his parents attacked the soldiers to give Derek a chance to escape**
- Believing them enemy combatants, **the soldiers killed his parents in self-defense**
- When they realized the parents were civilians, the soldiers **tried to apologize**. Derek had already **run away in tears**, not listening, **swearing revenge on Pristania**

### **Vista Alegre:**
- Survived **alone** in Vista Alegre, working however he could
- An **orphanage** took him in; he lived there until he joined the police [TBD: which orphanage]
- Rose through the ranks **on merit** to **commander**

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Battle**

**Concept:** like a Pokémon trainer who spots you: you stop dead, the trainer walks up to you, and you have to battle. **A fight you cannot run from.**

**How it works:**
1. Derek **points at someone with his index finger**. He only has to **see** them
2. The target **cannot move**, and **cannot use their Nouryoku**
3. Derek must **walk to the target in a straight line**. If he strays from that line, he feels **great pain**
4. Face to face, within **2 meters**, he strikes with a **blunt object** (his **bat**)
5. **The moment he swings**, the target can move again (and use their Nouryoku). They can try to dodge at point-blank range; **almost always, they take the bat**
6. He can **repeat it at once**, as soon as he has the target in sight again and points

**Rules:**
- **One target at a time**
- **There must be a logical straight-line path** to the target, or it does not activate
- **Obstacles:** he can swim a river. He **cannot break a wall**, because his first strike must land on the target
- **No ranged first strike:** pointing a gun does not work; the gun will not fire. The first blow is in melee, within 2 meters
- **Third parties can attack Derek** while he walks
- **Counter (Arc 4):** **Glayne breaks Battle** by wrapping himself in one of his **ellipses** (Ellipse Encapsulation nullifies external power control). The timing must be perfect, and living inside an ellipse is not sustainable

*Editor's note:* the power is the man. Once he has a target, he cannot step off the path without pain: a man who physically cannot let go of a grudge.

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Marjenis Morales (Chief):**
- Holds him in **great esteem**; sees their goals as alike
- Marjenis **does not know** what Derek does on his own (Arjan)
- To Marjenis, Derek is a **valuable asset**: a cop unafraid of informants and controversial methods
- **Neither knows the other is a Nouryokusha.** At Marjenis' public reveal Derek is **excited**: if the Chief is a Nouryokusha, he is equipped to fight Pristania

### **Kouji Morales (lieutenant and assistant):**
- Looks after him out of respect for Marjenis, and **genuinely cares for him**
- Sees him as a daddy's boy; Derek is the subdued one, Kouji the enthusiast
- **Saves Kouji's life** in the fight with Glayne, and **reveals his Nouryoku to him**. **Kouji keeps the secret**
- From Arc 6, Kouji's **right hand** and occasional advisor

### **Arjan (his informant):**
- When the protagonists reach Vista Alegre, Derek **takes Arjan aside** into a locked room. He knows who they are: an informant told him **some kids had won the race in Kamino**, with a rough description, and the group arrived right then
- **The deal:** he could send the whole police after them and it would all be over, but he does not want that. Arjan becomes **his spy**, passing him whatever Free learns about Pristania; in exchange Derek **reveals nothing** about who they are
- **Arjan accepts** under blackmail, but also because he sees in Derek an **ally against a common enemy**
- **Pure blackmail at first.** Over time Derek sees Arjan as **a victim like himself**, when he learns **Pristania captured Arjan's father**. He comes to **empathize** with him
- **Arjan shows Derek's human side**, as Glayne does for Perlereina

### **Bellsaw (fellow commander):**
- Learning after Bellsaw's death that he was a Pristania Emperor: **disgust and betrayal**

### **Mariana Caelestis (fellow commander):**
- **Diametrical opposites**; their personalities repel each other. Both orphans of violence and secret Nouryokusha in the police; he never lets go, she lets go of everything (MARIANA profile)

2. **Glayne:** Arjan's information lets the police **track Glayne**. Derek and Kouji chase him; the police uncover his identity. **Derek fights Glayne**: Glayne breaks Battle with an ellipse and **escapes**.
