### **Defining Traits:**
- **Calm** temperament
- **Inquisitive**; loves to **learn**
- Keeps **fit**; **enjoys life**
- **Driven by her mission** day to day
- **A mediator.** She grew up among all kinds of people and believes **everyone can change and be better**
- **Knows** that running a mafia is not right, and would rather be **the last one to do deplorable things** than let the city stay as it is
- **Peace matters more to her than revenge**

### **What She Wants:**
- **Peace without police brutality**, with Karauma able to come into the open, and a **safer city**
- Knowing how hard that is, above all: **stop Marjenis**

### **Fatal Flaw:**
- **Almost too perfect.** She fails to see the small illogical details in people's behavior. She **never suspected Bellsaw**

### **The spirits:**
- Each has their own personality, to be defined as they appear
- They have **great affection for one another**

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Victoria**

**The six:** **Mariana** (the original), **Lukas**, **Rosalía**, **García**, **Samuel** and **Teresa**.

**What the spirits are:**
- **Her power itself.** Parts of her, as if your powers had names and could talk to you. **They are not the dead** (unlike the spirits of Masaki or Gloria)

**How it works:**
- **Inside her body:** they help her with their different abilities. She can be looking one way while a spirit inside her looks another
- **Outside her body:** they take **human form**, with **their own personality** and some trait resembling Mariana
- **Shared field of vision** across all six bodies
- **Each body has a place in society**, and all serve Mariana. **García** is the boss of Karauma; **Mariana** is the police commander [TBD: Lukas, Rosalía, Samuel, Teresa]
- She tries to keep her spirits with her, for the advantages it gives
- **Rosalía** spreads a **dust that regenerates the body of a spirit**. This is **not resurrection**: the spirit never died, it only lost its form. She **cannot revive Mariana**. (Distinct from the gods' Resurrection: Power_System.md)
- **Death:** when **all** her spirits are gone, **Mariana dies**

**Why "Victoria":** even if you beat her, as long as she has spirits left she can recover. **You can never win a victory against her.**

**Comparison with Goro (author):** a similar power, with different strengths and weaknesses. *Editor's note:* Goro surrounds himself with obedient copies so as not to be alone; Mariana spreads herself among parts with their own voices so as to be everywhere. In Arc 4 Goro's clones defeat her spirits.

## **BACKSTORY**

- Her **parents were killed** in the **persecution of Nouryokusha**. Marjenis has been Chief for ~34 years, so this happened **under his policy**
- She grew up **on the streets of Vista Alegre**

### **The Setting (author, 2026-10-12):**
- **Mariana** is a police **commander**, with the goal of **ending police brutality** and **winning recognition for Karauma**
- **García** is the boss of Karauma, which gives Mariana her **alibi**

### **Karauma (空馬):**
- **Founded by García from day one.** To Karauma, García *is* the founder; there was never a time when Mariana led it in the open
- **A revolutionary organization** that also does **dirty business** with other organizations in the city. **The business finances the struggle**: it is how a clandestine fight pays for itself, not greed. Mariana knows it is wrong and accepts it as the price
- **Final goal: the end of police brutality.** That is what Mariana wants, and by extension García
- Its members are victims of police brutality and anyone who joins the cause. **Not saints**, but against the police
- **Name:** read in kun'yomi, 空馬 is *kara-uma*; in on'yomi, *ku-ba*: **Cuba**. Karauma is based on **Cuba's clandestine struggle of the 1930s**

### **Karauma's structure (editor's design; delegated by the author):**
Modeled on mafias and clandestine armies:
- **García:** founder; the only one who sees the whole organization
- **Captains:** each commands one cell. They know only García and their own cell. **No captain knows who García is**
- **Cells**, specialized and blind to each other:
  - **Trade:** the dirty business and trafficking that finance the struggle (including the trade with **DeathCake**, see below)
  - **Armed:** sabotage and strikes against the police
  - **Refuge:** safe houses for victims of brutality and Nouryokusha on the run
  - **Eyes:** informants across the city. **Inside the police, Mariana poses as the captain of this cell**, with Iris under her
- **Rules:** contact through couriers and codes; no names outside one's cell; if someone falls, only their cell is lost
- This is why nobody ever suspected Mariana: in Karauma she is just one more captain, and not even the captains know who García is

### **The trade with Pristania (author, 2026-10-12):**
- Karauma trades with **DeathCake**, the Vista Alegre confectionery company that **Shigo** secretly owns (his secretary fronts it as CEO). Through it, Karauma's goods reach **Pristania** under another name. **Karauma does not know** its partner is a Pristania Emperor
- **Gabriella (the Metal Knight)** has hit Vista Alegre's lesser organizations, Karauma among them, for ~4.5 years. She knows Karauma's business ends up in Pristania's hands, but **not who the intermediary is**, so Karauma is one of her main targets (GABRIELLA and SHIGO profiles)

### **The Police:**
- She rose to **commander** while García ran Karauma, so nothing ever pointed at her
- She slipped **Iris** in as her subordinate

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 4 - "Into the City of the Misbegotten":**
1. **Exposed:** **Karin had been investigating Karauma** before Bellsaw kidnapped her. **Space appears at the police headquarters and exposes Mariana as a Nouryokusha user**; from there it comes out that **Mariana is García**. She explains she infiltrated because Marjenis is not doing what is right; she wants an end to police brutality
2. **The coalition:** after Marjenis' public reveal, Mariana believes him; **Karauma and the police unite**
3. **The assault:** during the Free/Pristania attack on the police, **Goro defeats her and destroys almost all her spirits** (working for Pristania in his betrayal phase). The fight exists to show **Goro's efficacy as an assassin**. Mariana falls into a **coma**. *(Order in Goro's arc: assault → Diominiciel captures the beaten Ichiro and delivers him to Goro → reconciliation → L411D → Goro's death)*
4. **After the assault Marjenis is buried.** **Lukas**, the only surviving spirit, stays to **guard Marjenis' body**, afraid Pristania will desecrate it again
5. **Perlereina comes for the body.** **She kills Lukas.** With every spirit gone, **Mariana dies**
