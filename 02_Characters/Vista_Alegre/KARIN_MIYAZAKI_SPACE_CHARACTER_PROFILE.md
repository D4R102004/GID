- **Space** is her place in Pristania's series of units: **Land → Sea → Air → Space** (she is the last)
- **Marsella and Diominiciel** chose it to **complete the quartet**
- Her core, **宙**, sits **inside her body**
- *Editor's note:* Sea's core (海) becomes Perlereina's necklace; Space's stays inside a living woman

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Karin:**
- **Kind**, with a strong **sense of justice**
- **Quiet**; keeps to herself (Kouji barely knew her)
- **A machine fanatic**: an engineer who **admired Bellsaw's work** in Vista Alegre
- Joined the police for **personal reasons**: to be **under Bellsaw's tutelage**
- **Deeply idolized and trusted Bellsaw** as her commander, seeing him as a **savior** and a **father figure**. After the kidnapping she still clings to him: **Stockholm syndrome**, the product of his manipulation
- **Knows Space exists** (author, 2026-10-13: changed from an earlier "unaware", for a better dynamic between the two). She lives the ordinary life while Space handles the rest

### **Space:**
- **Playful**: the **opposite pole** of Karin
- **Speaks rough**
- **An empowered woman**; **likes to prove herself**
- **Responsible**
- **Cruel**
- **Loves and protects Karin.** Karin is the only person she truly cares about
- **Saw Bellsaw for what he was**, and **hates him**

### **How the two coexist:**
- **Space dominates Karin.** Space **knows everything** that happens to Karin
- **Karin knows Space exists** [TBD: since when, how much of what Space does she knows, and how the two relate]
- Space **surfaces from time to time**. Once she has appeared, she **lets Karin live the mundane life** while she **handles the fun things**
- **Space carries the truth** of what was done to Karin

### **What Karin wants (after the truth):**
- Karin **learns that Bellsaw was Pristania when Space exposes him.** She **feels used**, and it fills her with **determination to finish Pristania and Free**
- She wants to **change things**; her ideals about the Vista Alegre police move her

### **What Space wants:**
- To **protect Karin**
- To **hurt Pristania**, which is why she gets along with Marjenis

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Biological android**
- Karin's body was **partly rebuilt mechanically**: biomechanical alterations, hidden
- Injected with **Mei's cells** by **Marsella and Diominiciel** (Factions.md: what **Uragaeshi** would have become had the experiments continued)

### **Megami's power: Cartoon Transfiguration (via Mei's cells)**
- **The same power as Uragaeshi's** (URAGAESHI profile; Power_System.md): cartoon logic, limited by imagination

- **Space cannot kill**, like Uragaeshi. **She can hurt badly**

- **Frenetic**, with **true cartoon logic**

### **Combat style: Karin**
- Karin **never gets to fight**. If she did, it would be with **ordinary police training**

### **Weakness:**
- **Time.** When Space's time runs out, **she has to return to Karin**. Similar to Uragaeshi's limit [TBD: how long, and how it recharges]

## **BACKSTORY**

- An **engineer** who admired **Bellsaw's work** in Vista Alegre
- **Joined the police to be under Bellsaw.** **Bellsaw chose her specifically** as his subordinate **for her devotion**. He found in her **a true subordinate**, but **never revealed he was Pristania**
- **Karin had been investigating Karauma** before the kidnapping (Factions.md; MARIANA profile)

### **The kidnapping (Arc 4):**
- **Bellsaw planned it** to give her **"the honor" of becoming the biological unit**. In his head, someone as fanatical about machines as she was would have **loved to become a machine**. A **manipulation**, in keeping with a man who believes himself **above others** (BELLSAW profile)
- **Marsella and Diominiciel** inject her with Mei's cells and rebuild her: **Biological Unit 5P4C3**
- **Space is born of the trauma**

### **Bellsaw (James Whannell):**
- **Karin:** idolizes him (savior, father figure), and stays bound to him after the kidnapping (Stockholm syndrome). When she learns he was Pristania, she feels used

- Her makers. **Marsella ends her life** in the assault
