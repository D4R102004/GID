## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Kouji Morales (幸治)
**Age:** ~25 (Arc 1)
**Gender:** Male
**Occupation:** **Lieutenant**, Vista Alegre Police Department, under Commander **Derek Guzman** (and his assistant). **Chief of Police** just before Arc 6
**Family:** **Marjenis Morales** (father, Chief of Police; dies Arc 4). His mother died when he was small (editor's proposal, approved by the author)
**Nouryoku:** None
**Personal life:** No partner, no children. The important person in his life is his father

### **The name:**
- His **mother** chose it
- **幸** "happiness, fortune" + **治** "govern, heal, put in order"
- *Editor's reading:* "to govern well", the son of the man who governs the city "however is necessary"

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- **Slim**, in police shape; clearly takes care of his body for the job. **Handsome**
- **Green eyes**
- Light hair (not quite blond) [TBD: exact tone]
- Looks more like his **mother**, whose skin tone he has; but his **expression recalls his father**
- **Signature:** he dresses in **black**
- **Visual reference:** Gladion (Pokémon Sun/Moon), aged up to 25. Inspiration only: not the hair over the eye, not the hoodie design. The character is "based on" the reference, not a copy of it

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
- **Struggles with his father's legacy and expectations** while having a mind and ideas of his own
- **Insecure but determined**
- His **vision of the good** was instilled by his father
- **Acts from the heart**, and pushes you to do the same
- **Erratic at times**, above all on missions. **No subtlety**
- **Talks with you and tries to understand you, while trying not to let you understand him**
- Can be **immature and edgy**, but he is a **good person**
- **Loves justice**

### **What Drives Him:**
- To be a **good police officer** and do things right
- To give his **father** a good image
- To **hunt down the organizations**, Free and Pristania

### **What Enrages Him:**
- Being treated as **his father's son** instead of for who he is

### **What Terrifies Him:**
- That criminals like **Free or Pristania** end up **destroying his beloved city**

### **Fatal Flaws:**
- **Rush to action**
- **Idealism.** Unlike his father, he struggles to see nuance: **everything is black and white**

### **Impression:**
- A certain **naivety**, but also **determination**

---

## **VOICE & DIALOGUE**

- **Youthful.** Talks with street slang: "bro" and the like
- **Edgy** in that he **tells you things to your face**
- Calls his father **"Padre"**, even while talking slang with everyone else

### **Sample Dialogue (author):**
> *(When the police discover Glayne's identity, Arc 4)*
> **KOUJI:** "¡Lo tengo, padre! Descubrimos la cara de uno de los apóstoles."

### **Key line (author, gist):**
> *(To his father, against executing Lyzander)* That isn't right. **We represent justice.**

## **BACKSTORY**

- Born **~25 years before Arc 1**, during Marjenis' secret alliance with Kami (Marjenis ~40, Chief for ~9 years)
- His **mother died** when he was small, after naming him (editor's proposal, approved)
- **A happy childhood** with his father. A daddy's boy
- **Never met Kami** (he was ~7 when Kami died)
- Learned about the **3-Year War** later. All he has ever known of Free and Pristania is that they are **soulless: the enemy**
- **Worked hard from childhood** to become a police officer
- **Lieutenant at 25:** through his connection to Marjenis, **and** through his own effort. He accepts the post without believing he owes it to his father
- **Has never seen the brutality** of his father's police. **Marjenis shields him** from it, and **wonders whether he should have let his son become a police officer**. Derek, who looks after him out of respect for the Chief, keeps him away from it too

---

## **SKILLS & COMBAT**

- **No Nouryoku**
- **A modern police officer:** pistol, baton, whatever standard police equipment is in use
- **Expert at close combat.** Acrobatic, **hit-and-run** style. A **more human** way of fighting
- **Level:** similar to **Lopney**, and their powerless styles look alike. Lopney vs. Kouji is "a black belt against a trained police officer"
- **His real strength:** his **enthusiasm**. He is the **right mindset** inside the Vista Alegre police
- *Editor's note:* his style says what his ethics say. Marjenis is a tank who absorbs and crushes; Kouji hits and gets out

---

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Marjenis Morales (father):**
- A happy childhood; Kouji is a daddy's boy, and his father is the most important person in his life
- They **clash** because Kouji is more "liberal": a policeman on the side of justice and of doing things right
- Marjenis shields him from the department's dirty work and doubts he should have let him join
- **Kouji changes his father** (Arc 4). After the public reveal, a beautiful **father-and-son scene**
- Does not see his father die

### **Derek Guzman (commander):**
- Derek **looks after him out of respect for Marjenis**
- Kouji is the enthusiastic young man trying to do things; **Derek is more subdued**
- They talk easily, but **Derek sees him as a daddy's boy**
- Through Derek's reckless pursuit of Glayne, Kouji first sees the police's brutality

### **Iris (fellow lieutenant):**
- Both **young and inexperienced**; they **rub against each other** at work
- **Iris calls out his immaturity; he calls out her edgy moments**

### **Karin (fellow lieutenant):**
- Quiet, kept to herself; little relationship

### **Arjan and Lopney:**
- He **guesses** who they are when they infiltrate the department, and **brings them before his father**
- **Fights both** in Free's assault on the department. **They win**, but end up badly hurt

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 4 - "Into the City of the Misbegotten":**
1. **The chase:** goes with **Derek** to capture **Glayne**, who is being hunted in Vista Alegre. **The police uncover Glayne's identity** ("¡Lo tengo, padre!"), but **do not catch him**. Kouji sees Derek's recklessness, and Glayne's brutality: **his first shock**
2. **The infiltration** (after Bellsaw's death): Arjan and Lopney infiltrate the department to find where Lyzander is held. Kouji guesses who they are and **brings them before Marjenis**. They get out **thanks to Derek** [TBD: how and why]
3. **Father and son:** when Marjenis means to **execute Lyzander in public**, Kouji tells him it isn't right: they represent justice. It leaves Marjenis shaken
4. **The public reveal:** Kouji is **in shock** that his father is a Nouryokusha. After the speech he **understands his father's motives**; a beautiful father-and-son scene
5. **The assault:** Free attacks the department to rescue Lyzander. **Kouji fights Arjan and Lopney** and loses. He is **unconscious elsewhere in the building** when Diominiciel kills Marjenis
6. **Survives**

### **Arcs 5-6:**
- Vista Alegre is conquered by Pristania with Kamino's fanatics (end of Arc 4)
- **Just before Arc 6**, **Hirohiko lifts Kamino's occupation and frees Vista Alegre**. **Kouji is installed as Chief of Police**

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- **The pure police:** "the most beautiful ideal the people in blue have to offer" (author)
- Justice without brutality: his father's ideal without the devil inside, and the one who inherits it
- *Editor's readings (not author canon; use lightly):*
  - The one who changes his father is the one who sees the least nuance. A man who justified himself with reasons for fifty years is not moved by a finer argument than his own, but by someone telling him, from the heart, something obvious he refused to see
  - The father who saw his police as family hid the worst of himself from his real family
  - The man named "govern well" ends up governing the police
