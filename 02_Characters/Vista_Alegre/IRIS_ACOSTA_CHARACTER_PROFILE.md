## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Stardust**

**Ability:** generates **stardust from her hands**, and uses it in many ways.

**Uses (author):**
- **Armor:** covers herself in stardust to **reduce the damage of contact attacks**
- **Burst:** expels it in a **gust** that **strikes and throws targets back**, doing damage
- **Glare:** makes it **shine** to **blind** enemies

**Uses (editor's proposals, approved by the author 2026-10-13):**
- **Constellations:** points of dust left hanging in the air that **burst** when someone crosses them; terrain control
- **Trail:** marks a target with dust **only she sees shine**, to follow them later
- **Light messages:** makes the dust shine in **patterns, as code** for Karauma (couriers and codes)
- **Cloud:** a curtain of dust to hide in, then lights up only part of it to disorient
- **Star fist:** coats her fist and releases the burst **on impact**, point-blank

**Weakness:**
- **The shine gives her away.** For a Nouryokusha who has hidden all her life, her power is the opposite of hiding
