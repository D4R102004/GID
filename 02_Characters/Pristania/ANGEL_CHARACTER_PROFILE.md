## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Angel Velasco (author). "Angel" is his real name
**Codename:** **Tenshi** (天使, "angel" in Japanese). Self-given, because he has no Nouryoku and so no assigned codename. **Very personal to him:** he does not use it as a public name and introduces himself as **Angel**
**Age:** 43 (Arc 1) (author). He was about **25 when Kami died** (18 years before Arc 1). An adult, older than the Kamigami (Diominiciel ~40, Hirohiko ~38, Perlereina ~35)
**Occupation:**
- **Vagrant / beggar in Vista Alegre during Arc 4** (author). He lives **in a dump, in a lair** where he keeps Kami's gifts (author). **He really lives this way; it is not a disguise** (author). More in Backstory
- Pristania Emperor
- His **first contact with the protagonists is with Lyzander, asking him for money** (author)
**Affiliation:** Pristania
**Nouryoku:** **None** (author)
**Family:** Mother died giving birth to him; his father never appeared. Raised in an **orphanage** (author). No known family. Kami was his mentor, closest friend and lover (see Backstory)
**Status:** Antagonist. The "fourth" Emperor. Kills Gabriella in the Arc 4 finale. **Dies in the same finale**, killed by Lyzander (author)

**Rank:** The **4th Emperor** (author). Kami, as leader of Pristania, *was* the 4th Emperor, so for Kami the two titles were equivalent. Angel takes the 4th Emperor seat **but not the leadership of Pristania** (author). **Perlereina grants him the title when the protagonists arrive in Vista Alegre** and does **not** present him publicly, to make better use of his undercover role: he is an Emperor **in the shadows** and nobody knows it is him (author). He declares himself openly only in his Arc 4 combat with Lyzander. Only Perlereina and Diominiciel know he exists, and in practice he answers to **Perlereina**. Below the Apostles, above general membership (Factions.md). Alongside Bellsaw, Dragoon (Claudia) and Shigo.

**Known dates:**
- Joins the protagonists **at the start of Arc 4** (Factions; Gabriella's profile)
- Kills Gabriella **in the final act of Arc 4** (author)
- **Officially declares himself an Emperor** during his **combat with Lyzander in Arc 4** (author)
- **About 12-13 years after Kami's death, after Gabriella escapes and before the protagonists reach Vista Alegre:** Perlereina finds him and assigns him to tail **Derek Guzman**, a Vista Alegre police commander, to keep informed of his movements (author; see Editor's Flags for a date check)
- **When the protagonists arrive in Vista Alegre:** he starts tailing them and Perlereina grants him the Emperor title in secret (author)
- **Dies in that same final act of Arc 4**, in his battle with Lyzander (author). He is therefore dead well before Claudia wakes (end of Arc 5)

## **VOICE & DIALOGUE**

**Speech patterns (author):**
- Sounds like **someone with experience**, who **knows what he is saying**
- **A born speaker** ("un hablante innato")
- **What he says often has a double meaning** and **refers to hidden things**
- Recurring themes, always in pairs or tension:
  - **Greatness** and **humility**
  - **Duty**
  - **How disgusting the human race is**
  - **Triumph** and **defeat**
  - **Freedom** and **slavery**
  - **As an Emperor (author):** more direct, **worshipful of Kami**, full of **duty**; **more fanatical**

**Editor's observations (not confirmed):**

## **BACKSTORY**

*(All from the author, 2026-09-19.)*

**Birth and childhood**
- Born in an **orphanage**. His **mother died giving birth** to him and his **father never appeared**
- Born **without one arm**. As a child he suffered **discrimination** and had **few friends**

**The failed business**
- As an adult he wanted to **open a business** to make a living, but **never stood out**: he **had no talent** for it
- He clung to the **hope of a better life** and believed the world owed him **another chance**
- For that hope he was **robbed** and **ridiculed in the street**

**Kami**
- **Kami came to his shop.** Kami was looking for a **successor for Pristania who was not one of his children**, so that they would not fight over power
- Kami **knew Angel's past** and, on meeting him, resolved to **educate** him
- Kami **pulled strings inside his organization** so Angel's business **prospered**. Angel soon became **an icon of the city, a star**, with his pockets full
- Kami then visited him and revealed he had **chosen him as successor**, asking whether he was willing to **leave everything behind** to become it. **Angel accepted**
- Angel and Kami became **close friends and, in time, lovers**. Angel **idolized** Kami; Kami saw in Angel **a true friend**. Their relationship was **beautiful**

**Secrecy**
- Kami kept Angel **secret from Pristania** during his formation, in case anyone tried to **kill or attack** him. **Angel never knew Pristania**

**Kami's death**
- Kami decided Angel was **ready** and promised to **present him to Pristania as his successor**
- Angel had given Kami a **special handkerchief, his only memento of his mother**. **Kami was carrying it** when he was killed
- **Three days before** he could present him, **Meiji killed Kami** in the **public duel**, but it was an **assassination** (author)
- Angel was left **cut off and shattered**: his friend and lover was dead and his **link to Pristania was nonexistent**

**Aftermath**
- He **deduced the location of Pristania** but found **nothing** there; instead, **undercover Pristania members on guard beat him humiliatingly**
- With nothing else to do, he **reopened his business**, but without Kami his **capital collapsed**. He ended up **in debt and living on the street**

**Perlereina finds him** *(author)*
- When **Perlereina and Diominiciel hide Kami's body to study it**, Perlereina **notices the handkerchief** and starts investigating
- She **traces Angel's mother to her death at the orphanage**, and from there **traces Angel himself**
- She finds him and, once she **understands what her father had done**, decides to give Angel the chance to be **a spy for her, to honor her father**
- **Angel's existence is kept secret. Only Perlereina and Diominiciel know**
- This happens **12-13 years after Kami's death**, **after Gabriella escapes** and **before the protagonists reach Vista Alegre** (author). He has lived on the street all that time
- Perlereina **assigns him to follow Derek Guzman, another commander of the Vista Alegre police** (not Bellsaw), to **keep informed of his movements** (author)
- When the **protagonists arrive**, he **switches to following them**. Perlereina, who hears everything, **grants him the right to be an Emperor** at that point, but **does not bring him before the public**, to make better use of his undercover role (author)
- He is the **4th Emperor** (Kami's seat) but **not the leader** of Pristania. **An Emperor in the shadows: nobody knows it is him**

**Present (Arc 4)**
- He lives as a **beggar in Vista Alegre**, for real. His first contact with the protagonists is asking them for money
- He lives **in a dump**, in a lair where he stores **the gifts Kami gave him** (author). He still wears **the same suit from his days as a star**, now neglected
- He is an Emperor **in the shadows** and **declares himself openly in his combat with Lyzander** (Arc 4). Until then nobody knows it is him

**Timeline note:** Kami died at **50** (his profile) and Angel was about **25**. About **18 years** separate Kami's death from Arc 1. Perlereina found him **12-13 years** after (author), when Angel was about 37-38 and Perlereina about 29-30 (ages from the Quick Ref).
