## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** James Whannell
**Codename:** Bellsaw
**Public Identity:** Commander James Whannell, Vista Alegre Police Department
**Age:** 61 (Arc 1) [CONFIRM]. Looks older than he is: reads as mid-to-late 60s, frail and worn down, while his contemporary Shigeru still looks robust.
**Occupation:**
- Commander, Vista Alegre Police Department (one of three commanders; highest rank below Chief Marjenis)
- Pristania Emperor (mission supervisor, reconnaissance, engineering support)
- Automation engineer by training; builds devices of every kind
**Nouryoku:** None
**Status:** Antagonist. One of the three original Pristania Emperors (with Shigeru and Kensuke/Protos). The last of that generation still serving, and the longest-serving Emperor.
**Family:** Daughter Claudia Whannell (Dragoon, fellow Emperor). Wife died in childbirth (name TBD).

**Inspiration:** Tobin Bell and the character Jigsaw (Saw franchise). His name "James Whannell" combines the names of the franchise creators, James Wan and Leigh Whannell.

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

**Build:**
- Elderly and visibly frail. Not physically imposing at all
- Slightly stooped posture from age
- Quiet, deliberate frame; never in a hurry

**Face & Hair:**
- **White-blond hair, neatly combed** (a more groomed version of the Tobin Bell reference)
- **Light blue eyes**
- Lined, weathered face with the marks of age
- **No scars** (he does not fight directly)
- Unfriendly, severe default expression

**Distinctive Style — Two Outfits:**

*As Emperor (Pristania):*
- Cult-like attire: **black and red** suit/robes with a **hood**
- Red is his signature colour

*As Commander Whannell (Police):*
- Long dark coat, buttoned, with a high collar
- Dark gloves
- Light-coloured scarf
- Dark trousers and formal shoes
- Reference: Giovanni's outfit (Pokémon). The scarf insignia should be the Vista Alegre PD emblem, not a copied logo [DESIGN TBD]

**Hidden Equipment:**
- Mechanical appendages stored in his cape/beneath his coat (blades, extra arms, shields, etc.)
- Pistols

**Body Language:**
- **Default:** Still, quiet, slightly stooped
- **Studied air:** Carries himself like an educated man, someone who has read and learned much
- Economical movement; when he acts, it is planned

**Overall Impression:**
- A fragile old man who does not look like a threat, until the machinery comes out
- The disguise is nearly honest: the courtesy he shows the police is the same courtesy he shows Pristania

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Worldly and wise** — a person who knows life
2. **Understanding** — tries to grasp other people's motivations and worries, then pushes them to move forward
3. **Idealist** — genuinely fights for a better world (in his mind)
4. **Dedicated** — committed to Pristania, his plans, and his people
5. **Hard, but intelligent** — generally severe, never stupid
6. **Quiet and studied** — still, contained, deliberate

### **Core Values:**
- **Kami's philosophy** — the only path to a better world
- **Claudia and Kami** — "his life" is these two
- **Appreciating life** — wants his allies to value their own lives
- **War as necessity** — believes war is the only solution and that the deaths it causes are justified. This does not conflict, in his mind, with wanting his people to appreciate their lives

### **Dual Nature:**

**The Mentor:**
- Warm and motivating with his people
- Supervises, guides, gives escape routes
- Respected by everyone in Pristania as the voice of reason

**The Fanatic:**
- Ideological fanatic who also wants revenge against Meiji and Free
- Hatred of the entire organization (not personal, except Kensuke)
- Hatred poisoned him after Kami's death

### **What Drives Him:**
- **Primary:** Making the world better through Kami's philosophy
- **Secondary:** Revenge against Meiji and Free for Kami's death
- **Personal:** Claudia. Everything he does is also for her
- **Hidden:** Loneliness — the last of the original generation (Kensuke defected, Shigeru left)

### **Fatal Flaws:**
- **Hatred** — the poison that took hold after Kami's death
- **Arrogance** — believes he reads people well and that anyone can be persuaded. He believed Arjan, Lopney and Yonoa could be talked out of opposing Pristania. He was wrong, and it kills him

### **What Makes Him Happy:**
- Seeing his plans advance
- Time with Claudia
- Time with his fellow Pristania members (he has grown to love the organization over the years)

### **What Enrages Him:**
- **Free and Meiji** — killers of Kami
- **Kensuke** — desertion as personal betrayal
- **Shigeru's neutrality** — sees him as a traitor
- **Marjenis** — resents him for the brutality he represents (never shows it)

### **Nightmare Scenario:**
- Losing Claudia
- Losing Kami's legacy. Both are his life

## **VOICE & DIALOGUE**

### **Speech Patterns:**
- **Paused and courteous**, full sentences, never rushed, with a teacherly air
- **Enigmatic** — says more than he explains
- **Some humour**, but returns to how bad the world is and the evil in it
- **Mechanism metaphors** (gears, pieces, machines), from his engineering background
- Warm and motivating with allies; cold and polite with enemies
- **Design goal:** the reader must know he is wrong, yet understand him

### **Verbal Tics / Phrases (USE SPARINGLY — at most one per scene, never as a joke):**
- "Appreciate your life." (his mantra)
- "If you are good at building the machine, it does not break."
- "Everyone deserves a chance."
- "For Kami."

He does not say a fixed farewell before missions; he has several phrases and uses them naturally.

### **Sample Dialogue:**

**To a rookie officer (as Commander Whannell):**
> "Look closely, officer. Every person in this city is a piece someone decided was surplus. Our job is not to let them break. Appreciate your life, and theirs."
> *(Warm, tired, mechanism metaphor)*

**To Claudia, in private:**
> "You did well today. Better than I did at your age. Now eat something. An Emperor who is hungry makes a cadet's mistakes."
> *(Very tender, with humour; pride shows)*

**To Perlereina, when she opposes him:**
> "You may be right, little one. But a gear that spins too fast breaks the whole mechanism. Think about it."
> *(Voice of reason, never raises his tone)*

**To Yonoa, manipulating her:**
> "Your father would have wanted you to live. I want you to live too. We only have to decide on which side. Don't you think?"
> *(Cordial, cruel, and persuasive at once; uses Shigeru as leverage)*

**Before a mission:**
> "Everyone deserves a chance. For Kami."

### **Tone Shifts:**
- **With Claudia:** Very tender. Hard when it is needed
- **With Pristania:** Respectful, motivating, the elder voice of reason
- **With the police:** Respectful and courteous, using the same "appreciate your life / do good" line
- **With enemies (Arc 4):** Cordial, manipulative, and cruel, but also genuinely persuasive
- **On Free/Meiji:** Cold, absolute

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: None**
- Bellsaw has no Nouryoku. He is proof, in Kami's eyes, that people make a difference without one
- He does not fight directly

### **Combat Style: Engineer**
- **Traps** — his signature method; preparation and terrain
- **Robots** — he and Diominiciel built Pristania's robots (e.g., L411D "Land", a huge cannon-armed tank). Space (5P4C3) is credited to another creator in Diominiciel's profile (co-developed with Marsella)
- **Hidden mechanical appendages** — blades, extra arms, shields, stored under his cape/coat
- **Pistols** — conventional, not custom-designed
- **Surveillance devices**
- **Police training** — real, but he is now too old for it

### **Difference from Diominiciel:**
- Diominiciel is more of a **scientist**; Bellsaw is more of an **engineer**
- Bellsaw also contributed to Diominiciel's education by letting him see science in action up close

### **Weaknesses:**
- Depends on **preparation time, energy and terrain**, and on mechanical devices and his own ingenuity
- Old and physically frail
- Arrogance (misjudges people)

### **Design Note — VS Yonoa (Arc 4) [PROPOSAL, not canon]:**
- Yonoa's Nouryoku (Logia, tornado manipulation) has a close-range weakness and she has no martial training; Bellsaw's appendages and pistols are exactly the sort of tools that could exploit that
- One possible reading: he loses not for lack of means but because he came to persuade rather than to kill

## **RELATIONSHIPS & HISTORY**

### **Kami (Master):**
- Met Kami while working as a police officer. Kami "saw through him" and spoke with him about what is right and wrong in the world
- Bellsaw accepted the cause
- After Kami's death: devastated, poisoned by hatred, yet stayed loyal to the Kamigami. Decided the best favour to his master was helping his children, which ended up opposing Kami's own ideals

### **Claudia Whannell "Dragoon" (Daughter):**
- Her mother died in childbirth; Bellsaw raised her alone
- Loves her more than anything
- Raised her on Kami's ideals. She learned of Pristania and her father's role as a teenager, then chose to join and rose to Emperor
- Very tender with her; hard when needed. Proud she is an Emperor like him
- She admires him. She shares his devotion and sees truth in Pristania, but she is more inquisitive and not a fanatic like him
- Claudia's Nouryoku (Cloud), personality and full history are in CLAUDIA_WHANNELL_DRAGOON_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md
- Claudia is a **real nurse** at the Vista Alegre hospital where Bellsaw is taken and dies. She watches him die, helpless. It is a turning point for her
- **He does not know his daughter's best friend "Misty" is the fugitive Gabriella** until Perlereina's meeting in Arc 4 (see Arc 4). He knew the fugitive existed and stayed silent to protect his cover, but never connected her to Claudia

### **Diominiciel Dei:**
- Diominiciel holds special respect for him: veteran loyalty, with Kami since the founding
- Bellsaw contributed to his education in engineering/science

### **Perlereina Dei & Glayne:**
- Supports their hard line
- Respected by all in Pristania as the oldest and a voice of reason
- Perlereina will oppose him if she dislikes something, but respects him at heart
- Bellsaw learned of Arjan and Lopney from Perlereina (all the Emperors know)
- **He does not know about Yurisha.** Only Glayne knew

### **Shigo:**
- Treats him like a spoiled child, which in part he is. (Details for Shigo's profile)

### **Shigeru Houzu & Kensuke (Protos) — the original three Emperors:**
- Found Shigeru's departure controversial, but accepted it since they knew each other from before
- Sees Kensuke's desertion as betrayal. Shigeru feels similarly about Kensuke
- Does not sympathize with Kensuke. Today sees **Shigeru as a traitor** for taking a neutral position during the war
- The three were brothers in arms. Bellsaw feels alone as the only one left of that generation
- **Irony:** Shigeru understood Kami's ideal best but left; Bellsaw betrayed it out of loyalty to Kami. Each calls the other a traitor
- Knew **Taeko** (Shigeru's wife) from Shigeru's Emperor days. Never met Yonoa

### **Free / Meiji:**
- Hates the whole organization; nothing personal except **Kensuke** (Acting President of Free)
- Wants revenge on Meiji for Kami's death

### **Gallan, Arjan, Lopney:**
- Knew Gallan personally (Gallan was Hirohiko's Apostle)
- Knows Arjan and Lopney were raised by Gallan and believes they can be persuaded to leave the war
- Tries to guide them out of the conflict, not kill them

### **Yonoa Houzu:**
- Never met her before Arc 4
- Believes she can be persuaded to become an ally
- Despises Shigeru but does not want his daughter involved in the war

### **Vista Alegre Police / Marjenis:**
- Police officer before joining Pristania
- After Kami's conversation, decided to spy on his boss and the department for Pristania
- Uses his real name; no one there knows his Emperor name "Bellsaw"
- Resents Marjenis (brutality, hatred of nouryokusha, innocents killed "in the name of justice") but does not show it
- Treats everyone with the same "appreciate life / do good" ethos. **No one suspects him**
- Marjenis dies later at Diominiciel's hands, after Bellsaw is already dead

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Before Pristania:**
- Automation engineer; police officer in Vista Alegre
- Innately good, wants to make the world better
- Growing disillusionment: Marjenis' police stood for brutality, hatred of nouryokusha and of crime in general. He saw innocent people killed in the name of justice and concluded this was not the way

### **Joining Kami:**
- Kami met him on the job, saw through him, and convinced him to join his cause after a conversation about right and wrong
- Bellsaw stayed in the police as an informant

### **Contributions:**
- Provided police information
- Designed Pristania's base
- Acted as a spy inside the department
- Kami used his intelligence to assault the police headquarters, and afterwards named him Emperor
- Was with Kami from Pristania's founding

### **As Emperor:**
- Supervises missions rather than fighting: investigates locations, warns his people, gives escape routes
- Uses his devices to help others
- Invaluable to Pristania because of his position in the police

### **Kami's Death:**
- The worst days for many in Pristania. Hatred entered him and poisoned him
- Remained loyal to the Kamigami

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

**What Bellsaw Represents:**
- The idealist who sees the world's ugliness correctly and chooses the wrong cure
- The tragedy of loyalty: faithful to Kami, unfaithful to what Kami actually believed
- Same fall as Yurisha: underestimating the young. Here the cost is personal

- Puts Vista Alegre and its police at the centre of Arc 4

## **WRITING BELLSAW - QUICK REFERENCE**

### **Dialogue Do's:**
- Slow, courteous, enigmatic
- Mechanism metaphors, but sparingly
- Let humour be dry and small
- Return to how bad the world is

### **Dialogue Don'ts:**
- Do not make him a joke
- Do not overuse the mantra (max one phrase per scene)
- Do not make him openly cruel with his allies
- Do not give him Nouryoku or direct-combat feats

### **Action Beats:**
- Traps sprung, devices deployed from beneath the coat, robots doing the fighting
- He watches more than acts

### **Tonal Shifts:**
- Tender with Claudia, warm with Pristania, polite with the police, cold to Free, cordial-cruel to Yonoa
