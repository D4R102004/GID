# **JASHIN (OPUS DEI) - COMPLETE CHARACTER PROFILE**

---
ROLE: Final antagonist (Arc 6). Redeems himself at the end
FACTION: None. Ryakujin prototype; sealed inside Pristania HQ; possesses Hirohiko Dei
STATUS: Dies in Arc 6 (spends both resurrections on Diominiciel and Perlereina; his body turns to dust)
AGE_ARC_1: ~73 (comatose body); sealed at ~18
---

> **How to read this file:** unmarked text comes from the existing GID files (each source is listed at the bottom). **(author)** = confirmed by the author in the interview of 2026-10-06. **[TBD]** = no answer yet. **Editor's inference / Editor's flag** = a suggestion or a detected tension, NOT confirmed by the author. Before this file, Jashin had **no dedicated profile**: his canon was spread across the Master Lore, Kami, Hirohiko and the Power System.

## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** **Jashin** (邪神, "evil god"). **No other name, past or present** (author)
**Codename:** **Opus Dei** - "The Negative God" (Ryakujin designation)
**Age:**
- Created at Year 0 together with Zenshin and Megami. **Sealed at ~18**, in the facility confrontation
- **Body ages while sealed** (author): ~55 when Kami dies (18 years before Arc 1), **~73 in Arc 1**
**Occupation:** None. Ryakujin weapon prototype, then sealed for his whole adult life
**Affiliation:** None. Never part of Pristania or Free. His body lies hidden **inside Pristania HQ**, and his consciousness lives **inside Hirohiko (Gyrus)**
**Nouryoku:** "Dark God's Arsenal" (see Powers & Combat)
**Family:**
- **Zenshin / Kami:** his "brother" (fellow prototype). Jashin saw himself as the **older brother** (author)
- **Megami (Rajin):** the third prototype; mutual romantic tension, never consummated (author)
- **The Kamigami** (Diominiciel, Hirohiko, Perlereina): Kami's children, his nephews in practice
**Status:** Final antagonist. **Dies in Arc 6** by his own choice (author)

**Key dates:**
- **Year 0:** created by the Ryakujin
- **~Year 18:** the Three Cities Test, the facility massacre, **sealed by Zenshin**
- **Kami's death (18 years before Arc 1):** seal weakens, consciousness partially returns
- **During Gyrus's coma:** transfers his consciousness into Gyrus (years-long process)
- **Arc 3:** guides Gyrus to the protagonists (senses Lopney)
- **Arc 5:** through Gyrus, hears Megami's account: learns **Rajin is Megami**, that she lives, and that he has the **resurrection power** (author)
- **Arc 6:** the seal breaks when Arjan and Lopney use their 2nd resurrection. Freed, confronts Gyrus, **dies**

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

**Face and build (author):**
- **Identical to Kami, except for black hair** (Kami's is white). Still **distinguishable** from him at a glance
- **Yellow eyes** (Kami's are green)
- Instead of Kami's small circles under the eyes, **lines that run through his eyes**. Reference: **Ulquiorra (Bleach)**. They make him look **evil but also playful**
- **Build:** Hirohiko's build recalls Jashin's more than Kami's (author, clarifying Hirohiko's profile)

**The comatose body (author):**
- **Emaciated, bearded, unrecognizable. But alive**
- First **buried** by Kami and Megami after the escape (Master Lore). Later **moved by Kami to Pristania HQ**, hidden behind a wall (author; see Backstory)

**Free manifestation, Arc 6 (author):**
- Takes **his own body at the age Kami was when he died (~55)**
- *Editor's observation:* to everyone who knew Kami, he looks like **Kami with black hair**. The image will hit Gyrus, and later the revived siblings, hard

**The black eyes in others:**
- Gyrus's eye surroundings blackened the moment Jashin's consciousness entered him (Hirohiko's profile)
- **Lopney** has the same black eyes (Kami's "negative" fragment)

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

**As a youth in the facility (author):**
- **Charismatic and cheerful**, but **very ambitious**
- Always wanted to **leave the facility and do something big**, a dream he **shared with Megami**
- **Stubborn**
- **Confident**, where Zenshin was insecure
- **Cruel**, but **not chaos for its own sake**. A normal person for the most part, but **you can tell something in him is twisted**
- **Loves his friends deeply. Does not tolerate betrayal**

**Canon behavior:**
- Kills his assigned target **gleefully** when ordered (Master Lore)
- When Hide's agents ambush him, he feels betrayed and **destroys the entire city in rage**, then returns and massacres the scientists (Master Lore)

**After the seal:**
- **Toward Kami:** **hatred**. He wakes to find him already dead, which turns it into **resentment** with no target. **He keeps hating him even after death** (author)
- **Toward Megami:** **still loves her** (author)
- **Toward the world:** his Arc 6 plan is **chaos against the world** (author)

**The core contradiction (author):** he will not forgive betrayal, yet the man he hates most taught him nothing but betrayal, and the one he grows to love (Gyrus) is the one he planned to use.

**Editor's observation:** much of his hatred is **wounded pride**. The "younger brother" he underestimated beat him, kept Megami, and built an empire on top of his tomb.

## **VOICE & DIALOGUE**

**Speech patterns (author):**
- Talks like someone **ambitious**, who **understands the world better than everyone else**. In that way, **somewhat like Kami**
- **Mocking, confident, playful, enigmatic**
- Fights like he talks: **Vegeta** (enjoys the fight, shows off, but **does not forgive**)

**How he addresses people (author):**
- **Kami:** **still calls him "Zenshin"**, refusing the name his brother chose. Arrogant, speaks as the **older brother**
- **Megami:** haughty, speaks **to impress her**, and **teases her a lot**. **His strongest chemistry** is with her
- **Gyrus:** mocking tone, but he speaks like someone who **somehow loves him**, masked behind his plan to possess his body and dominate the world

**Inside Gyrus (author):**
- **He does not talk to Gyrus.** He acts directly **only through the trance**
- Otherwise he **influences his psyche**, creating **premonitions**. Gyrus does not know what is happening to him

**Canon lines (author):**
- Hirohiko asks who Zenshin is:
  > *"¿Tu padre ni siquiera te dijo su verdadero nombre?"*
  > ("Your father didn't even tell you his real name?")
- **The Arc 6 exchange (paraphrased by the author):** Jashin asks Gyrus why he would protect a world that has shown him only misfortune. Gyrus answers that he will make it a better place, as his father would have; that it is his wish and his destiny. Jashin reproaches him: **he is lying to himself, his heart wants something else**. Jashin is right: **what Gyrus wants is to see his siblings again and start over with them**

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

**Nouryoku: "Dark God's Arsenal"** (Power System). Inverted mirror of Kami's:
1. **神の矛 (Kami no Hoko) - God's Halberd:** multiple dark energy projectiles from dimensional portals
2. **神の壁 (Kami no Kabe) - God's Wall:** flat barrier, absolute frontal block
3. **神の招き (Kami no Maneki) - God's Beckoning:** dark beam that **rises from the ground**
4. **Flight**

**Sealing:** he can seal beings or powers. **He can use it once free**, but by then **Kami's powers no longer exist** (author)

**Resurrection (two uses):**
- **Never used until the end.** He did not even know he had it until Arc 5 (sealed before Hide revealed it)
- **The rule for the originals (author, 2026-10-06):** if **Kami or Jashin** use both resurrections, **they die and their body disappears**. **Hide told the truth.** Heirs (Arjan, Lopney) only lose the power. See Editor's Flag 1
- **Jashin believes he will die, and he is right** (author)

**The healing spear:** developed in his mind **after Kami's death**, during the semi-conscious years (author; Hirohiko's profile). Given to Gyrus as his first "gift"

**Power split (Power System):** 40% escaped with his consciousness into Gyrus, 60% stayed in the comatose body. 100% returns when the seal breaks

**Inside Gyrus (author; Hirohiko's profile):**
- **Occasionally takes control** of Gyrus's body (Gyrus enters a trance and remembers nothing). **Only then can he use his powers**
- **Rarely does it**, because he wants to **stay hidden**

**The Inverse World (author, 2026-10-06):**
- Inside the Inverse World, **its owner can imagine any ability and use it**. **Only inside it**
- Because Jashin has lived inside Gyrus, **the world belongs to both of them**: both can do anything there
- **Gyrus does not know this** until the Arc 6 fight. **It is his discovery**
- **Why Jashin wins:** he **knows the Inverse World better**, **uses the power better**, and has **more experience**
- *Editor's observation:* he survived decades in the void **with imagination alone**. His torment trained him for exactly the world where imagination is power. Best left as a visual echo in the prose, never explained

**Combat style (author):** enjoys fighting, playful and taunting, but **direct and merciless**. Vegeta

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Zenshin / Kami (brother, sealer):**
- **Rivalry from the start** (author): both took part in the experiments that made them stronger, and were in conflict
- Jashin **underestimated** him and showed **arrogance**; the passive Zenshin **accepted it**
- Still brothers: **deep down they loved each other**, with typical sibling fights
- **Contrasting personalities:** Kami insecure, Jashin confident. **Jashin saw himself as the older brother, Zenshin as the younger**
- Zenshin, jealous over Megami and manipulated by Hide, conspired to kill him, then sealed him (Master Lore)
- **Now:** hatred that outlives Kami's death. Calls him "Zenshin"
- **Arc 5 blow (author):** through Gyrus, he hears Megami tell Free that **Kami considered using his remaining resurrection on him** and never dared. **It hits his hatred**
- *Editor's observation:* with Hide's rule now true for the originals, Kami's fear was **founded**: reviving Jashin would have meant his own death

### **Megami / Rajin:**
- **Romantic tension, never consummated** (author)
- **Megami loved both brothers**, but found **Jashin's personality more pleasing**, while loving Zenshin a lot too. **Jashin and Megami were closer, by very little**
- Sealed **believing she was dead**
- **Arc 5 (author):** through Gyrus, discovers **Rajin is Megami**, learns she survived and learns of the resurrection power. **He cannot go see her**
- **He still loves her** and **would recognize her under the Rajin disguise**. **They never meet** (author)
- Megami's pacifism kept her from killing him (Power System)

### **Hirohiko Dei / Gyrus (host):**
- Found next to his chamber, mind destroyed, imprisoned by his siblings. **Saw an opportunity** (Hirohiko's profile)
- **Grows to love him gradually, and never admits it** (author)
- **He sees himself in Gyrus:** attacked by his siblings, forced to be alone, but also someone who wants to do good (author)
- Mocking toward him, but loving under the mask (author)
- **Gives his life for him** in Arc 6 (see Arc Role)

### **Arjan and Lopney (Kami's heirs):**
- **Recognizes them and can sense their presence** (author)
- **This drives the plot** (author): the **Arc 3** meeting between Dr. Araki and the protagonists happens because **Jashin senses Lopney**; the **Arc 5** reunion happens because **Jashin guides Gyrus to them**
- **Lopney:** seeing him, he feels **warmth, as if he had found family** (author)
- **Arjan:** seeing him, he feels **hatred**, because he **reminds him of Kami** (author)
- *Editor's observation:* the twins replay Zenshin and Jashin. The black-eyed one feels like family; the one with Kami's face feels like the enemy

### **Diominiciel and Perlereina (Kami's children):**
- To him they are just **"Kami's children"** (author)
- But **because they matter to Gyrus**, he **sacrifices his life to revive them**, as an **act of redemption** (author)

### **Hide:**
- Ordered the ambush that turned him. Jashin was **killing him** when Zenshin arrived (Master Lore)

## **BACKSTORY**

### **The Ryakujin facility (Year 0 - ~18):**
- Created as **Opus Dei**, the Negative God, alongside Zenshin (Positive) and Megami (Neutral). Design: positive and negative clash, and their powers grow through conflict
- Charismatic, ambitious, dreams with Megami of getting out and doing something big (author)
- Rivalry with Zenshin; closer to Megami (author)

### **The Three Cities Test (Master Lore):**
- Kills his target **gleefully** when ordered
- Ambushed by Ryakujin agents hired by **Hide**; kills them
- Feels **betrayed**: destroys the whole city
- Returns and **massacres the scientists**

### **The facility confrontation (Master Lore):**
- Zenshin arrives while Jashin is killing Hide, and tells him **Megami is dead**
- Hide confesses he and Zenshin conspired to kill Jashin
- The brothers fight over Megami's corpse. **Jashin is winning**
- Hide reveals the sealing technique; **Zenshin seals him**
- Jashin goes under **believing Megami is dead** (author). He never learns that Zenshin revived her and lied to her

### **The body's journey (author, 2026-10-06):**
- After the escape, Kami and Megami **bury** the comatose body
- Years later, **Kami confesses to Megami** what really happened (Kami ~40). Afterwards **Megami goes back to the burial site** to see if he is still there, **and so does Kami**
- **Kami takes his brother's body to Pristania** and hides it behind a wall in HQ
- [TBD: who reaches the grave first, and what Megami finds there]

### **The sealed years:**
- **While Kami lives:** he perceives **nothing**. His consciousness is in a **void, with nothing to do: a torment**. He survives the solitude **with his imagination, imagining all kinds of things** (author)
- **After Kami's death:** the seal weakens (Kami's power is fragmented). His consciousness partially returns; he still cannot move or speak, but can influence his surroundings (Hirohiko's profile). Develops the healing spear (author)
- **The transfer:** notices Gyrus imprisoned in the next room; over the years of the coma, **transfers his consciousness into him**. His body stays behind the wall, alive and empty (Hirohiko's profile)
- **The escape:** takes control of Gyrus, walks him out of Pristania HQ to the **Ryakujin ruins near Kamino** (his original home), feeding him healing spears; wakes Gyrus there with no memory (Hirohiko's profile)

## **ARC ROLE**

### **Arcs 1-2:** hidden inside Gyrus, who lives as Dr. Yoshikage Araki near the ruins

### **Arc 3:**
- **Senses Lopney** near the coast and plants the urge to go to the beach. Gyrus meets the protagonists (author; Hirohiko's profile)

### **Arc 4:**
- At the end, **restores Gyrus's memories** and guides him to **join Free** (Hirohiko's profile)

### **Arc 5:**
- **Guides Gyrus to the protagonists** for the reunion (author)
- Empowers Gyrus for the fight with Diominiciel (God's Halberd) (Hirohiko's profile)
- **Hears Megami's account to Free** (author): Rajin is Megami, she is alive, Kami's confession, the resurrection power and its rule, and **that Kami thought about reviving him and didn't dare**

### **Arc 6 - "The Dark God Arises":**
1. **The seal breaks:** Arjan and Lopney use their 2nd resurrection (on Gallan). Kami's power ceases to exist. Jashin is free with **100% of his power** (Power System)
2. **Free, he can manifest** on his own, in his body at ~55 (author)
3. **His plan:** **take Gyrus's body** and unleash **chaos against the world** (author)
4. **The Inverse World:** he **drags Hirohiko into the Inverse World** and **confesses his intentions** (author)
5. **The fight:** both can imagine any ability there. **Jashin proves superior**: he knows the world better, uses it better, has more experience (author)

7. **The realization:** Jashin understands that **what Gyrus wants most is to be with his siblings again** (author)

9. **The revived siblings:** they come back **as adults, as they were**, but with **no memories of their past life**, like the Arjan and Lopney cycle. They do **not** keep Jashin's powers (consumed) (author). It works even though Perlereina's corpse had turned to dust (author)

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- **The mirror of Kami** (editor's reading of the author's ending): Kami spent his first resurrection on selfish love (Megami) and built a life on a lie. Jashin, the "negative god", spends **both** on someone else's family, knowing it kills him. **The most selfless act in the story belongs to the "evil god"**
- **The one who never forgave betrayal** dies giving everything to the nephew of his betrayer
- **Free will:** Gyrus believed he acted on his own while being guided; in the end Jashin, the manipulator, tells him the truth about what his heart wants
- **What people carry from violence:** decades in the void, a hatred with no living target, and a love he never admits
