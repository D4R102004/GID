## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Claudia Whannell
**Codename (Emperor):** Dragoon. Written this way so readers can spell it. It comes from "Dragun": *dragon* + *un* (雲, the on'yomi of the kanji for **cloud**, kumo)
**Alias (civilian, with Gabriella):** "Misty"
**Age:** 21 (Arc 1). About 16 when she met Gabriella
**Occupation:**
- **Nurse** at the Vista Alegre hospital. A real job. **The hospital is not part of Pristania**; Claudia is the one who helps get wounded Pristania members taken away from it
- Pristania Emperor (frontline fighter)
**Affiliation:** Pristania
**Nouryoku:** Cloud (see Powers & Combat)
**Family:** Father: James Whannell (Bellsaw, fellow Emperor, Commander in the Vista Alegre police). Mother: died in childbirth (name TBD)
**Status:** Antagonist. Recently made Emperor. Coma in Arc 4

**Rank:** Emperor alongside Bellsaw and Shigo. (There is a fourth, **secret** Emperor, Angel, known only to Perlereina and Diominiciel. **Claudia does not know he exists.** He dies in the Arc 4 finale.) Below the Apostles, above general membership, reports directly to the Kamigami. Rose through prowess; her family connection helped.

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Shy and reserved.** She doesn't let much out (the author describes her as sort of an opposite to Yonoa)
2. **Speaks little, but what she says has weight**
3. **Snaps at those she loves.** With people she knows, she opens up, and can snap at them and show she cares
4. **Determined and a hard worker**
5. **Wants things to be better.** She wants a better world
6. **Can joke**, partly because Gabriella taught her
7. **Inquisitive** (Bellsaw's profile: more inquisitive than her fanatic father)
8. **Devoted but not a fanatic.** Admires her father and sees truth in Pristania
9. **Avoids killing.** She has a healer's mindset (she is a nurse), and killing people doesn't fit in her head. She has not had many missions, and she does not kill

**Reconciling "shy" and "inquisitive":** she is a quiet observer. She questions inwardly and asks only the few people she trusts.

### **What Drives Her:**
- **A better world.** She sees in Pristania a real chance to improve it
- **Her father.** He told her Kami's ideals and she believes them; she also wants to honour him
- **Being part of something great.** She feels honoured to belong to Pristania
- Gabriella. She values her deeply

### **What Makes Her Happy:**
- Gabriella: talking when bored, and volleyball together
- Time with her father
- [TBD: others]

### **What Terrifies Her:**
- **Losing her loved ones**
- **Fear for Gabriella.** Once she discovers Gabriella is the Metal Knight, she is terrified and does not know how to resolve it
- Watching her father die helpless (her turning point)

### **Fatal Flaws:**
- **Her shyness makes her stay silent when she should speak.** She never told Gabriella she was an Emperor, and never told her father about her worries
- **Overlooking:** she doesn't connect the clues about Gabriella until it is too late
- **Fear.** Fear for the people she loves can break her concentration, and her form

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Cloud**

**The Power (author):** she can become a cloud, in whole or in part. Fighting her is fighting a cloud.

**Abilities:**
- **Manifest parts of her body as clouds**, or her whole body
- **Discharge lightning and rain**, like a cloud would
- **Incorporeality:** she can pass as a cloud, without a physical body
- **Regeneration:** she absorbs **nearby clouds** to restore parts of her body
- **Clones:** she can turn parts of her cloud into **clones of herself**
- **Flight**
- **Disperse** her body

**Author's assessment:** devastating, top tier. The protagonists have to *figure out how to beat a cloud*. Not invincible.

**Limits & Weaknesses (author):**
- **Strong wind scatters her.** Yonoa's tornadoes are the hard counter
- **Losing concentration / emotional shock** breaks her form
- **She can be damaged in corporeal form**
- **Parts of her body that stay dispersed are lost:** if the scattered parts do not return to her, she cannot reintegrate them (author). She needs more clouds to recover
- **Enclosed spaces are a disadvantage:** no clouds nearby to regenerate
- **Clones are weaker than her** and vanish when hit
- No further limits beyond wind and concentration


### **Combat Style:** frontline fighter
- Emperor by prowess; fights on the front line (unlike her father, who has no Nouryoku and never fights directly)
- **She does not kill.** She avoids it when she can and has not had many missions. **The exception is the Yonoa fight:** she starts it wanting to kill, then **doubts mid-fight**
- [TBD: martial training, weapons, signature techniques, whether she uses the umbrella]

### **Matchup Note (Yonoa):**
- Yonoa's **tornadoes and Aeronaval Gigablasts** are the counter: they scatter her. In Arc 4, Yonoa beats her badly with them and she falls into a coma
- **Editor's proposal (not confirmed):** her doubt mid-fight is the *concentration/emotional shock* weakness in action: hesitation breaks her form, and Yonoa's wind scatters what breaks off. This makes the loss come from her character as well as the matchup
- Because she is a cloud, the tornadoes cannot easily kill her. **Editor's proposal:** they scatter her until only a core survives, which is why she ends in a coma rather than dead

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

**Editor's proposal (based on established traits):**
- **The believer who asks questions.** Her father is the fanatic; she is the one who could doubt
- **Mutual protection, mutual secrets.** She and Gabriella are each other's shelter, and each is hiding which side she is on
- **A believer who finally finds a Pristania she can believe in (editor's proposal).** Her wish for a better world and her refusal to kill match Hirohiko's peaceful leadership, so following him answers the question her father never asked. Hirohiko is also a doctor and she is a nurse
- **Persuasion, twice.** Bellsaw believed he could talk the protagonists out of the war; now Claudia is sent to talk Gabriella out of hers. It is her father's method, and it will fail the same way (editor's proposal)
- **She refuses to kill, then wants to.** She disobeys Perlereina's order to kill her best friend, then later goes after Yonoa to kill for her father
- **Caught between a friend and a cause.** She discovers the friend she is protecting is fighting the cause she believes in, and her silence and her fear leave her unable to save either
- **The cost of the family cause.** She loses her father and her friend to it, and sleeps through its end
