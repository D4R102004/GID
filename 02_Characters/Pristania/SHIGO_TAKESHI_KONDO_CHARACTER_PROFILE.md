## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Takeshi Kondo (近藤毅)
**Codename (Emperor):** Shigo (死後, "after death") — **the name comes directly from his Nouryoku**: the ability to wield the powers of the already-dead
**Age:** 15 (Arc 1-2). The **youngest Emperor** — a child prodigy
**Occupation:**
- **CEO of DeathCake**, a successful confectionery and bakery company in Vista Alegre — in truth. He is **too young to appear publicly as CEO**, so his secretary fronts the company. The public believes the secretary is the CEO; this is false. Shigo, trained by his father in business from a young age, actually runs everything behind the scenes
- Pristania Emperor
**Affiliation:** Pristania
**Nouryoku:** 死後 (Shigo) — see Powers & Combat
**Family:** Sister: **Aoi** (real name **Mizuki Kondo**), his only family. She is his elder by a wide margin — their parents had him much later than her. Father: deceased, killed in conflicts with Vista Alegre's criminal underworld (mafia); left DeathCake to Shigo as successor
**Status:** Antagonist. Already an Emperor by Arc 2. **Dies in Arc 4**

**Rank:** Emperor alongside Bellsaw and Claudia. (There is a fourth, **secret** Emperor, Angel, known only to Perlereina and Diominiciel. **Shigo does not know he exists.**) Below the Apostles, above general membership, reports directly to the Kamigami. Rose through his own talent and through Aoi's influence — Perlereina, out of affection for her former teacher, allowed it, though **not everyone in Pristania agrees with the decision**. Entered Pristania through Aoi and became Emperor **not long before Arc 2**

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

**Build:** A young teenager, unmistakably so — baby-faced. Slim, slight, almost delicate. Without his combat gear, he would read as adorable

**Face and Hair:** Short, simple black hair, cut like any teenager's. Vivid blue eyes. **He and Aoi resemble each other in the face**

**Palette:** His blue eyes give him an otherworldly quality; his black hair evokes death — fitting for the name 死後

**Emperor Outfit:**
- A Halloween-style **skull mask**
- A long, **tattered cape**
- **Bandages** wound over his torso (over the suit) and over his hands, giving him a mummy-like look

**DeathCake Heir Outfit:**
- Imposing, businesslike — **Seto Kaiba-coded**
- Deliberately formal, an attempt to **hide that he is 15**

**Posture & Body Language:** A child playing at being an adult. He is constantly trying to look "cool" and impress — which mostly reads as **comedic rather than threatening**. Look through the act and point out how ridiculous it is, and he will **give you a dirty look**

**Notes for the artist:** Emperor outfit and DeathCake-heir outfit are two deliberately contrasting designs for the same body — a fragile, adorable teenager underneath a death-cult mummy-mask silhouette, and underneath a would-be tycoon's suit. All three looks are confirmed canon (no [TBD] on design intent, only on final linework)

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Anxious to prove his worth** — constantly performing competence
2. **Arrogant on the surface, genuinely self-assured underneath.** He really does feel *badass*, not just pretends to
3. **Can place duty above family ties**
4. **Reasonable — doesn't kill for killing's sake.** In this restraint he echoes Kami's ideology, and his own family's values
5. **A child performing adulthood.** Tries to sound older than he is through vocabulary and posture, which tends to produce comic situations
6. **Snaps into irritation** when someone sees through the act and says so

### **What Drives Him:**
- **Clearing his sister's name** and honoring the legacy Aoi left in Pristania — this is **central**
- **Child-prodigy ambition**
- **Platonic devotion to his mentor, Perlereina** — felt deeply, shown rarely

### **What Makes Him Happy:**
- **Practicing his stolen repertoire alone**, a hobby he keeps up on a mountain he goes to specifically for this
- Feeling **powerful and important**, and being around powerful people — Perlereina above all
- **The easy life** his money affords him
- **Time with Aoi** — it comforts him, because he sees himself as redeeming her

### **What Terrifies Him:**
- **Failure**
- **Not being good enough**
- **Failing Perlereina**, his idol

### **Fatal Flaws:**
- **He overestimates himself** and tends to act on his own judgment without listening to advice
- **Blind adoration of Perlereina**

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: 死後 (Shigo — "After Death")**

**The Power (author):** He ingests something carrying another person's DNA — hair, most commonly — and in doing so copies their Nouryoku **in its entirety**.

**Mechanics:**
- **Full power requires the target to be dead.** Against a living target, he can still extract an object and gain their power, but this **costs him enormous energy**, so he does not use it indiscriminately — in practice, he overwhelmingly favors dead sources
- He can only have **one Nouryoku active at a time.** Switching to another requires consuming an object from that person
- Each object grants a **fixed number of uses**
- If the copied Nouryoku is energy-costly for its original owner, **Shigo pays that same cost physically** when he uses it

**Reach and limits relative to existing canon:**
- **Kami** qualifies in principle (he is dead), but **Shigo has not obtained his hair or any object of his** — he has no access yet. This stays an open thread
- **Protos (Kensuke)** also qualifies, since Kensuke is dead
- **The Arc 6 resurrection is out of his reach.** That requires being a designated **recipient** of Kami's powers, which Shigo is not

**Source of powers:** In theory, any dead person in the world. In practice, he **overwhelmingly collects from enemies he kills himself**, which keeps his arsenal unpredictable fight to fight. Example: having killed **Tased** at the end of Arc 2, he later wields Tased's Nouryoku against **Lopney** in Arc 4

### **Combat Style:**
- **Avoids melee when he can** — he can defend himself competently, but it's not his preference
- **Strategist.** Overwhelms opponents by cycling through his repertoire until he finds something that works against them
- **Can lose the plan to his own immaturity mid-fight** — he is, after all, a teenager

## **BACKSTORY**

- Born Takeshi Kondo, younger brother of Mizuki Kondo. Their parents had him **much later** than her
- **Father:** built DeathCake, a real family-owned confectionery and bakery company in Vista Alegre. Killed in **conflicts with the local mafia**, leaving Shigo as successor
- **Too young to front the company publicly**, Shigo had his secretary pose as CEO instead. The public believes the secretary runs DeathCake; in truth, Shigo — trained by his father in business from an early age — controls everything from behind
- **Entered Pristania through Aoi**, who had been Empress (succeeding Shigeru and Kensuke in the seat) before later resigning
- **Rose to Emperor** through his own talent, helped by Aoi's influence and Perlereina's affection for her former student — a decision **not universally supported** within Pristania
- Already an Emperor by Arc 2, having become one **not long before**
