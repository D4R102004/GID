# **LAND (HEAVY UNIT L411D) - COMPLETE CHARACTER PROFILE**

---
ROLE: Antagonist (Arc 4, Arc 5); Bellsaw's first creation; first of Pristania's four units
FACTION: Pristania (technological unit)
STATUS: Survives. Shut down by Elysia in Arc 5; reactivated by Pristania and serves Hirohiko in Arc 6
AGE_ARC_1: A robot; built 20+ years before Arc 1, while Kami was still alive
---

## **BASIC INFO**

**Designation:** Heavy Unit L411D ("L411D" is LAND written in numbers)
**Name:** **Land** (地)
**Builder:** **Bellsaw**, alone. Land is his first contribution to Pristania's firepower and his primordial creation
**Second form:** given to him later by **Diominiciel** (when: TBD)
**Series:** first of Pristania's four units: **Land** (first) → **Sea** → **Air** → **Space** (last)
**Gender:** male (this profile uses "he")
**Status:** Survives the story. Shut down in Arc 5; back in service in Arc 6

---

## **THE NAME**

- **Land** is his place in Pristania's series of units: Land, Sea, Air, Space
- The designation **L411D** spells LAND in numbers
- He is the land unit: Sea is his counterpart for the sea (see Key Relationships)

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

### **First form: the tank**
- A **war tank built like a tower**: a tall armored block sits on the hull
- **Tracks / big wheels** (six large wheels in the reference)
- A **long main cannon** at the front
- **No face.** Only a **visor** at the front. The visor does not express emotion

### **Second form: the humanoid**
- He **sheds all his tank armor** and stands up as a **humanoid robot**, still clearly mechanical
- **Smaller and more agile** than the tank, but **tall** for a humanoid
- Slim, **jointed** frame; **long, sturdy arms**; **metal legs**
- **Small wheels in the feet** (author approved), a remnant of the tank
- The same **mouthless gaze**: no face, still no expressions

### **The core**
- A core with the kanji **地**. It is what controls him (like Sea's **海**)
- Elysia shuts him down by **piercing the core** (Arc 5)

### **Visual references**
- Shared by the author: a toy-like grey six-wheeled tank with a tall turret block (first form); a jointed, mouthless robot from official Yu-Gi-Oh! card art (second form)
- **Shape inspiration only**: the character is "based on" the references, not a copy. Distinctive elements of the references are **not** canon: the tank's orange crossed-scimitar emblem and red cannon tip; the humanoid's red cone helmet, yellow lightning bolts, green eye, red arm plates and color scheme

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits (first form):**
- **A soldier.** Does what he was programmed to do: **serve Pristania**
- Sees himself as **the leader of the robotic units**, in a sense: the eldest, and the one with the most destructive power
- Does not interact much in tank form
- **Ruthless.** Bellsaw's death gives him more drive to carry out his mission and makes him crueler

### **The friction in his programming (second form):**
- In the second form he becomes **someone who likes to test himself**: how far can a machine go?
- **Proud of being a robot**
- **Seeks fights**: wants to measure himself against strong enemies
- **Shows respect for his rivals**
- *Editor's reading (not author canon; use lightly):* the first form carries Bellsaw (the engineer: purpose, duty, a weapon for Pristania); the second carries Diominiciel (the scientist who creates out of passion for research: curiosity about himself). The friction would be his two makers inside him. It also echoes Kami, who saw Land as **something new for the world**: in the second form Land believes it

### **What Drives Him:**
- **His programming**: serving Pristania
- **Bellsaw's death**: carrying on the mission of the father who made him
- (Second form) **Proving what a machine can do**

### **Fatal Flaw:**
- **Bellsaw.** He cannot stop thinking about his father, and that is what lets Elysia in (see Powers & Combat)

## **VOICE & DIALOGUE**

- **Mechanical voice.** He speaks **like a programming language**: conditionals, loops, operators
- His emotion lives **only in his voice and the way he speaks**, never in his visor
- **Same speech in both forms** (author)

### **Known lines (author):**
> "IF Free forces, THEN laughter."
> "IF Land destroyed, THEN insignificant."
> "WHILE clones NOT damage Land, THEN victory EQUALS secured."

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **First form: tank**
- Everything a real war tank has, and more
- **Main cannon**
- **Tomahawk missiles**
- **Minigun**
- **Runs people over**
- Heavy armor; the most destructive power of the four units
- **Combat style:** a fortress on wheels. Overwhelms by firepower and mass

### **Second form: humanoid**
- Revealed **when the tank is disabled**: he drops the armor and fights on
- **Smaller and agile**; moves on the wheels in his feet
- **Hand-to-hand combat** with his long, sturdy arms
- **Small missiles** and other **ranged attacks**
- **Combat style:** a duelist. This is the form that seeks fights and respects rivals
- In this form he **defeats every Free fighter** in the Arc 5 battle

### **Weak point:**
- **The core (地).** Piercing it shuts him down

### **Against Elysia (Arc 5):**
- Elysia's Nouryoku takes the form of **the person the victim is thinking about most**. With Land, she becomes **Bellsaw**. She does not choose it: **Land cannot stop thinking about his father**
- Land **hesitates**. In that moment of doubt Elysia **slips behind him and pierces his core**, shutting him down
- The grief that made him more ruthless is the opening that beats him

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arcs 1-3:**
- **Not shown.** The reader sees him for the first time in Arc 4

### **Arc 4 - "Into the City of the Misbegotten":**
- **First appearance.** Goro signals the Kiryuin mansion as his location, drawing L411D away from Ichiro and the others
- **Diominiciel and Shiro (as Zafiro)** are present
- Land fights **in his first form**: **destroys the Kiryuin mansion**, destroys **every one of Goro's clones**, and leaves **Goro critically wounded**
- Knows of Bellsaw's death this arc; it makes him more ruthless

### **Arc 5 - "A Sanctuary of Peace and Order":**
- Defends Pristania HQ against **Tairin, Terror, Policia and Uragaeshi**
- The four **disable the tank**. Land **sheds his armor and reveals his second form**
- **Incapacitates Terror**, then **defeats Policia and Uragaeshi**, leaving both dying
- **Elysia arrives**, becomes **Bellsaw** in her dimension, and while Land hesitates **pierces his core from behind**. Land is **shut down**, defeated

### **Arc 6 - "The Dark God Arises":**
- **Reactivated by Pristania**. Continues serving, now under **Hirohiko**
