# **SEA (SURVEY UNIT S34) - COMPLETE CHARACTER PROFILE**

---
ROLE: Antagonist (Arc 3); Perlereina's beloved robot
FACTION: Pristania (technological unit)
STATUS: Dies Arc 3 (destroyed by Yonoa); his core (海) becomes Perlereina's necklace
AGE_ARC_1: A robot; built for Perlereina's birthday when she was little, upgraded ever since
---

## **BASIC INFO**

**Designation:** Survey Unit S34
**Name:** **Sea** (海)
**Builders:** **Diominiciel**, with **Bellsaw**: Sea began as a small robot and both kept adding functions as Perlereina grew
**Owner / best friend:** **Perlereina Dei**
**Series:** second of Pristania's four units: **Land** (first) → **Sea** → **Air** → **Space** (last)
**Gender:** none in theory; **sees himself as a man** (this profile uses "he")
**Status:** Destroyed by Yonoa (Arc 3). First of Perlereina's losses

---

## **THE NAME**

- **Sea** is his place in Pristania's series of units: Land, Sea, Air, Space
- He was already called Sea as the little birthday robot
- It also fits what he became: **Pristania's best asset for naval combat**

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

### **Today (Arc 3):**
- A **big robot, the size of a plane**
- **Armored, metallic** color
- **Two missile rails** along his sides
- **Thrusters** at the back
- **His face is a screen** at the front: a smiley face drawn over a **grid**, making **expressions**
- **The core** sits **behind the face**: a sphere with the kanji **海**. It is what controls him
- **Visual references:** shared by the author (a round armored robot with hexagonal pods; a hovering saucer-like Pokémon). Shape inspiration only: the character is "based on" the references, not a copy. Distinctive elements of the references (yellow-black stripes, magnets, antenna, single central eye) are **not** canon

### **Origin:**
- A **little robot**, a toy-sized birthday present. Upgraded step by step into what he is now

---

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
- **Arrogant**: a proud Pristania robot
- **Sense of duty**
- **Somewhat insecure**
- **Feels in control** of every situation
- Much like **Perlereina** in this

### **What Drives Him:**
- **His love for Perlereina**: he wants to show her how much he loves her (why he disobeys in Arc 3)

### **What Terrifies Him:**
- **Failing Perlereina**

### **Fatal Flaw:**
- **Overconfidence**: he believes he controls the fight, and disobeys the plan to prove his love

---

## **VOICE & DIALOGUE**

- **Mechanical, robotic voice**
- His screen-face **gestures** as he speaks
- Provokes enemies with **Pristania pride**
- Strikes where it hurts, using what Pristania knows (Free's attack, Yonoa's losses)

### **Known line (ARC_3_SUMMARY, paraphrase):**
> He has Perlereina; Yonoa has no father, was betrayed by her best friend, and will die alone.

---

## **BACKSTORY**

### **The Birthday Present:**
- **Diominiciel gave him to Perlereina for her birthday** when she was little
- **Her first bond outside the family**: imagine your best friend was given to you as a toy on your birthday
- How they started to bond is the heart of the character [scene TBD]

### **Growing Up Together:**
- As Perlereina grew, **Diominiciel and Bellsaw kept upgrading him**: surveillance, reconnaissance, companionship, and in time combat (a survey unit can carry arms, like the Survey Corps in *Attack on Titan*)
- Became Pristania's best naval-combat asset

---

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

- **Flight**, propelled by rear thrusters
- **Underwater**: can dive and fight below the surface
- **Missiles** (side rails) and **side cannons**
- **Ramming**: rams his opponents
- **Drops bombs**
- **Carries a person inside** (Lyzander, Arc 3) and can transport Perlereina
- **Linked alarm**: Perlereina's clothes sound an alarm when Sea is in danger
- **Combat style:** a heavy combat drone, fought creatively
- **Weak point:** the core behind the face. Destroy it and he dies

---

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Perlereina Dei:**
- **Best friends, genuinely.** He loves her most of all
- Her first bond outside her family
- Assistant, confidant, friend; family, because her brother made him
- She **never neglects him** for Glayne
- After his death she **wears his core as a necklace** from then on

### **Diominiciel Dei and Bellsaw:**
- His **builders**: he sees them as **mechanics**, and likes them

### **Glayne Byronstrike:**
- **Somewhat jealous**, but his love for Perlereina is stronger than the jealousy

### **Land, Air, Space:**
- **Like rival siblings**; they interact at Pristania HQ
- **Land** sees Sea as his counterpart (land / sea) and mourns him like a fallen comrade (LAND_L411D_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

### **Yonoa Houzu:**
- His enemy and his killer (Arc 3)

### **Lyzander Sugimori:**
- The prisoner he is meant to carry to Pristania (Arc 3); Yonoa finds him unconscious inside

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 3 - "5 Souls to the Rescue":**
- **Carries Perlereina** to the sea route
- **The plan:** attack to distract and hold the heroes at sea, then leave; Perlereina teleports Lyzander inside him; he takes the prisoner to Pristania; she finishes the rest and blows up the ship
- **Disobeys:** to show Perlereina how much he loves her, he waits for Yonoa to chase him and leads her where he thinks he can win
- **Yonoa vs. Sea:** he provokes her as a proud Pristania robot; a savage fight; she wrecks his side cannons; no missile or turbine stops the Logia
- **Last defiance:** he has Perlereina; Yonoa has no father, was betrayed by her best friend, and will die alone
- Yonoa answers with tornado after tornado until only the core remains, and smashes it. **Sea dies.** She finds Lyzander unconscious inside
- **Aftermath:** his alarm pulls Perlereina off the ship before she finishes the twins. She finds his remains, punches a **crater** into the island, and takes his **core (海)**, which she wears as a **necklace** from then on. At the Kamino beach, Glayne helps her bear the loss
- **First of Perlereina's losses**

  - He attacks Yonoa's solitude with the one thing he is sure of: he has Perlereina
