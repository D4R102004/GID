## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

### **Body:**
- An **android**: a robot **covered in skin**. Looks and walks like a person (think **Android #16**, *Dragon Ball*)
- **You cannot tell he is a machine at a glance.** You would have to find out by hurting him, or something like it
- **His look is sinister.** This is a character who has to read as a villain
- **Face:** inhuman in a sense. His **eyes are light bulbs with skin stretched over them**: sinister eyes
- **Hair:** open between **long white**, **red** or **blue** (author likes all three; decide with the design)
- **A button panel on his arm**, which he uses to activate his combat system

### **What he can do with his body:**
- **Turns parts of his body to metal** at will
- **Removes pieces of his skin** and puts them back on
- **Removes limbs or his head** and keeps functioning
- **Does not bleed: he leaks oil**
- **His skin is very hard**

### **The core:**
- In his **heart**, under that hard skin. **Piercing it is the only way to kill him**

### **Clothing:**
- Dresses **cool, in human fashion**: a **grey turtleneck**, a **long black coat**, **dark trousers**
- **Visual reference:** shared by the author for the **clothing only**. The reference's face, skin, hair and build are **not** canon

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
- **Wants to be human.** He knows he is a machine, but **considers himself a person**. Opposite to Land, who is proud of being a robot
- **Fascinated by human life**, by mundane things: **walking, having an ice cream**. Sees humans as **very interesting creatures**
- **Loves feeling emotions**
- **Optimistic.** Wants to see the world as **wonderful**, and likes being part of it. To him **the world is a beautiful temple**, and he loves humans
- **Overflows with personality**, despite being an android
- **What he likes he enjoys; what he doesn't, he can't stand**
- **Doesn't take things too seriously**
- **But efficient and cruel**

### **What Drives Him:**
- **To be human** and **live in the world of humans**. Impossible, so he shows it by adopting human attitudes and doing human things, and enjoys them
- **To leave a mark on the world as the first human machine**
- **Devotion to Pristania**: he admires **Kami's idea of improving the world**

### **What Terrifies Him:**
- **That people will despise him if they find out he is a machine**

### **Sinister by look and by act:**
- The menace comes from both: the light-bulb eyes, the face that is almost right, and a man who enjoys a fight like a pleasant chat while being cruel and efficient

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

**Not a Nouryoku. Technology** (Diominiciel's). It has **no name**: it is simply his way of fighting.

### **The cubicle:**
- He releases **four metal pieces** that scatter across a range and extend, enclosing **him and his opponent in a cubicle**
- Inside, he presses **button combinations on his arm panel** to call up **interactive holographic projections he has prepared in advance**: the floor turning to spikes, a metal robot attacking, **dragons**, **traps**, **projectiles**
- In theory he can materialize **anything**, but **only inside the cubicle**
- **Only one set of holograms active at a time**

### **Augmented reality:**
- Everyone inside sees the holograms and **interacts with them as if they had mass**
- **They hurt but do not wound**: a holographic warrior's knife through you is **felt**, but you do not get cut or bleed (like Yu-Gi-Oh! anime duels, where duelists take direct attacks from dragons and knights without real injury)
- Riding a materialized plane inside the cubicle, he might "fly"; but he does not fly

### **The real weapons:**
- He exploits the fact that holograms don't wound: from the **walls or floor of the cubicle** he makes **real metal weapons** appear (guns, launchers) that **do** cause damage
- The fight becomes a question of **what is real**. Inside the cubicle he has every kind of advantage

### **How to fight him:**
- **You have to use your intelligence**
- **Weak point:** the core in his heart, behind very hard skin. Taking off his head or limbs does not stop him

### **Combat style:**
- **Plays with his victims**, but remains **very efficient**
- Treats the fight as a **conversation**: opens up to see the **potential of the humans** who fight him
- **Enjoys it enormously**
