# **RAJIN / MEGAMI / MEI DEI - COMPLETE CHARACTER PROFILE**

## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Megami (女神) — true name; abandoned after Kami's confession
**Codename:** Mei Dei
**Current Alias:** Rajin (雷神) — permanent disguise maintained for 30+ years
**Age:** ~40s (Arc 1)
**Occupation:** Free member (non-combatant, wandering); acting Commander of Free (end of Arc 2 onward, during Meiji's capture)
**Status:** Supporting character; becomes central pillar in Arc 5
**Family:**
- Kami/Zenshin (ex-husband — married after resurrection, separated after his confession)
- Jashin (former lover — killed during Ryakujin tests, resurrected by Kami, buried comatose)
- Kensuke Sugimori (current husband — the only living person who knows she is Megami)
- Meiji (adoptive son — unaware of her true identity; she unaware he is a Ryakujin weapon)
- Lyzander Sugimori (adoptive grandson)
- Diominiciel Dei (biological son — estranged, does not know she is alive in Free)
- Hirohiko Dei (biological son — estranged, amnesiac)
- Perlereina Dei (biological daughter — estranged; kills Megami in Arc 5)

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

### **Current Form — "Rajin" (Permanent Disguise)**

**Build:**
- Slim, stylized, deliberate femininity
- Not soft — composed. Every element of her appearance projects command
- She looks like someone who does not show weakness because she has decided not to

**Face & Hair:**
- **Long pink hair** — very similar to Perlereina's when untied; same color
- **Pink eyes** — match the hair; arresting
- The overall impression is of someone elegant and poised in a way that reads as authority, not ornament

**Style:**
- Commandment aesthetic — not ostentatious, but nothing about her invites casual treatment
- Carries herself with the bearing of someone who could lead an organization if she had to
- No unnecessary softness in her presentation

**The Unconscious Echo:**
- Her Rajin form resembles Perlereina — her youngest daughter
- This may be subconscious: something in Megami reached toward Perlereina when she reinvented herself
- The reader will notice this before they know why

---

### **True Form — "Megami"**

**Build:**
- Small woman; noticeably shorter
- Less imposing physically, but not fragile
- Before the confession she carried herself with ease and playfulness; now there is no true form in public

**Face & Hair:**
- **Short pink hair** — same pink as Rajin, but cropped; simpler
- **Blue eyes** — distinct from Rajin's pink; a different person lives here
- Her true face resembles Meiji — this is structurally intentional: Meiji was modeled on Megami by Ryakujin
- It also carries features recognizable to a perceptive eye as the mother of the Kamigami
- Both Meiji and Perlereina share something in her face — sworn enemies, same mother

**Distinctive Mark:**
- **Left eye is blackened** (Ryakujin design mark)
- Kami has normal eyes. Jashin has both eyes blackened. Megami, the neutral prototype, has one of each.
- She hides this permanently under her disguise
- She will notice that Lopney's eyes carry the same mark — and she will say nothing

**What the True Form Represents:**
- She almost never shows it
- The only people who have seen it in the present story are Kensuke — and briefly, Hirohiko, at the moment of revelation
- It is the face she had when she was happy. Before Kami confessed. It belongs to a person who no longer exists in full.

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Before Kami's Confession (Megami)**
- Playful, warm, capable of levity
- A genuine mediator — not just by design, but by temperament
- Loved Kami. Raised three children. Had a life.
- This version of her is not gone, but it is buried very deep.

### **After Kami's Confession (The Transformation)**
- Changed for good the moment Kami told the truth
- Did not gradually harden — it happened in one night
- She reinvented her face, her name, her bearing
- She became Rajin: composed, performatively normal, capable of projecting authority
- Over decades, the mask and the woman fused. She is both Megami and Rajin now — an amalgamation. She could not fully separate them even if she tried.

### **Present Day (Arc 1 — Early Arc 2)**
- Detached as a default — emotionally present in small doses, absent in large stretches
- With family (Kensuke, Meiji, Lyzander) she is warmer — closer to Megami
- But deep remorse over Kami's death and its consequences colors everything
- Her behavior is marked by:
  - **Resignation** — she sees what she has done and does not argue with it
  - **Sadness** — carried quietly, not performed
  - **Escape** — she travels constantly; her power means she can become anyone, go anywhere; she uses this to forget
  - **Love, still** — she shows up for her family. She feels. She just cannot stop long enough to fully reckon with what she feels.
- Not suicidal. Not broken. Still living — but living as someone running from herself.

### **When Kensuke Dies / Meiji is Captured (End of Arc 2)**
- She takes the blame on herself for not being there
- This guilt does not destroy her — it ignites her
- She steps forward and becomes the acting commander of Free
- In this role, something of the original Megami resurfaces: strategic, clear-eyed, calm under pressure
- People already respect Rajin. They feel safe with her. She does not disappoint them.
- She proves her intelligence in decision-making during the worst possible circumstances
- This is not a recovery. This is a woman using action to outrun grief — but it is also genuinely her, finally standing still enough to matter.

### **Fatal Flaw**
- She chose revenge over her children. She knows this.
- She has never allowed herself to fully face it — she keeps moving so she does not have to.
- Her arc is about stopping. Turning around. Too late.

---

## **DEFINING TRAITS**

1. **The weight of correct choices made for wrong reasons** — Kami deserved to die. She still grieves the damage it caused.
2. **Love expressed through action, not presence** — she shows up when it counts; she disappears when it doesn't cost anything.
3. **Guilt that does not perform itself** — she does not weep openly. She just leaves. She just runs.
4. **Competence that surprises no one who knows her** — when she steps up in Arc 5, it feels inevitable.
5. **A mother who forfeited the name** — she thinks about her children. She does not seek them. She thinks she lost the right.

---

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Kensuke Sugimori (Her Husband)**
- The true love of her life
- She tells him when she leaves. He accepts it. He understands her grief, even when it takes her away from him.
- The distance is a source of quiet tension — not resentment, but the longing of a man who loves someone who cannot stop moving
- He holds her secret with pride, not burden. She asked him to. That is enough for him.
- His death is the event that stops her. She cannot run from that.

### **Meiji (Her Adoptive Son)**
- She treats him with genuine mother's love
- She does not know he is a Ryakujin weapon. She found a baby and raised him.
- Their dynamic is complicated in the aftermath of Kami's death — Meiji falls into guilt and depression; she does too; they are both absent in different ways
- She loves him. The irony is that she raised her dead husband's killer and loves him completely.

### **The Kamigami (Her Biological Children)**
- She knows what they have become
- She does not seek them out. She thinks of them as a mark of Kami's lies as much as her own children — the complication is real and unresolved.
- She does not watch them, but she thinks about them. About everything.
- She believes Megami is a thing of the past. She will not reach back for it if she can avoid it.
- She contacts them for the first time in Arc 5 — at the point of no return.

### **Hirohiko (The Reunion)**
- The most important single moment in her arc before her death
- When she reveals herself, she drops her true form in front of him
- Hirohiko bursts into tears — and simultaneously, his amnesia breaks
- She returns to Rajin quickly. There is no time. The world moves on immediately.
- But the moment happened. She saw her son's face.

### **Perlereina (Her Daughter — Her Killer)**
- Perlereina kills her in Arc 5 while Megami is saving Meiji
- Perlereina has already absorbed Kami's 40% and believes herself a fusion entity called "Paladio" — she is not fully lucid
- But she does realize she killed her mother
- She does not stop. To her, Megami was a disgrace: she abandoned her children, raised their father's killer, and brought this war to their doorstep.
- It is complicated. The narrative does not simplify it.
- Megami's last act is to apologize to both Perlereina and Meiji and ask them to live together without violence.
- She dies before she knows if they heard her.

### **Jashin**
- Her first love; killed during Ryakujin tests; resurrected by Kami, then sealed comatose
- She could not kill Kami even when she wanted to — her power forbids it
- She could not free Jashin either
- The full emotional weight of this relationship is part of what broke her when Kami confessed

## **SECRETS & KNOWLEDGE**

### **What Rajin/Megami Knows:**
- Her own true identity (obviously)
- That she is one of three Ryakujin prototypes
- The full history of the facility — Kami, Jashin, Hide, the conspiracy
- That Meiji killed Kami (she arranged for it, in effect, by raising him)
- That the Kamigami are her biological children
- Free HQ's location — she reveals this at the revelation scene

### **What She Does NOT Know:**
- That Meiji is a Ryakujin weapon (she found a baby; she never knew his origin)
- That Arjan and Lopney exist and carry Kami's reincarnated fragments
- Why Lopney has a blackened eye (she notices; she has no answer)

### **What the Audience Knows (That She Doesn't):**
- Meiji was designed by the same facility that created her, using her as a model
- Her "son" is her own biological mirror as a weapon
- The Kamigami and Meiji are her children on two sides of the same war — she is the mother of both camps
- The irony of her situation is total: the woman designed to be neutral has caused more division than anyone

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Cartoon Transfiguration**

**Core Ability:**
- Can transform into anything — objects, people, animals, abstractions
- Follows cartoon/toon physics: reality-bending, defying normal physical limits
- Reference: "The Mask" (Jim Carrey) — that register of impossibility
- The limit is her imagination, not any internal mechanics
- She can also transform individual body parts independently

**Copy Component:**
- She can replicate the appearance of any person perfectly
- She can approximate some abilities she has observed through physical mimicry
- She does NOT permanently steal or retain others' Nouryoku — their power stays with them

**Current Primary Usage:**
- The Rajin disguise — maintained permanently for 30+ years
- Different face, different voice, different mannerisms from Megami
- Only Kensuke has ever seen her drop it voluntarily (in private)
- She drops it once in front of Hirohiko at the revelation scene — then immediately returns to Rajin

**Power Scaling:** God-Tier (alongside Kami, Jashin, and Meiji's contingency form)

**Combat Applications:**
- Versatile offense and defense — she can become anything
- Reality-bending evasion
- Infiltration and impersonation
- Creative problem-solving in any situation

### **Fatal Limitation: Pacifism Lock**

- **She cannot kill**
- Hardcoded into her power by Ryakujin designers
- She can brutally harm, incapacitate, and torture
- But the final killing blow always fails: weapon breaks, attack misses, target survives
- She discovered this on the field when she was sent to the city by Ryakujin in her early life
- She feels nothing dramatic about it. It is a limitation she knows she has.
- Thematic weight: The mediator cannot destroy. She could never kill Kami herself. She had to build someone else to do it.

### **The Ryakujin Design Mark:**
- Left eye is blackened in her true form
- Kami = normal eyes (positive). Jashin = both eyes blackened (negative). Megami = one of each (neutral).
- She hides this under her disguise at all times.
- When she notices Lopney's eyes carry a similar mark, she wonders — but reaches no conclusion. She says nothing.

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 1 (A Wondrous Island)**
- Not present on the island
- Exists within Free; emotionally detached and traveling
- No active role

### **Arc 2 (The Land of the Free)**
- Present at Free HQ in the Rajin persona
- Peripheral for most of the arc — the story focuses on Arjan and Lopney entering Free
- Warmer in private with Meiji, Kensuke, Lyzander
- Publicly composed and detached

**End of Arc 2 — The Pivot:**
- Kensuke dies at the climax (Shiro exits his body, leaves him mortally wounded)
- Meiji is taken captive by Pristania in the same sequence of events
- She takes responsibility — blames herself for not being present
- Steps forward as acting Commander of Free
- **This is her first true narrative arrival**
- Proves her strategic intelligence; people follow her
- She becomes the central pillar holding Free together during the worst situation it has ever faced

### **Arc 3 (5 Souls to the Rescue) — Arc 4 (Vista Alegre)**
- Continues as acting Commander throughout both arcs
- Manages Free's operations and strategic decisions during Meiji's captivity
- Holds the organization together while the protagonists work toward the rescue
- Her presence in the story grows — no longer background

### **Arc 5 — The Revelation (Pre-Assault on Pristania HQ)**

**The Scene:**
- Free is planning the assault on Pristania HQ to rescue Meiji
- Their plan depends on Uragaeshi — who has been embedded in the Kamigami's manor — knowing Free HQ's location inside Pristania
- He does not know
- They are stuck
- Then Hirohiko appears. He says he knew he would find them there.
- Rajin had not planned to reveal anything. She had intended to carry this to her grave.
- She looks at the situation: her captive son, Hirohiko standing in front of her, the grief of Lyzander, the death of Gabriella, the death of Kensuke, the entire weight of what this war has cost everyone in this room.
- She snaps.
- **She reveals herself — Mei Dei. The former wife of Kami. She knows where Free HQ is inside Pristania.**
- She drops her true form in front of Hirohiko.
- Hirohiko sees his mother's face. His amnesia breaks. He bursts into tears.
- She returns to Rajin immediately. There is no time.
- Everything moves on.

**Present at the Revelation:**
- All surviving Free members and remnants
- The protagonists
- Hirohiko

### **Arc 5 — Her Death**

- During the assault on Pristania HQ
- Perlereina has already absorbed Kami's 40% power and believes herself a fusion entity called "Paladio"
- Megami is saving Meiji
- Perlereina kills her
- Perlereina is not fully lucid — but she realizes what she has done and does not stop
- To Perlereina: her mother abandoned them, raised their father's killer, and brought this war here. There is no forgiveness in her madness.
- Megami's final act: she apologizes to Perlereina and to Meiji. She asks them to live together without violence. To be happy.
- She dies before she knows if either of them heard her.

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

### **What Megami Represents:**

1. **The cost of revenge as a purpose** — she fulfilled it. She is still paying for it. This is not a story that lets revenge be satisfying.

2. **The neutral made impossible** — she was designed to mediate between extremes. She ended up the cause of the war between them. Her children are on both sides. The mediator failed the one job that mattered.

3. **The mother who forfeited the name** — she thinks about Diominiciel, Hirohiko, Perlereina. She believes she lost the right to be their mother. The story does not fully disagree with her. But it lets her try, at the very end.

4. **The irony at the center of the entire cast** — Meiji and Perlereina are enemies. They share a mother. Neither knows. She has to live with that.

5. **Love that outlasts purpose** — she raised Meiji not as a weapon but as a son. She loves Kensuke not as an ally but as her person. When everything else falls away, what she had was real. This does not redeem her. But it is true.

## **WRITING RAJIN/MEGAMI — QUICK REFERENCE**

### **Dialogue Do's:**
✅ Composed, measured, never rushed
✅ Warmth that surfaces around family — not performed, just there
✅ Silences that mean things
✅ Strategic clarity when commanding in Arc 5
✅ No self-pity — she carries guilt without announcing it
✅ Brief, precise sentences in high-stakes moments

### **Dialogue Don'ts:**
❌ Melodrama about her past
❌ Explaining her grief to people (she does not)
❌ False cheer (she is not Tairin)
❌ Detachment that reads as coldness toward loved ones

### **Action Beats:**
✅ Stillness — she does not fidget
✅ The transformation is effortless (cartoon logic; no buildup)
✅ She notices things she does not comment on (Lopney's eyes)
✅ When she drops the Rajin form, it is significant — a rare event
✅ Strategic decisions delivered calmly, without theater

### **Emotional States:**
- **Default (Rajin persona):** Composed, performatively normal, slightly distant
- **With family:** Warmer; closer to Megami; still marked by remorse
- **Traveling/alone:** Playful in small doses; mostly sad; escaping
- **Arc 5 command mode:** Clear, decisive, the most present she has been in years
- **The revelation scene:** She snaps — not into chaos, but into truth
- **Her death scene:** Quiet. Asking. Not knowing if she was heard.

## **VISUAL NOTES FOR ARTISTS**

**Rajin Form:**
- Slim, stylized build; deliberate elegance
- Long pink hair (same color as Perlereina's when untied)
- Pink eyes
- Bearing of command — not aggressive, just immovable
- Clothing that reflects authority without ostentation

**Megami Form (True):**
- Small woman; noticeably shorter
- Short pink hair
- Blue eyes
- Left eye blackened (Ryakujin mark)
- Resembles Meiji (same source material)
- Also carries features recognizable as the Kamigami's mother
- Expression: in the past, relaxed and playful; in the present, very rarely seen and carrying everything

**Key Visual Irony:**
- Rajin looks like Perlereina's mother (pink hair, similar features)
- Megami looks like Meiji's mother (structural resemblance to the man modeled on her)
- Both are true. The audience should feel this before they understand it.
