## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Yumi Akina (秋名裕美)
**Codename:** Elysia (from her Nouryoku, also named Elysia; from *Elysium*: dreamlike, otherworldly)
**Age:** 40s (Arc 1), about Terror's age
**Occupation:** Free Security member and **instructor** (trains Cristopher; oversees Tased's training). Also runs **Delicya's**, an ice cream shop at Free HQ
**Former roles:** One of the first Free Elite (三傑) with Terror and Uragaeshi; before Free, a **psychologist** with a practice in Vista Alegre
**Family:** Husband **Uragaeshi** (Kevin Raynor), believed dead, alive in Vista Alegre; daughters **Policia** (Satsuki Raynor, 21) and **Suilan** (Kelen Raynor, 18; dies Arc 2)
**Status:** Supporting

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Depressed, but going on.** She no longer sings. She keeps going above all because her husband would have wanted her and their two daughters to keep going
2. **Carries a lot inside**; distant on the surface
3. **Clinging to the past.** Sometimes struggles to move on
4. **Feels nobody understands her**; prefers to isolate herself
5. **Sometimes fails to see the treasure her daughters are to her**
6. **Prejudiced from a war wound** (see below)
7. Patient but firm as a teacher; affectionate with the young people she trusts

### **The Prejudice:**
- **Discriminates against Lyon** (the Kyousen) and **distrusts Ichiro** (a Kiryuin)
- A **war wound**: she knows what it is to lose everything and doesn't want it to happen again
- She **genuinely believes** those people are a threat

### **Revenge (her arc):**
- **Before Arc 2:** she saw revenge as something already overcome
- **After Tased's and Suilan's deaths (Arc 2):** it becomes **central to her life** and doesn't let her move on
- Resolved in Arc 5 (see Arc Role)

### **Singing:**
- Elysia was the singer of the family. She **stopped after Uragaeshi's "death"**. Suilan learned to sing for her
- **She sings again** at the end of the story

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Vista Alegre: the Psychologist**
- Yumi worked at a **psychology practice in Vista Alegre**
- She **used her Nouryoku with her patients**, and it was **genuine therapy**
- She used her abilities to survive
- The **Vista Alegre police** found out and **confiscated her clinic** (consistent with Marjenis' police and their hatred of nouryokusha; BELLSAW_JAMES_WHANNELL_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

### **Joining Free**
- Joined hoping to **earn a place to live** and to **bring about change**; she did not see Kami as viable either
- One of the very first members. **Terror, Elysia and Uragaeshi became the first Free Elite (三傑)**; Tairin was Head of Security

### **Uragaeshi**
- Met him **inside Free**. Their love story began because **he became interested in her power** (he has no Nouryoku)
- They bonded genuinely as part of the Elite: **a working relationship that escalated**
- Daughters: Policia, then Suilan

### **The 3-Year War and Uragaeshi's "Death"**
- Uragaeshi was captured by Pristania and presumed dead ("the Martyr of Free"); Suilan was a baby
- Elysia fell into a **deep depression** and stopped singing. Policia had to grow up fast

### **The Attack (5 years before Arc 1)**
- Hid with Suilan during the attack (SUILAN_KELEN_RAYNOR_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Elysia**

**Ability:** Traps the space around a person in an **empty alternate dimension**, where she must remain with her victim, and **takes the form of the person the victim is thinking about most**

**Mechanics:**
- **Form only:** copies **appearance and voice**, **not powers**
- **She knows who she becomes** with specific people (with Lyzander she always becomes **Gabriella**)
- **Exit:** the victim leaves only by **defeating Elysia** (knocking her unconscious or otherwise), or **automatically when she chooses to leave**
- **No time limit**, but Elysia must stay in the dimension with her victim
- **Works on machines** (Arc 5: against the autonomous AI Land she becomes **Bellsaw**)

**In therapy:** before Free, she used it with patients as real therapy (facing the person who occupies their mind)

**In combat:** psychological warfare. The victim has to strike the face of whoever they love or fear most, in an empty space with nothing else to look at

### **Combat Style:**
- **Technically very skilled**
- Focuses on **attacking her opponents' weak points**
- Willing to win by low deception (Land)

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Uragaeshi / Kevin Raynor (husband):**
- See Backstory. Believed dead since the war; actually alive in Vista Alegre
- **Arc 5 reunion:** after she defeats Land and saves him; **a shock for her, a joy for him**. He already knows Suilan died (Policia told him at the gathering): the family together is joyful and tragic at once. [Details TBD] (URAGAESHI_KEVIN_RAYNOR_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

### **Policia (elder daughter):**
- Loves both daughters. Sees Policia as **a reflection of her father**
- Gave her Uragaeshi's uniform (Policia dyed it black)

### **Suilan (younger daughter):**
- Sees Suilan as **a reflection of herself**
- Suilan sang for her; Elysia does not always see what she has
- **Devastated by her death** (Arc 2); one of the two people Suilan's last words are for

### **Tairin (best friend):**
- Old friends since Free's founding; tell each other everything
- **Elysia is the only exception to the couple's secret:** she knows the real reason for Tairin and Terror's divorce
- Supports Tairin through her grief

### **Terror (old Elite comrade):**
- Friends since the Elite. He knows her well and supports her in whatever he can (TERROR_EZEQUIEL_SERRANO_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- **Crossing arcs (editor's note):** he goes from revenge to letting go; she goes from having let go to being trapped by revenge

### **Tased (pupil under her oversight):**
- Tairin delegated his training oversight to her; she monitors Cristopher's treatment of him. He calls her "mylady"
- **Cares about him deeply.** One of the most affected by his death

### **Cristopher (student):**
- Respectful student-teacher relationship: she trains him in martial arts and discipline, patient but firm with a difficult student
- Travels with him to Vista Alegre (Arcs 3-4)
- **He kills Shigo** in Arc 4, taking the revenge she wanted. She only learns in Arc 5: **frustration**

### **Lyzander:**
- With him she always becomes Gabriella; it **saddens her to see him obsessed**
- Arc 2: warm with "Lyzander"; later apologizes to him for having let "Yonoa" stay with him that night

### **Light:**
- Mutual respect and affection; he is her daughters' friend. One of the three who mourn him most

### **Lyon / Ichiro:**
- Discriminates against Lyon (Kyousen); distrusts Ichiro (Kiryuin). See The Prejudice

### **Kensuke/Protos:**
- He refuses her request to use her power on Yonoa. She **prefers not to give it much importance**: worried about the situation, but glad nothing bad happened to Lyzander or anyone
- **Editor's note:** let the reader glimpse the doubt for an instant (a look, a silence), as one more silent clue against Kensuke

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 2 - "The Land of the Free":**
- **Night one:** on guard in the phone room. Greets "Lyzander" warmly; detects Yonoa's transmission and traps her. Becomes **Meiji**; almost holds back, then realizes this is not Lyzander (with him she always becomes Gabriella) and attacks anyway, as Meiji. When the necklace breaks she recognizes Yonoa as **Meiji's best friend**, apologizes, warns her the night is dangerous, and sends her to Lyzander's room while she guards the zone
- **Lyzander realizes:** comes in to Kensuke's office, hugs Lyzander and apologizes for having let "Yonoa" stay with him
- **Life in Free:** asks Kensuke to use her power on Yonoa; refused; left with a doubt she chooses not to pursue. Interacts with Arjan during his training
- **Climax:** fights **one of Jitsumoto's daimyos** during Jitsumoto's attack. **[Daimyo and fight details: TBD]**
- **Aftermath:** badly wounded. Loses Suilan and Tased: revenge becomes central

### **Arc 3 - "5 Souls to the Rescue":**
- Once recovered, she and Cristopher **find the boat Gallan arrived on, years ago**, on the island
- Free sends **both of them** to help the protagonists
- Elysia does not appear on the page, but she is known to be **around**

### **Arc 4 - "Into the City of the Misbegotten":**
- In Vista Alegre with Cristopher. She **may meet** Arjan with him [TBD]
- Cristopher splits off to join Arjan and Lopney: the Shigo incident, his capture and death. **Elysia is left cut off**

### **Arc 5 - "A Sanctuary of Peace and Order":**
- **Arrives late** to the assault (she didn't learn of it in time); that is why she is absent from the start of the fight against Land
- Land transforms and **defeats Policia and Uragaeshi**. At that moment **Elysia enters and defeats Land**: she becomes **Bellsaw** (Land's dead creator) and beats it with a **low deception**
- **Saves Policia and Uragaeshi**, both dying, getting them out of there. **Reunion with Uragaeshi** after the battle
- Learns that **Cristopher killed Shigo**: frustration at the revenge taken from her
- **Resolution (editor's reading, pending author confirmation):** she never gets her revenge; instead she arrives in time to **save the living**, the daughter she has left and the husband she believed dead: the treasure she did not see

### **Arc 6 - "The Dark God Arises":**
- **Survives.** Together again with her husband and Policia
- **Sings again**
