## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Satsuki 皐月 Raynor
**Codename:** Policia (from her fighting style and her clothing: she dresses and fights like a police officer)
**Age:** 21 (Arc 2), a little older than Light (~19)
**Occupation:** Free Elite (三傑); **Free's teacher** (inherited the post from Tairin at ~18)
**Nouryoku:** **None**
**Family:** Mother **Elysia** (Yumi Akina); father **Uragaeshi** (Kevin Raynor), "the Martyr of Free", believed dead, actually alive in Vista Alegre; younger sister **Suilan** (Kelen Raynor). English and Japanese names because her parents have both
**Status:** The only Elite who survives Arc 2

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

**Build:** Quite tall, good figure

**Hair:** **Black base with a light pink/purple streak**, worn tied up

**Eyes:** Purple

**Clothing:**
- **A police uniform in black and purple** (not blue), with **cap**, **baton** and full kit
- **The uniform was her father's.** Uragaeshi's was blue; Elysia gave it to her as a gift, and Policia **dyed it black in honor of her dead father** and modified it to her taste
- **Signature feature: the cap.** Recognizable by silhouette alone, even in a small panel or against the light

**Visual reference:** the author shared an image by another artist as a guide (not stored in the repo). Usable cues: the black hair with the pink/purple streak, the tall confident figure. The character is based on it, not copied from it

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Intelligent and serious.** More mature than Light and Suilan
2. **The "know-it-all" of the trio.** Loosens up a little in private, but stays the clever one
3. **Hates passivity.** Believes Free should act more actively against Pristania, or against the world
4. **Distrustful and cunning**
5. **Overanalyzes**
6. **Can lie to your face, subtly.** Unpredictable
7. **Duty and intelligence in the air around her, but not a rock**
8. **Cool, empowered swag**

### **What Enrages Her:**
- **The state things are in.** Passivity. Free hiding instead of acting

### **What Terrifies Her:**
- **That something happens to her sister.** It does (Suilan dies in Arc 2)

### **Formative Weight: The Martyr's Daughter**
- **Pride** in her father
- After Uragaeshi's "death", **Elysia fell into a deep depression**. Policia had to **grow up fast** and start actively contributing to the household
- She, her mother and her sister had to make their own way in the world

### **Lines She Will Never Cross:**
- **She will never accept her companions sacrificing themselves for her**
- **She will never accept being looked down on for having no Nouryoku**

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Childhood**
- Daughter of two war-era Elite: father **Uragaeshi** and mother **Elysia**
- **Remembers her father vaguely.** He was captured by Pristania during the 3-Year War (when she was ~3-6) and presumed dead; Free calls him "the Martyr of Free"
- Grew up in a trio with **Light and Suilan**, today the whole Elite

### **Training**
- **Combat:** trained by **Tairin**
- **Intelligence:** self-taught, studying and solving real problems

### **The Attack (5 years before Arc 1, ~16)**
- Was in Free during Pristania's attack on the old HQ and survived

### **Elite and Teacher**
- **Entered the Elite by passing the specialized test** (Factions: entry is by test or by an Elite's endorsement). Like her father, an Elite with no Nouryoku, admitted for intellect and skill
- **Inherited the post of Free's teacher from Tairin at ~18**, because of her intelligence. The children of Free need to learn the basics somewhere

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **No Nouryoku**

### **Style: Pure Gadgetry**
- Fights like a real-life police officer
- **She makes her own equipment:** an inventor

### **Arsenal:**
- **Pistol** of her own making, firing **rubber bullets** (lead cannot be obtained in Free)
- **Baton**
- **Handcuffs**
- **Electroshock**
- **Wires**
- **Tear gas**
- **Smoke grenades**
- **Flashbang grenades**
- Any other invention the situation needs

### **Weakness:**
- **Having no Nouryoku.** Enemies can exploit it
- (Practical: her electroshock needs charging. With Free's power cut in Arc 2, **Light charges it**)

### **Signature Fight: Policia vs. Ichiro (Arc 2 climax) - editor's proposal, approved**
- Ichiro's base Saimin controls **only two of the five senses at once**; the canonical counter is to trust the other three (ICHIRO_KIRYUIN_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- Policia doesn't break the illusion: she **floods reality with signals through all five senses**, so the two he controls can't cover everything
- **Context:** in the first fight (the shop) Ichiro beat her with assassin tactics. She overanalyzes and hates losing ground, so she **prepares**, and she has already deduced the rescue plan
1. **Before they arrive**, she strings wires along the corridor to the cells, tied to bells/cans. Illusory copies don't trip them; the real Ichiro does, and it rings
2. **Ichiro covers Arjan with illusions** so he can pass, which spends part of his capacity
3. **Tear gas.** Copies don't cough; the real Ichiro does. If he covers sight and hearing, he can't also cover smell, tears and coughing
4. **Smoke grenade.** The real body displaces the smoke; illusions don't
5. **One hit is enough.** Ichiro is physically frail: a rubber bullet or the electroshock, then handcuffs
- Ichiro loses with dignity: he was protecting Arjan

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Suilan ("hermana"):**
- **Overprotects her.** Believes Suilan **isn't ready for the Elite yet**, but gives her the chance anyway
- They don't fight. **Policia and Light are Suilan's idols**
- They complement each other: security vs. insecurity, no Nouryoku vs. Nouryoku
- **Suilan dies in Arc 2** (killed by Shigo after seeing Tased dead). Policia's worst fear comes true, and she is left with the question of whether she was right that Suilan wasn't ready

### **Light:**
- Trio-mate since childhood. He is the carefree one; she is the serious, more mature one
- He calls her "agente", "maestra"
- **Light is fine living hidden with his loved ones; Policia hates the passivity.** A real tension inside the trio, never resolved, only glimpsed
- He dies the same night as Suilan. Policia mourns both her trio-mates at once
- (See LIGHT_RAYDEL_LINARES_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

### **Elysia (Mother):**
- **No resentment.** Policia understands the situation; both miss her father
- Normal mother-daughter love
- Elysia's depression is why Policia had to grow up fast

### **Uragaeshi / Kevin Raynor (Father):**
- Remembers him vaguely; wears his uniform, dyed black in his honor
- Believes he is dead (the Martyr of Free). **He is alive in Vista Alegre**, where he freed Gabriella and has hidden with her (GABRIELLA_CENTERFOX_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- **Reunion at the start of Arc 5**, when all the allies gather before the assault on Pristania (the same gathering where Megami reveals herself to Free)
- A **happy reunion**: he is overjoyed she is well and tells her he loves her. **She tells him Suilan died** (URAGAESHI_KEVIN_RAYNOR_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- They fight side by side in the Arc 5 assault (see Arc Role)

### **Tairin:**
- **Mentor.** Trained her in combat; handed her the teacher's post
- **Policia respects Tairin deeply**

### **Ichiro:**
- **Distrusts him for being a Kiryuin.** She is well-read and knows what the Kiryuin family is capable of. Also distrustful and cunning by nature
- Her sister is his **stage partner** at Terror's theater: an overprotective sister watching a Kiryuin share a stage with Suilan every day
- Arc 2: loses to him once (the shop), beats him in the climax (see Combat)

### **Meiji:**
- **Disagrees** with his decision to hide Free again after the attack, but has little voice in the matter

### **Lopney:**
- **Notices** that Lopney is also a fighter without a Nouryoku. It makes her **curious**: they are mirrors
- Arc 2: arrests Lopney for the attack on Tased and gains the upper hand on her, before Ichiro intervenes
- The natural scene for her move from distrust to trust (or a truce) with the protagonists

### **The Protagonists:**
- **Partly blames them** for Arc 2: beating the Elite left Free weak right before the attack. **Distrusts them**

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- **The adult mirror of Lopney:** what a fighter with no Nouryoku can become through skill alone (Power System: *Skill > Raw Power*)
- **Action vs. hiding:** the voice inside Free against passivity. After Arc 2, with Free in ruins, her "we have to act" weighs much more
- **Inherited identity:** her codename, silhouette and cap all come from a father she believes dead, who is alive
- **Was she right?** She thought Suilan wasn't ready, gave her the chance anyway, and lost her
