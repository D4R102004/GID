# **LIGHT (RAYDEL LINARES) - COMPLETE CHARACTER PROFILE**

---
ROLE: Supporting (Arc 2 arc boss)

STATUS: Dies Arc 2 (beaten by Claudia, finished off by Shigo)
AGE_ARC_2: ~19
---

## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Raydel Linares
**Codename:** Light (from his Nouryoku: he manipulates electricity)
**Age:** ~19 (Arc 2); ~14 at the Pristania attack 5 years before Arc 1
**Occupation:** Free Elite (三傑), under Tairin's command
**Rank:** No formal leader among the Elite (Light, Suilan, Policia), but Light is the most powerful of the three
**Family:** Adoptive son of Tairin, raised in her orphanage; grew up in the same home as Lyon and Tased. Biological parents unknown
**Status:** Arc 2 arc boss. Not a villain: a perfect enforcer who does his job too well

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

**Build:** Fairly tall, slim and athletic

**Face & Hair:**
- **Blond hair**, short and spiky, standing on end
- **Light blue eyes**

**Signature feature:** **Two lightning-bolt earrings.** This is what a reader recognizes him by in a panel

**Electricity at rest:**
- Not visible when he isn't fighting
- He can make it visible at will, but rarely does
- No burn scars, no glowing eyes

**Clothing:**
- Light, fashionable clothes, dressed like the young people of his time
- **The Elite has no uniform**

**Visual reference:** `LIGHT_visual_reference.png` (same folder) is **inspiration only, not the canonical design.** The character is based on it, not copied from it. In particular, the cyan crystal on the forehead is **not** part of Light's design. Usable cues from it: the blond spikes, the light blue eyes, the yellow/black/cyan electric palette, the casual-fashionable silhouette

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Joking, cocky, relaxed.** Strongest with his fellow Elite, where he is the one who gets the most out of everyone
2. **Deadly serious about orders.** Not fully carefree: when he receives an order, it is the most important thing in the world
3. **A genius with a purpose.** Being a prodigy is, for him, proof that he can make the difference for Free. He feels it as a duty
4. **Demands more of himself for being the strongest.** Calm about it most of the time, but the responsibility weighs on him
5. **Trusts his teammates fully.** He does not try to do everything for Suilan and Policia
6. **Tests himself in combat.** He wants to know how far his power goes
7. **Holds grudges** (see Terror)
8. **Carefree at times**, despite all of the above

### **What Drives Him:**
- Protecting Free, which is his country, his land. He never had another
- Proving that his talent makes a difference

### **What Enrages Him:**
- **Losing.** He is furious with the enemy *and* with himself

### **What Terrifies Him:**
- **Not being able to protect his loved ones**

### **Formative Wound:**
- The Pristania attack 5 years ago (most of the orphanage's children killed, Gabriella captured) showed him how dangerous the world outside is and how responsibly he has to act for Free

### **Line He Will Never Cross:**
- **He would never leave Free.** Loyal to the end

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Origins (before the war against Kami)**
- **Abandoned as a baby in Vista Alegre.** Free picked him up while it still operated in that city
- Nothing is known about his biological parents
- Raised in **Tairin's orphanage**. Terror was never involved with the orphanage

### **Childhood**
- His Nouryoku was known from a young age
- Called a **prodigy** because he showed great skill in combat and strategy from childhood
- Grew up in his own trio with **Suilan and Policia** (Elysia's daughters), who are today the whole Elite
- Friend of Gabriella and Lyzander, but not part of their trio (Lyon, Gabriella, Lyzander)
- Grew up in the same house as **Lyon**; they never got along
- As a child he admired the war Elite: **Uragaeshi, Elysia and Terror**

### **The Attack (5 years before Arc 1, age ~14)**
- Survived Pristania's attack on the old HQ
- Most of the orphanage's children died; Gabriella was captured and presumed dead

### **After**
- Tairin and Terror's marriage ended. **Light blames Terror for leaving Tairin**, and has disliked him since
- **Joined the Elite after the move to the island**

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Light**
- **Generates electricity in his hands**
- **Electrocutes whoever he touches**
- **Conducts it through conductive objects**
- **Can charge electrical appliances**

**What he CANNOT do:**
- Gain speed, turn into lightning, or control devices

**Output point:** **Only his hands.** Seal his hands and you take away his Nouryoku. This is how Arjan beats him (spheres around his hands)

**Weaknesses:**
- **Sealed hands** (see above)
- **Insulators** (rubber, plastic, dry wood, ceramic, glass)
- **Water favors him:** fighting him wet is a bad idea

### **Equipment:**
- **Extendable metal staff:** conducts his electricity to opponents at range

### **Combat Style:**
- **Martial arts prodigy.** Prefers close combat
- **Signature move:** grab the enemy and discharge into them
- Uses the staff for reach, but his instinct is to close the distance
- Fits the Power System rule *Skill > Raw Power*: a simple power in the hands of a fighter who only needs to touch you

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Tairin (adoptive mother, "jefa"):**
- Raised him in her orphanage; he still lives with her
- His hardness is hers, taken further
- She is one of the three who mourn him most

### **Suilan ("Su") & Policia ("agente", "maestra"):**
- **His trio since childhood, and today the whole Elite**
- Dynamic: Light is the carefree one who gets the most out of the others; **Suilan** is insecure but tries to do everything as well as she can; **Policia** is intelligent and serious
- The closeness between the three is very palpable
- Suilan dies the same night (killed by Shigo). **Policia is the only Elite who survives**, and mourns both her trio-mates at once
- **Light charges Policia's electroshock** (he can charge appliances); the trio repairs Free's power failure together in Arc 2
- Tension inside the trio: Light is happy living hidden; Policia hates the passivity (see POLICIA_SATSUKI_RAYNOR_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

### **Elysia:**
- One of his childhood idols (war Elite)
- Today: **respect** (she has earned it from most of Free) and **mutual affection**; she is his friends' mother
- One of the three who mourn him most

### **Terror:**
- One of his childhood idols (war Elite)
- Today: **dislike.** He blames Terror for leaving Tairin
- In Arc 2 Terror lies to the Elite to cover for Ichiro

### **Lyon:**
- Raised in the same house; **they never got along**
- Light considers him **a danger to Free because of the Kyousen**
- Past clashes over it (he reprimands Lyon in Lyzander's flashback of the attack)
- Uncomfortable truth: Lyon's Kyousen did wound Gabriella just before her capture. Light is right about something nobody wants to say aloud
- Lyon leaves Free in Arc 2, which crosses Light's one red line

### **Tased:**
- Raised in the same house. **Not much of a relationship**
- In Arc 2 Light wakes him with electricity to get a name out of him ("Ichiro")

### **Cristopher:**
- Light thinks he is **"un grande por gusto"**: all size, little else (his arrogance shows)
- Doesn't attack him, out of respect for Elysia's affection for Cristopher

### **Meiji:**
- **The man who made his wonderful life possible.** His president
- Light doesn't mind living hidden as long as it is with his loved ones

### **Kensuke/Protos:**
- **Does not suspect him.** Nobody does: he is the founder of the organization

### **Gabriella & Lyzander:**
- Friends, but from a different trio

## **ARC 2 ROLE (ARC BOSS)**

### **0. Opening Flashback (short)**
1. Baby Raydel is picked up by Free in Vista Alegre
2. He is told he is a genius; he reflects a little on what that means to him
3. He watches the war Elite (Uragaeshi, Elysia, Terror) and admires them
4. Explosion behind him: Pristania's attack on the old HQ (he is ~14)

### **5 Years Earlier (Lyzander's flashback)**
- Appears briefly to reprimand Lyon over past Kyousen clashes

### **The Elite Hunt**
- Summoned by Tairin with Suilan and Policia
- Wakes Tased with a brutal electric shock; gets only "Ichiro"
- Searches Terror's bar with Suilan (Terror, whom Light dislikes, lies to them)
- **vs. Lopney:** holds back at first to see what she has up her sleeve, then fights normally. **Light wins** and leaves her unconscious; she tells him Tased attacked her by mistake, he doesn't believe her
- Ichiro barely escapes him after beating Suilan
- Captures Lopney and takes his two companions to the hospital

### **Life in Free**
- Interacts with Arjan during his training
- **Light with Tairin:** mother and son; his hardness is hers, taken further
- Keeps hunting Ichiro, who stays hidden

### **Climax**
- Leaves his post at an entrance to back up his companions; **Arjan finds him waiting**
- **Arjan vs. Light: the hardest fight of the arc.** Arjan wins by encapsulating Light's hands in spheres
- **Reaction: a tantrum.** No recognition of Arjan whatsoever
- **→ Event from the past of Zenshin, Megami and Kami** (arc structure)

### **Death**
- Already beaten by Arjan, **he still rises to fight the Emperors**, and dies as Elite
- **Claudia (Dragoon) beats him** and leaves, convinced nothing more is needed (she does not kill)
- **Shigo finishes him off** behind her back and **takes his DNA**
- **None of his own see him die**
- Last words on panel: *"Todos ustedes están muertos."*
- Mourned most by **Tairin, Elysia and Policia**

### **After Arc 2**
- **Shigo uses Light's Nouryoku in Arc 4** (cross-reference: SHIGO_TAKESHI_KONDO_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- **The child who wanted to be like the Elite dies as Elite.** The flashback opens with him admiring the war Elite; the arc closes with him dying alone, with no one to tell him he did well
- **The cost of winning (Arc 2 theme):** Arjan's victory over him leaves Free weakened right before the attack. Light's death is part of that price
- **Hardness without cruelty:** he treats every stranger as Pristania because that is his job and Free's trauma, not because he enjoys it
- **Loyalty vs. leaving:** Light, who would never leave Free, dies defending it the same night Lyon leaves
