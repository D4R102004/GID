## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Kelen Raynor
**Codename:** Suilan 水濫 ("water overflowing"), taken from her Nouryoku
**Age:** 18 (Arc 2). Younger than Light (~19) and Policia (21)
**Occupation:** Free Elite (三傑); **singer** at Terror's bar/theater, stage partner of Ichiro
**Nouryoku:** Water expulsion from the fingers
**Family:** Mother **Elysia** (Yumi Akina); father **Uragaeshi** (Kevin Raynor), "the Martyr of Free", believed dead, actually alive in Vista Alegre; older sister **Policia** (Satsuki Raynor)
**Status:** Dies in the Arc 2 climax, killed by Shigo after seeing Tased dead

### **The Glasses**
All three functions apply (editor's proposal, approved):
1. **Prescription glasses.** A nearsighted sniper. Without them her precision drops; a smart enemy goes for them
2. **Shield against her own water.** At high speed her water splashes and rebounds; the lenses keep her sight clear while she fires or floods
3. **Hiding her gaze.** Her eyes are more intimidating than she feels. **She takes them off when she gets serious** (a strong visual beat)
- **Payoff:** Policia learns of her death by finding **the glasses, broken and bloodstained** (see Death)

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Shy and insecure, but gives everything she has.** Tries to do everything as well as she can
2. **Speaks softly.** Apologizes, sometimes when there is no need
3. **Formal**, but ordinary in how she talks
4. **Two people:** offstage she is quiet; **on stage she speaks up and is decisive**. Singing is where she feels she can be loud
5. **Occasional outbursts ("prontos")**, mostly with Light

### **What Enrages Her:**
- **Not being good enough.** She takes it out on herself, never on others

### **What Terrifies Her:**
- **Not measuring up** (no dar la talla). To her sister, to Light, to Tairin who recommended her

### **Why She Sings:**
- **Elysia used to sing.** After Uragaeshi's "death" she never sang again
- Suilan **learned to sing and works at it for her mother**

- Youngest daughter of two war-era Elite, **Uragaeshi** and **Elysia**
- **Never knew her father.** She was a baby when he was captured by Pristania during the 3-Year War. She wishes she had known him
- Grew up with Elysia's depression and a sister who had to grow up fast (see POLICIA_SATSUKI_RAYNOR_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- Grew up in a trio with **Light and Policia**, today the whole Elite. **Light and Policia are her idols**

### **The Attack (5 years before Arc 1, ~13)**
- Was in Free during Pristania's attack. **Lived through it hidden with her mother**

### **Singing**
- Learned to sing for Elysia, who stopped singing after Uragaeshi's "death"
- Performs at Terror's bar/theater; stage partner of Ichiro (he does magic, she sings)

### **Elite**
- **Entered the Elite by Tairin's endorsement** (Factions: entry is by test or by an Elite's endorsement). Tairin recognized her talent
- **She knows**, and it weighs on her: she does not want to disappoint the commander of Free's forces
- Contrast: **Policia entered by the test**, and believes Suilan wasn't ready yet. The two doubts (Policia's and Suilan's own) are the same question from both sides

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Water Expulsion (from the fingers)**

**Core Ability:**
- **Generates water from nothing** and expels it through her fingers
- Can produce **a great deal of water**

**Applications:**
1. **Pressure shots.** Thin, fast jets. **They don't cut**, but a fast jet to the chest hurts a lot. Fine and fast enough, a shot reaches very far: **like a sniper**
2. **Barrages.** Combines water with her speed to fire rapid volleys
3. **Flooding.** Can flood whole spaces
4. Other imaginative uses of volume and pressure

**Limits:**
- **Fatigue.** She cannot expel water endlessly; prolonged use wears her down
- **Needs pauses to recharge** between bursts

### **Style: Real-Life Sniper**
- **Fully ranged and precise.** Fast, but **never fights hand to hand**
- Without her glasses her precision drops (see The Glasses)

### **Matchups (Arc 2):**
- **vs. Ichiro (Elite Hunt):** **Ichiro wins**, badly hurt
- **vs. Lyzander (climax):** **double advantage for Suilan**
  - **Elemental:** Lyzander's ice is solidified blue fire; her water puts out the flame before it can set
  - **Range:** his fire cannot leave his body (flamethrower, no projectiles); she is a sniper who never closes in
  - His only way in is solidified shields or bridges, and her water douses them first
  - **Suilan is winning when the explosion hits.** The interruption spares her from finishing him
- **vs. Shigo:** dies (see Death)

### **Inherited by Shigo (Arc 4):**
- Shigo takes her DNA and **uses her water against Arjan and Lopney** in Arc 4 (SHIGO_TAKESHI_KONDO_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Policia ("hermana"):**
- Her idol, together with Light. With her Suilan is **quite submissive**
- Policia overprotects her and believes she isn't ready for the Elite, but gives her the chance anyway
- They don't fight. They complement each other: security vs. insecurity, no Nouryoku vs. Nouryoku
- **Finds out about her death through the broken, bloodstained glasses**

### **Light ("Su"):**
- Trio-mate since childhood and her idol. Her insecurity shows most with him, but **she snaps at him sometimes**
- Light is the carefree one who gets the most out of the others (LIGHT_RAYDEL_LINARES_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- Searches Terror's bar with him in the Elite Hunt. Both die the same night

### **Elysia (Mother):**
- **Sings for her.** Elysia was the singer; she stopped after Uragaeshi's "death"
- Hid with her during the attack 5 years ago
- One of the two people her last words are for
- Elysia is devastated by her death and wants revenge (TASED_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

### **Uragaeshi / Kevin Raynor (Father):**
- **Never knew him**; she was a baby. **Wishes she had known him**
- Dies believing he is dead. He is alive in Vista Alegre; she never learns

### **Tairin:**
- **Recommended her for the Elite** because she recognized her talent
- Suilan speaks to her with respect and is afraid of disappointing her

### **Ichiro:**
- **Stage partner** at Terror's bar: he does magic, she sings. **Good collegial relationship**, professional rather than close
- Arc 2: he tries to convince her to help him; she refuses (orders to arrest him); they fight and **he wins**
- **She lives it as a betrayal and cannot understand it**

### **Tased:**
- **Friendship.** They get along
- Watches over him in the hospital during Arc 2
- **Sees him dead at Shigo's hands and fights Shigo**, which costs her life

### **Lyzander:**
- Arc 2 climax: he tries to convince her; when he fails, he fights her to free the prisoners. **She is winning** when the explosion hits
- Meiji then orders her to gather the rest of the Elite

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- **"Did I earn it?"** Entered by endorsement, not the test. Her sister doubts she was ready; she doubts it herself. Facing Shigo alone is the moment she decides to measure up
- **Was Policia right?** Policia gave her the chance and lost her. The question stays open for Policia
- **Two voices:** quiet offstage, loud only on stage. A sniper fights the same way, from a distance and unseen
- **Singing for a silenced mother:** she keeps alive what Elysia lost with Uragaeshi
- **A father she never knew**, alive the whole time she believed him dead
- **Deliberate mirror of Claudia (author):** shy, glasses, yellow eyes, entered with someone's backing, daughter of an elite fighter. Not a flaw: the difference is the side each fell on. They stay apart through their flaw (Claudia's silence vs. Suilan's stage voice), killing (Claudia won't; Suilan dies fighting), fathers (a present fanatic vs. a martyr she never knew), power tier, and fate. Art note only: differentiate the two in silhouette
