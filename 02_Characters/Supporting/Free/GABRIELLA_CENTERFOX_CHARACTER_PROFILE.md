## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Gabriella Centerfox
**Nickname:** Metal (from her Nouryoku)
**Alias in Vista Alegre:** The "Metal Knight" / "Armor Bastard" (Pristania believes the knight is a man)
**Age:** 20 (Arc 1). Was 15 when captured, 5 years before Arc 1
**Birth:** Born in peacetime, **before** the 3-Year War (~20 years before Arc 1). A little older than Lyzander and Lyon
**Occupation:** Free Security member (joined at 15, just before her capture). Now a lone fugitive and vigilante in Vista Alegre
**Affiliation:** Free
**Family:** Tairin (Delara Centerfox, mother), Terror (father)
**Nouryoku:** Iron materialization from her own body
**Status:** Believed dead by Free for 5 years. Alive, hidden in Vista Alegre. **Dies in Arc 4**

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

- **Cheerful, lively, with "chispa" (spark), optimistic.** This is who she was at 15 and **it is still who she is at 20**
- **Somewhat haughty**
- **Straight to the point:** tells people their truths
- **More mature at 20**, but **more insecure**, because she now knows the magnitude of what she is facing
- **The soul of the Lyon-Lyzander-Gabriella trio**

### **What Drives Her:**
- She **wants to return to Free**. She cannot (Free is hidden and she has no way to find it), so she **helps Free however she can**
- She keeps **hope of defeating Pristania once and for all**
- She fights for **the friends she loves**

### **What Terrifies Her:**
- **Being recaptured by Pristania for life**, with no possibility of ever seeing her friends again
- She is **traumatized** by what she suffered in captivity. She **fights that trauma every day** by facing Pristania and taking risks

### **What Makes Her Happy:**
- Talking with her best friend "Misty" (Claudia) when bored, and playing volleyball with her
- Practicing **new ways to use her Nouryoku**
- **Drinking at bars**: a habit from her old life (her father owned a bar)

### **When Afraid:**
- Her speech **breaks up** ("se entrecorta") and she **acts differently**
- Her Nouryoku can **fail** when fear breaks her concentration. She has to convince herself and push through it

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Childhood in Free:**
- Born in peacetime, before the 3-Year War, to Tairin and Terror. Raised with "outmost care" by both
- **Tairin:** very strict. Taught her manners and wanted her to become an exemplary person, but loved her deeply and was a **caring mother**. She loved seeing her daughter with Lyzander and Lyon
- **Terror:** "a softie" (un pan). The playful dad. She teased him with jokes and he was childish with her, but he constantly **challenged her to improve herself**. They even held **friendly combat**. She snapped at him often, while she was **more reserved with Tairin**
- Trained by Tairin **together with Lyon and Lyzander** (Lyzander's profile). All three developed their Nouryoku early
- The trio was **inseparable**. She spent a lot of time at Tairin's house. She was "the soul" of the group (Lyon's profile)
- **Joining Free Security was the trio's shared dream**; Lyon's profile notes she was especially passionate about it
- Joined Security at **15**

### **The Attack (5 Years Before Arc 1):**
- Pristania attacks Free's original headquarters. Gabriella (15, newly in Security) is **captured**
- Free presumes her dead. Lyzander woke up to find her missing (his worst memory)

### **Captivity:**
- Held at **Pristania HQ** for **several months**
- Her captors were **Perlereina and Diominiciel Dei**
- Kept as a **hostage** (leverage against Free)
- Suffered **mistreatment**; her health became very bad
- She **only ever saw the room she was confined in**. She does **not** know where Pristania HQ is or what it looks like inside

### **The Escape:**
- The Kamigami decide to move her to **their mansion** and from there to a **hospital**, because her health was so poor
- **Uragaeshi**, infiltrated in the Kamigami's mansion, discovers her there
- He frees her, **at first only to hurt Pristania**. They did not know each other
- Once she is healed she tells him **who her mother is**, and he tells her **who he is**
- **Uragaeshi got her out**

### **Life in Vista Alegre (roughly 4.5 years, approximate):**
- Lives **alone** in a hidden refuge inside an **abandoned building**
- Sees Uragaeshi from time to time to **plan upcoming operations**
- **Pristania actively hunts her.** Bellsaw, a commander in the Vista Alegre police, has told the police nothing (they would suspect him)
- She gathers **information on Pristania's movements** and **hinders Pristania's plans and those of the Vista Alegre police**, without any contact with Free
- She could not return to Free, and neither could Uragaeshi: **Free is a hidden secret organization, exactly like Pristania**, and they have no way to find it
- Had to use her Nouryoku **in real time** to survive: her skills improved and she found more ingenious ways to use them
- As the **Metal Knight / Armor Bastard** she wreaks havoc on Vista Alegre's lesser organizations and on its police

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Metal (Iron Materialization)**

**The Power:**
- She **materializes iron from her own body**: from any part, but **mainly the hands**
- The iron **comes out of the skin** and takes shape. **It does not hurt and leaves no mark**
- **Shape is free**: blades, spikes, pipes, barriers, armor, shields, spears, and rods long enough to run through an enemy

**Rules (author-confirmed):**
- **Attached iron:** while it stays attached to her she can mold it at will. She can also **make it retract**, molding it into nothing so it leaves no trace
- **Detached iron:** once it separates from her she can no longer control it. It is just **iron, permanent, does not dissolve**
- **No quantity limit.** In practice she holds only 1-2 blades because she has two hands
- She **cannot fire projectiles**, but she can **form a spear or blade, detach it and throw it by hand**; once released it is uncontrolled
- **Rod range:** she can extend it a long way if she wants, but **not kilometers** (author's reference: Gin Ichimaru's blade from Bleach). No number fixed
- Consistent with the system rule that Nouryoku have no inherent stamina cost: her limits are concentration, pain, distance and neutralization

**Limits & Weaknesses:**
- Being **cornered**
- **Interrupting her creation of metal** causes her **pain**, because shaping metal is a *process*. Cutting off the flow hurts; **damage to already-formed attached metal does NOT hurt her**
- **Loss of concentration** makes attached metal vanish unconsciously. **Distraction is a valid tactic** against her
- Being **beaten at range**
- Being **neutralized**
- **Trauma:** fear of Pristania can make her Nouryoku fail

### **Combat Style: The Iron Swordswoman**
- Primary style: **materialize a sword and fight as a swordswoman**
- Also spears, spikes, and a full **armor** around her body
- **Ingenious techniques (developed in Vista Alegre):**
  - Iron emerging from her **feet**, traveling along the **ground** to the target and impaling it
  - **Shield** of metal to block attacks
- **Training:** from Terror and Tairin she learned **martial discipline and psychological preparation**, everything a Free Security member needs. In Vista Alegre she learned to **put it into practice**
- Real-time survival experience: she adapts her power creatively rather than relying on raw force

## **ARC 4 ROLE & DEATH**

### **First Contact:**
- The protagonists investigate the **Metal Knight / Armor Bastard** of Vista Alegre: an armored knight with helmet and sword who wreaks havoc on lesser organizations and the police
- They believe the knight could **help them fight Pristania**
- **Shock:** the knight is **Gabriella**. This is how the protagonists find her
- Lyzander tells her about Lyon

### **Structure:**
- **All the events of Arc 4 happen between finding her and her death**

### **Death (author-defined):**
1. After the fall of the Vista Alegre police department, **Angel** guides **Lyzander and Gabriella** to a building holding **intel on Pristania's Emperors**
2. On arrival Angel **reveals himself as an Emperor**, boards a **giant mecha (megazord-style)** and fires an **energy beam** at both
3. Gabriella **blocks it with a metal shield**. It is too strong and she knows she cannot hold
4. She **opens a path for Lyzander to escape** before she is **brutally wounded**
5. Lyzander goes to her. With her **last breath** she asks him to **save Lyon** and gives him **metal wings** so he can fight Angel
6. **Claudia is not present**

**The Wings:**
- They work as a **jetpack**: Lyzander can **fly** with them (not merely hover), **powered by his blue fire**
- Consistent with her rules: she forms and **detaches** them before dying, so they persist as plain iron (attached metal would vanish when she loses concentration)

### **Aftermath:**
- **Claudia** fights Yonoa in that same period, ends up in a **coma**, and only wakes **after the assault on Pristania**, when she learns her friend is dead
- **Lyzander** carries the loss (see his profile). Lyon learns in Arc 5
- **Tairin** learns her daughter was alive only to lose her again [TBD: how and when she learns]
