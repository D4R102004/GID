## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Ezequiel Serrano
**Codename:** Terror (from his Nouryoku, also named Terror). No kanji, on purpose: a foreigner keeps a foreign name
**Age:** 40s (Arc 1), similar to Tairin
**Occupation:** Owner of **Terror's Bar** at Free HQ (bar with a performance stage). Retired from Free Security
**Former roles:** One of the first Free Elite (三傑) with Elysia and Uragaeshi, during the 3-Year War
**Family:** Ex-husband of **Tairin** (Delara Centerfox); father of **Gabriella** Centerfox. Reunites with Tairin in Arc 4
**Origin:** Foreigner. Former member of the criminal underworld (mafias) in his home country
**Status:** Supporting. Arjan's mentor in Arc 2, alongside Cristopher

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Warm and cheerful.** Jokes constantly; the same playful man he was as Gabriella's father
2. **Rough, frightening exterior; a great friend inside.** Someone you can lean on
3. **Protective.** What matters to him now is protecting his loved ones and making their lives happier
4. **Stubborn and distrustful** (flaws)
5. **Unpredictable.** Capable of controversial decisions
6. **Lives by threat** in a fight, an underworld habit (see Combat Style)

### **What Drives Him:**
- **The bar**, and the fact that thanks to it there is more happiness in Free
- Protecting the people he loves

### **The Grief (central tension):**
- After Gabriella's "death" he became fixated on avenging her. That fixation broke his marriage
- He **resigned from Security because Free decided to hide** (Meiji would not risk open war again). Unable to get revenge from inside, he chose to bring joy to his comrades through the bar instead
- **Five years later** the resentment is still alive, but he has **accepted that Gabriella is dead**. He lives peacefully now
- **Irony (editor's note):** he is the one who truly finished grieving (Lyzander denies, Tairin hardened, Lyon is consumed by guilt), which is exactly why the Arc 4 truth hits him hardest

### **Habits:**
- **Does not drink** (a bar owner who doesn't drink)
- **Smokes.** Started after Gabriella's death
- **Keeps some of Gabriella's belongings**, as Tairin does

### **His Past:**
- Free knows about his mafia past. **He doesn't care**

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Origins**
- A foreigner involved in the **mafias of his home country's underworld**
- **Fled his country** when the police learned too much
- Ended up in **Vista Alegre**

### **Joining Free**
- In Vista Alegre he met **Tairin**, who **brought him into Free**
- Met **Elysia** and **Uragaeshi** inside the organization
- One of the very first members. **Terror, Elysia and Uragaeshi became the first Free Elite (三傑)**; Tairin was Head of Security (TAIRIN_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- Married Tairin before the 3-Year War; **Gabriella** was born in peacetime, before the war

### **The 3-Year War**
- Fought as Elite. The bond between the old Elite and Tairin is genuine: comrades who trusted each other with their lives on the battlefield
- **Uragaeshi's capture and presumed death** devastated him (brother-in-arms)

### **Fatherhood**
- With Gabriella he was "a softie" (un pan): the playful dad, childish with her, but always challenging her to improve. They held friendly fights. She snapped at him more than at Tairin (GABRIELLA_CENTERFOX_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- He and Tairin taught her martial discipline and psychological preparation

### **The Bar (before the attack)**
- The bar already existed at the old HQ: the trio (Gabriella, Lyzander, Lyon) played there as children, and Terror, Lyon and Lyzander were in it the day of the attack (ARC_2_SUMMARY.md)

### **The Attack (5 years before Arc 1)**
- Carried Lyon and Lyzander out of the attack (Lyzander had been knocked out by a Kyousen bomb)
- Before the attack he distrusted Shigeru, Kami's former disciple
- Gabriella was captured and presumed dead

### **After**
- Fixated on avenging Gabriella. **Tairin ended the marriage**: the relationship became a mutual failure and they stopped seeing each other the same way
- **The real reason for the divorce is a secret of the couple.** Nobody else knows it
- **Resigned from Security** because Free decided to hide. Devoted himself to the bar (rebuilt at the new HQ on the island)

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Nouryoku: Terror**

**Ability:** Materializes the **ghost of a black saber-toothed tiger** with **human, sulfur-colored eyes** and **phosphorescent parts**. A true demon

**Mechanics:**
- The ghost can be **incorporeal or corporeal**
- He can **materialize and dematerialize it instantly**
- **Short range:** he cannot project it far from himself
- Used to **frighten**: seeing it up close, for an instant, while it roars, can stop anyone's heart
- Also used to **fight at close range**

**Cost:** None (consistent with the general rule: Nouryoku has no inherent stamina cost)

**Weaknesses:**
1. **Habituation:** opponents who get used to the fear lose the opening it creates
2. **Damage feedback:** if the ghost is attacked, **Terror takes the damage**
3. **Short range**

### **Combat Style:**
- **Scare, then strike:** uses the ghost to freeze the opponent and attacks in the moment they can't react
- **Lives by threat**
- Knows how to fight from his **mafia past**, and also has **learned technique**

### **Power Level:**
- **Not rusty** despite five years behind the bar. Roughly **Tairin's level**

- Today they act **like friends, with some friction**. Tairin can confide in him

- See Backstory. He believes her dead for five years and has accepted it
- Never sees her again alive (Arc 4)

### **Ichiro:**

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 2 - "The Land of the Free":**
- **Night one:** Ichiro recommends Terror to Lopney (he knows everyone); the bar is empty. Later Terror is there for Lyon: tells him to look after himself and Lyzander
- **Flashback (5 years earlier):** in the bar with Lyon and Lyzander, distrusts Shigeru; carries both boys out of the attack; present when Lyzander wakes
- **The Video:** shows Ichiro the footage of "Yonoa" destroying the electrical installations; two hypotheses (Yonoa works with Pristania, or an impostor wore her face)
- **The Elite Hunt:** lies to the Elite that Ichiro isn't working today
- **Arjan at the bar:** hides the wounded Ichiro in his room; receives Arjan; tells Kensuke Ichiro isn't here and that Arjan is a new kid Cristopher beat up. Becomes Arjan's mentor alongside Cristopher (Tairin assigns Arjan to Cristopher as punishment; Terror also takes charge out of fondness)
- Shows Lyzander where Ichiro is
- **Life in Free:** Arjan lives at his bar; Suilan sings there; crosses paths with Tairin over Arjan (Gabriella's absence felt, never stated)
- **Climax:** fights **one of Jitsumoto's daimyos** during Jitsumoto's attack on Free. **[Daimyo and fight details: TBD]**
- **Aftermath:** badly wounded

### **Arc 3 - "5 Souls to the Rescue":**
- No active role. Can appear in cutaways to Free (recovering)

### **Arc 4 - "Into the City of the Misbegotten":**
- Learns that Gabriella was alive **when it is too late**: never sees her
- His thirst for revenge revives, but above all **his love for Tairin revives**
- Learns that **Gabriella felt guilty for causing her parents' separation** and wished to reunite them (she learned of the separation from Lyzander, who relays her wish)
- **He lets go of revenge.** He and Tairin **get back together, for their daughter**

### **Arc 5 - "A Sanctuary of Peace and Order":**
- **Reunion with Uragaeshi** at the allies' gathering: **warm**; they catch up on several things, but there is no time to celebrate: they must assault Pristania HQ to rescue their leader
- **Fights Land (Heavy Unit L411D)** with **Tairin, Policia and Uragaeshi**. The four disable the tank, but **Land reveals another form** and Terror is **incapacitated**

### **Arc 6 - "The Dark God Arises":**
- **Survives.** Reconciled with Tairin, he looks with the others toward the new future between Free, Pristania and Jitsumoto

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- **Grief done right, then reopened:** the only one who accepted Gabriella's death, so the truth costs him the most
- **The daughter who reunites her parents:** her absence separated them; her last wish brings them back
- **Revenge set down:** he chooses love over vengeance, a counterpoint to Tairin's hardening, Perlereina's devotion and Lyzander's obsession
- **The demon who is a softie:** the most frightening Nouryoku in Free belongs to its warmest man
- **Joy as resistance:** in a hiding, traumatized Free, his bar is where people go to rest
