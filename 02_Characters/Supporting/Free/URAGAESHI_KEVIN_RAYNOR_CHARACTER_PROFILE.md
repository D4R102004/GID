## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Kevin Raynor (the surname is a deliberate homage by the author)
**Codename:** Uragaeshi (裏返し: "turned inside out", to switch sides). From his role, not a Nouryoku: he was the Elite's **spy**
**Known as:** "The Martyr of Free" (believed dead since the 3-Year War)
**Cover identity:** **Ulises Wolmark**, an old man working as the **janitor** of the Kamigami's mansion
**Age:** Late 40s (Arc 1), a bit older than Terror and Elysia
**Occupation:** Infiltrator and saboteur inside Pristania; formerly Free Elite (三傑) with Terror and Elysia
**Family:** Wife **Elysia** (Yumi Akina); daughters **Policia** (Satsuki Raynor) and **Suilan** (Kelen Raynor, dies Arc 2)
**Origin:** A farm near Vista Alegre
**Nouryoku:** None originally. Since the war: **Megami's power (Cartoon Transfiguration)**, from Mei's DNA injected in Pristania's experiments, with limited uses (see Powers)
**Status:** Supporting

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

### **In Free (war era):**
- Well-kept, with the air of a **secret agent**
- **Long dark hair, tied back**; **red eyes**
- **Blue uniform** (today worn by Policia, dyed black in his honor)

### **Today:**
- **Older.** Visibly **not at his best**: a man who has **suffered experimentation**
- He can **change his appearance** with his power (it hurts to hold a form; see Powers)
- When infiltrated he looks like **Ulises Wolmark**, an old janitor

**Echoes in his daughters:** Suilan evokes his secret-agent look (glasses, formal buttoned clothes); Policia carries his uniform and his gadget-based, Nouryoku-free fighting

**Visual reference:** the author shared an image by another artist as inspiration for his Free-era look. The character is based on it, not copied from it; canon is the description above

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
1. **Decisive and determined.** Somewhat serious
2. **Active**: what he lived through made him a man who has to be doing something
3. **Inquisitive and analytical.** He finds out what lies behind things
4. **An iron sense of duty**
5. **Liberty** is the core of who he is
6. **Methodical**

### **What Drives Him:**
- Believes **he is the only one who can make a difference** and help Free **from inside** Pristania
- The experiments and the years alone only made him **more determined**

### **Flaws:**
- **Too pragmatic.** Sometimes misses the nuances
- **Overestimates himself**
- **Can be cold**
- Still feels fear and other human emotions

**Editor's note:** "only I can make a difference" and "overestimates himself" are the same flaw. Arc 5 tests it: Free's plan depends on him knowing where Pristania HQ is, and he doesn't

### **His Power, as He Knows It:**
- He does **not** know it comes from Megami. He does not even know **who Megami is**
- He only knows he survived something, and that now he can do these things (until Arc 5)

## **BACKSTORY**

### **The Farm**
- Grew up on a **farm near Vista Alegre**, where he spent his first eighteen years
- His **grandfather** taught him to hunt and shoot
- His **father** taught him to fight and to stand up to bullies, and also where the limits were
- A good student who was also handy with the farm machinery; learned to drive all kinds of vehicles

### **Intelligence**
- Joined an **intelligence agency**, wanting to become a secret agent
- Got in, but realized it was not what he wanted: **he wanted liberty**
- **Joined Free** for that reason

### **Free and Elysia**
- One of the very first members. **Terror, Elysia and Uragaeshi became the first Free Elite (三傑)**; Tairin was Head of Security
- **The Elite's spy**: espionage and covert operations
- Met **Elysia** in Free. He became interested in her power; a working relationship that escalated
- Daughters: Policia, then Suilan

### **Capture and Experimentation (3-Year War)**
- **Captured by Pristania** during the war, when Suilan was a baby. Free believed him dead: "the Martyr of Free"
- **Bellsaw and Diominiciel experimented on him**, injecting part of the **DNA of Mei** (Megami), Diominiciel's mother
- The DNA came from **objects of hers left in Pristania**: **Diominiciel keeps mementos of his mother**
- They used a prisoner because **they didn't care if he died**
- **Pristania believes the experiment failed and the prisoner died**

### **Freed by Gallan**
- **Gallan freed him on Hirohiko's orders** (before Hirohiko's amnesia and imprisonment, right after the treaty). To Gallan, he was just "a Free prisoner"
- Uragaeshi was in a deplorable state, barely able to make anything out: **he knows someone freed him, but not who**
- Gallan took him to **Vista Alegre**, to be treated at the hospital

### **The Fall**
- He escaped from the hospital by **jumping from the 34th floor**. He bled, but **did not die**: the cartoon physics of Megami's power saved him
- The hospital staff believed him dead

### **Why He Never Returned**
- After the war, **Free moved**: Pristania knew the war-era HQ. Uragaeshi does not know where Free is now
- **There is no way to find Free** (it is hidden exactly like Pristania; not even the Vista Alegre police can find it)
- He could have started a new life. **His sense of duty did not allow it**: the best he could do was **sabotage Pristania**

### **Ulises Wolmark**
- Hid, and over time learned about the **Kamigami's mansion**
- Took the appearance of an old man, **Ulises Wolmark**, and began working there as the **janitor**, infiltrated

### **Gabriella (~5 years before Arc 1)**
- Found **Gabriella** held at the mansion and freed her, at first only to hurt Pristania
- Once she healed, they learned who each other was (Tairin's daughter; her mother's old friend)
- Became her **mentor and savior** (not a father figure). They plan operations together; both want to return to Free and cannot find it (GABRIELLA_CENTERFOX_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Megami's Power: Cartoon Transfiguration (via Mei's DNA)**

**Ability:** The same power as Megami (Power_System.md): cartoon logic. **Its limit is imagination**: in theory he can do anything, including imitating a Nouryoku he sees (it is not a copy mechanic; if he sees someone with a bomb, now he can make bombs too)

**The Battery (his catch):**
- He can only use it **a limited number of times**, like a phone battery
- **Recharges by resting**, at intervals
- **Only Mei** can use it without limit

**Pacifism Lock (inherited):**
- **He cannot kill**
- His power turns even his **guns' bullets into cartoon bullets** that do not kill
- **He knows it**

**Holding a form:**
- **It hurts**, but he endures it (similar to Rajin, who also holds a form for very long periods)
- Ulises Wolmark is a long-term form

**Survival:** cartoon physics let him survive what should kill him (the 34th-floor fall)

### **Combat Style:**
- **Combines the power with weapons and gadgets**
- Close to **Policia's** style, but with **conventional secret-agent weapons** (think FBI agents)
- Farm skills (shooting, brawling, vehicles) plus spy training
- Methodical and analytical; every use of the power is a tactical decision because of the battery

- Old Elite comrades. **Reunion: warm, like old friends meeting again**

- Experimented on him. Both believe the prisoner died and the experiment failed

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- **Liberty as identity:** he left an intelligence agency because it wasn't freedom, then spent fifteen years alone in the enemy's house because duty would not let him be free
- **The engineered survivor:** Pristania did to him what the Ryakujin did to Kami, Jashin and Megami: a human experiment with a god's power
- **The spy who cannot kill:** the most versatile power in the story, in the hands of a methodical agent, rationed by a battery and forbidden from the killing blow
- **The indispensable man who isn't:** Arc 5's map he does not have
- **The mother's power returns:** Diominiciel stole his mother's legacy for an experiment; it ends up in Free, beside the mother herself
