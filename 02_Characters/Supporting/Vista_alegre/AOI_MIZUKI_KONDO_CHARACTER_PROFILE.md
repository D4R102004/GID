## **BASIC INFO**

**Real name:** Mizuki Kondo
**Name as Empress:** **Aoi**, also the name of her Nouryoku, **葵**
**Age:** ~41 (Arc 1). Perlereina (~35) became her student at ~10, when Aoi was ~16. About **26 years older than Shigo** (15)
**Occupation:** **Sensei of a martial-arts dojo** in Vista Alegre
**Former rank:** Pristania **Empress** (after Kensuke and Shigeru left, alongside **Goro Kiryuin and Bellsaw**, before Kami's death). Resigned after the 3-Year War
**Nouryoku:** **葵 (Aoi)**: generates and controls flowers that cut (see Powers & Combat). Awakened late; she was already a monster without it
**Family:** Younger brother **Shigo** (Takeshi Kondo), her only family. Parents: estranged from her since her youth; father killed in conflicts with Vista Alegre's mafia (SHIGO_TAKESHI_KONDO_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
**Status:** Survives the story

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- **Very Japanese** in features
- **Intense red eyes**
- **Hair:** green with a touch of white, **short and tied up**. Her hair and palette **recall Gallan** (green hair): **intentional**, the reader should associate her with him
- **Athletic, like Perlereina.** Keeps herself very well despite her age
- **Walks with elegance**; gives the presence of being **stronger than she looks**
- **Dresses Japanese**: long-sleeved kimono and a long hakama
- **Colors:** white, green and red
- **Face:** resembles **Shigo** (SHIGO_TAKESHI_KONDO_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- **Visual references:** shared by the author: one for the **palette** (a Pokémon), one for the **silhouette and type of clothing** (fan art of a woman in kimono and hakama). Shape and color inspiration only. Distinctive elements of the references are **not** canon: the tiara, the gohei with bells, the sakura print, the voluminous curly hair; the first reference's haircut and red chest fin

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Then (youth): the dojo yaburi**
- Fighting was **everything** in her life. Cared only about the fight and about being strong, and **had enormous fun fighting**
- Picture her **sitting on a mountain of defeated people**

### **Now (Arc 4):**
- **Strict with her students**
- **Tries to find the fun in life**
- But **carries a great deal of regret** inside, and **doesn't know what to do**
- **Lonely.** She hasn't heard of Gallan, who left long ago, nor of Perlereina since the war
- **Living on inertia, confused**

### **What Changed Her:**
- **Perlereina** showed her there were **more important things in life than fighting**
- **The war** threw her back into who she had been: massacres, piling up bodies. That is what she cannot forgive herself, and what she cannot forgive Pristania

### **What Drives Her:**
- **Doing what is right**, even at the cost of what she loves (she left Pristania and Perlereina for it)
- **Shigo**

### **Fatal Flaw / Wound:**
- The **fall-out with Perlereina** still **eats at her**. She believes she did right, and it still hurts

## **BACKSTORY**

### **The Dojo Yaburi:**
- **Very strong from childhood**
- As a **teenager** she went from dojo to dojo **defeating martial-arts masters one after another** (*dojo yaburi*)
- Her life of fighting caused a **fall-out with her family** at an early age; she went to **live on her own**

### **Kami:**
- One day **Kami came to see her** just as she had **beaten everyone in a dojo in Vista Alegre**
- He told her she was very strong and asked if she felt **on top of the world**
- True to her thirst for a fight, **Aoi attacked Kami**. He repelled her; a **brief encounter**; Kami, using his Nouryoku, **left her immobilized**
- Kami told her he **needed her talent for his daughter**; that he **knew about her and her parents**; that he would give her **a place to make amends with her life**, and **strong opponents** at the same time
- He offered: **train his daughter Perlereina**, in payment for having lost to him. **She accepted**
- Kami put Perlereina under her because Aoi, **even at that young age, was ridiculously strong** (without a Nouryoku)

### **Teacher and Student:**
- What began as an **obligation**, done grudgingly, became a **real teacher-student bond**, then something **maternal**: **like mother and daughter**
- Aoi **trained Perlereina into the fighter she is**
- Perlereina **changed Aoi**: showed her that life held more than the fight
- **Her Nouryoku awoke** during that bond: one day, after a training session, while the two were spending time together, Aoi discovered **葵**

### **Pristania:**
- Began working for Pristania; became **Empress** (after Kensuke and Shigeru left, with Goro and Bellsaw)
- Met **Gallan** there. They grew close, and there were **romantic sparks** between them (once both were adults; Gallan is ~6 years younger) *[TBD: see Open Questions]*
- Both **abhorred the war**; Aoi above all, having known **Kami's idea**

### **The War and the Break:**
- After **Kami's death**, during the **3-Year War**, Aoi saw herself again **committing massacres and piling up bodies**: the dojo yaburi on her mountain of the defeated, now with corpses
- **Fall-out with Perlereina**
- **Joined Gallan and Hirohiko to stop the war** (Hirohiko engineered the peace treaty with high-ranking members including Gallan: HIROHIKO_DEI_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- **Resigned as Empress** at the end of the war: to do what was right, and because she did not align with the decisions the **Kamigami** were making
- Has **not heard from Perlereina since**. Knows what happens in Pristania **only through Shigo**

### **Shigo:**
- Shigo **entered Pristania through her**; Perlereina let him rise to Emperor out of lingering affection for Aoi
- Shigo **doesn't understand why she left** and sees his rank as a way of **redeeming her name**
- They **live together**; she handles the domestic side, and **calls him out** when he overestimates himself

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **Before the Nouryoku:**
- A martial artist strong enough to **beat masters as a teenager** and to be trusted with training Kami's daughter. *[More in her backstory scenes]*

### **Nouryoku: 葵 (Aoi)**
- **Generates flowers** and **controls them**: **hundreds** of them, as projectiles and blades. **They cut**
- **Uses:**
  - **Projectiles** against the enemy
  - A **cutting whirlwind** of flowers
  - **Closing off an area** so the opponent cannot escape
  - Attack, defense, and anything else imagination allows
- **Reference:** Byakuya Kuchiki's **Senbonzakura** (*Bleach*): control of countless cutting petals for many purposes
- **No cost.** She creates the flowers and controls them
- **Weaknesses:** an opponent **faster than the flowers**, or one able to **repel them**
