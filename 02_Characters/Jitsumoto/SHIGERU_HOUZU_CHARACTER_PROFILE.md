## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

**Build:**
- Robust and strong despite his age — not a bodybuilder physique, but a body that reads as still powerful and battle-capable
- Carries himself with visible authority; his presence alone commands respect, not fear or weakness

**Face & Hair:**
- Sharp, distinctly Japanese features
- **Black hair** (long, worn in a traditional style)
- **Orange eyes** — the same warm tone Yonoa inherited from him (she also has light orange eyes; visual proof of lineage)
- Default expression: stern, severe (面構え - recio)

**Distinctive Style:**
- Traditional, ornate Japanese shogun attire — same outfit for daily administration and for combat, no separate "battle gear"
- Gold hair ornament marking his status as Shogun
- Habitually carries a Japanese fan (uchiwa/sensu) as a recurring accessory
- Visual palette leans red/orange/gold — coincidentally (or not) evocative of his phoenix Nouryoku

**Body Language:**
- Solemn, composed, authoritative by default — closely mirrors Kami's own presence and aura
- Does not soften around subordinates or even Yonoa; warmth is expressed through gesture and action, never through overt affection or verbal tenderness
- Reads as caring toward those under him, but always through a lens of reserved, traditional Japanese formality

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**

1. **Self-perceived righteousness** — sees himself as a just man, though not "perfect" (that status belongs to Kami alone in his mind). He considers himself the truest living embodiment of Kami's ideal.
2. **Pragmatic to the point of ruthlessness** — willing to make controversial, even brutal decisions if he believes they serve the greater good.
3. **Disillusioned optimist** — genuinely wants to make the world better as Kami envisioned, but is quietly disappointed by how the world (Free, the Kamigami) has strayed from that vision since Kami's death.
4. **Rigid and stubborn** — technically persuadable, but in practice nearly impossible to move once his mind is set.
5. **Consensus-seeking leader, but only in form** — governs like "a pirate captain," trying to leave no one dissatisfied and earning genuine respect from his Daimyos this way. But this consensus-building is a leadership style, not a reflection of true flexibility — his own private decisions (the Bakuzoku genocide) are made entirely alone, without consulting anyone, not even his wife.
6. **Humble in self-image, if not in practice** — believes himself to be humble and working for the greater good. The narrative treats this self-image with quiet irony ("o eso es lo que él cree").
7. **Occasionally warm/funny** — can drop a joke here and there, but is fundamentally a practical, action-oriented man, not a jester.
8. **Profoundly lonely** — the last true believer in Kami's unaltered ideal, surrounded by people who distorted it (the Kamigami's vengeance) or destroyed it (Meiji). Carries this ideological isolation silently.

### **The Bakuzoku Genocide — His Rationalization:**

Shigeru does **not** experience this as hypocrisy or a crack in his philosophy. He frames it entirely as a justified, closed chapter — a public service, not a sin.

- He did not know the Bakuzoku were Ryakujin experiments. To him, they were simply a primitive, unstable race made dangerous by Kyousen — with rare exceptions like Nantes, whom he never identified as such.
- He saw them as instruments of destruction (objetos de destrucción) that threatened his people and territory.
- Crucially, his motivation was **not purely defensive**: he also coveted the unclaimed Bakuzoku territory (no one approached it out of fear), and exterminating them secured that land for Jitsumoto. This layer is unknown to Yonoa and to everyone except Shigeru himself — a "second truth" that, if revealed, could be even more devastating to her than the genocide itself.
- He does not carry guilt day-to-day. He genuinely believes he performed a public service.
- **The one exception to his certainty:** he never told Taeko, specifically because she was a better person than him and he did not want her to see him differently. This isn't guilt over the act — it's protecting the image she held of him. He carries this secret entirely alone; not even Umeda, his closest confidant, knows.

### **How He Reacts to Being Challenged:**
Rigid and stubborn. Can technically be convinced, but rarely is. Tries to build consensus and leave everyone satisfied when leading Jitsumoto's affairs — which is precisely how he's earned his Daimyos' respect — but once he has personally decided something is right, he does not seek approval or turn back.

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Origins (Pre-Pristania):**
- Born into a wealthy, noble-leaning family. Raised from childhood to govern and bear responsibility — disciplined, but not spoiled.
- Possessed his phoenix Nouryoku from birth, which combined with his upbringing gave him early airs of superiority.
- Joined Pristania to learn from other Nouryokusha and initially viewed Kami as a rival to his own family's standing.
- Meeting Kami taught him a lesson in humility — the first person he ever truly admired and deferred to. Became Kami's Harbinger — his Emperor.
- Cut no ties with his birth family, but became independent from them, mirroring the way he would later leave Pristania: amicably, without rupture.

### **As a Pristania Emperor:**
- One of the three original Emperors alongside Bellsaw and Protos (Kensuke). Of the three, Shigeru is considered the one who **best understood Kami's true ideal** — Bellsaw became a war fanatic, Kensuke eventually defected to Free.
- His combat record under Kami was relatively clean — in Kami's philosophy, killing was a last resort, reserved only for unforgivable acts. The Bakuzoku genocide would later be Shigeru's own first true crossing of that line, made entirely under his own judgment, without Kami to provide a framework.
- His relationship with Kami had genuine warmth. Kami tailored his mentorship to each subordinate's needs; for Shigeru, he provided an image to admire — a savior figure.

### **Leaving Pristania & Founding Jitsumoto:**
- Left gradually, not from any single conflict — after years following Kami, he felt ready to build his own version of the ideal, inspired directly by Kami's example.
- Before leaving, pledged his loyalty would never waver. Kami embraced the decision and gave his blessing — an amicable, warm parting (think Guts leaving the Band of the Hawk, but Griffith embraces him and wishes him well instead of retaliating).
- Founded Jitsumoto with Taeko, a woman with no prior faction ties. Their marriage was one of genuine love — she supported him unconditionally and was, in his own eyes, a better person than him.

### **Kami's Death & The 3-Year War:**
- Reacted to Kami's death with initial shock — he never expected Kami to lose. The grief that followed was profound.
- Believes Kami himself would have accepted the outcome, and clings to that belief because he considers himself the one who understood Kami's ideals best.
- Resents Meiji for killing Kami, and separately resents the Kamigami for tarnishing Kami's legacy with their vengeance-driven war.
- Declares Jitsumoto neutral and becomes the treaty's mediator, shouldering what he sees as the burden of carrying Kami's legacy forward alone. This is the root of his profound loneliness — the last true believer, surrounded by people who betrayed or distorted the vision.

### **Killing Nantes (The Bakuzoku Genocide, ~19 years before Arc 2):**
- Detected a Bakuzoku presence in Marsella's house and went there personally with two subordinates.
- Sent his men after a fleeing figure he believed to be a Bakuzoku female — in reality, this was Marsella escaping with baby Lyon. He never saw her face and never learned the truth.
- Nantes opened the door; Shigeru stabbed him instantly using his Nouryoku (hand transformed into a blade-like wing), without a word exchanged. Nantes screamed for them to flee before Shigeru sent his men after "her."
- Left alone with the wounded Nantes, who tried to attack him. Shigeru finished him off and transformed fully into his phoenix form to burn the house down.
- He never discovered that Nantes was a conscious exception among the Bakuzoku, nor that he had just orphaned a child and destroyed a family.

## **RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Yonoa Houzu (Daughter):**
- Loves her and indulges her (ojou-sama upbringing) but does not coddle her — his affection is expressed through action and provision, filtered through his formal, authoritative Japanese demeanor.
- Trained her personally in Nouryoku control; neither of them ever learned hand-to-hand combat.
- His final words before dying are dedicated to her, Taeko, and above all Kami: he tells her he loves her, that she is now Shogun of Jitsumoto, and to be happy — then sees Kami extending his arm to him with a smile, signaling he did the right thing.

### **Taeko (Wife, deceased):**
- Genuine love, not arrangement. She supported him unconditionally and was, in his eyes, morally better than him.
- Died of illness before the Kami-Meiji Duel, never learning about the Bakuzoku genocide — he specifically never told her, not wanting her to see him differently.

### **Kami (Mentor):**
- The central relationship of his life. Kami gave him the first lesson in humility he'd ever received and became the ideal he has spent his entire life trying to embody and carry forward.
- His last vision before dying is of Kami — the ultimate validation he sought his whole life.

### **Umeda (Advisor/Disciple):**
- Met after leaving Pristania. Umeda is his most trusted confidant and disciple — a deliberate echo of his own relationship with Kami, but now with Shigeru in the mentor's role. An important, trusted voice in his decision-making.
- Does NOT know about the Bakuzoku genocide — no one does except Shigeru himself.

### **Marsella de Sores (Imperial Scientist / his killer):**
- Views her primarily as good company for his daughter — an intermediate relationship, warmer than a typical subordinate but not as close as Umeda. He trusts her completely and has no suspicion of her true identity or intentions.
- Her genuine affection for Yonoa (developed while posing as her best friend) is ultimately what saves Shigeru's life during her attack — she calculatedly chooses to wound rather than kill him, though the wound proves fatal regardless.

### **Meiji (Free's founder):**
- Considers Free's ideology entirely, genuinely wrong.
- Had recurring personal contact with him over the years: as a child playing with Yonoa, present the day Meiji killed Kami, and again five years before Arc 1 at the Jitsumoto-Free cooperation treaty signing. Their final confrontation in Arc 2 is the culmination of a long, complicated thread, not a first meeting.

### **The Kamigami:**
- Resents them for pursuing vengeance rather than honoring Kami's true vision, viewing their war as a betrayal of his legacy.

### **The Daimyos:**
- Governs in consultation with them, seeking consensus and leaving no one dissatisfied — the source of the genuine respect they hold for him. His final authority, however, remains absolute and unchallenged.

## **POWERS & COMBAT**

### **NOURYOKU: PHOENIX (不死鳥) — Codename: Fénix**

**Nature:** Full or partial transformation into a fire phoenix, in the vein of Marco (One Piece).

**Capabilities:**
- **Full transformation:** Body wreathed in flame taking phoenix form. Burns enemies on contact, grants flight.
- **Partial transformation:** Can materialize only parts of his body — turning his hands into wings, his legs into talons/claws — without committing to a full transformation.
- **Ranged attacks:** Can shoot fire, and fire cutting feathers from his wings.
- Considered the **most destructive** of the three original Pristania Emperors (Bellsaw, Kensuke/Protos, Shigeru).
- A fight between Shigeru and Kami would end in Kami's victory, but it would be a close, hard-fought battle.

**Weaknesses:**
- His body can be damaged if an opponent manages to get past/through the flames.
- Water is a genuine weakness — though largely theoretical, since no one in the world of Gid currently uses water as a weapon. A potential Chekhov's gun for later.
- No fixed time limit on the transformation itself — simply being transformed costs no energy. Fatigue only sets in from sustained combat exertion; a long, hard-fought battle will eventually force him out of the form.

**Combat Style:**
- Calculated, methodical — he measures his opponent rather than rushing in, though this can shift depending on context (political mission vs. defending his home/family).
- Favors ranged engagement: keeping distance, raining down cutting feathers, or diving on enemies from above like a bird of prey; can also close in with claws when needed.
- Believes himself superior to most opponents, but — having watched Kami's fatal overconfidence against Meiji — never underestimates a rival.

## **DEATH (Arc 2 Finale)**

**Context:** During the climax of Act 2, Shigeru — believing Free is holding Yonoa captive — has a brief confrontation with Meiji. Pristania then interferes in the crossfire. Their true objective: capture Meiji to kill him and capture Yonoa, decapitating Free's leadership while gaining leverage to force Jitsumoto's compliance.

**The Wound:** Marsella stabs Shigeru, wounding him gravely — but her intent, shaped by the real affection she developed for Yonoa while infiltrating as her best friend, is calculated: to wound him out of the fight, not to kill him. She tells herself it's to avoid sinking to his level, but the truth is her attachment to Yonoa.

**The Sacrifice:** Refusing to let Pristania succeed, Shigeru uses his last strength to summon a giant phoenix, driving Pristania back and preventing Yonoa's capture. Meiji, however, is captured.

**Final Moments:** His last words are for Yonoa — that he loves her, that she is now Shogun of Jitsumoto, and to be happy. He then sees Kami extending an arm to him with a smile, a final sign that he did the right thing.

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- The one character who inherited Kami's true ideal intact and tried to execute it through teaching rather than force — proof the vision itself wasn't flawed, only Kami's authoritarian method.
- A study in unexamined self-righteousness: a genuinely well-intentioned leader capable of ordering genocide without ever perceiving a contradiction in his own morality.
- His death exposes his daughter to the exact kind of painful, complicated truth his own life was built on avoiding — setting up Yonoa's Arc 5 reckoning with the fact that even her flawless father was capable of atrocity.
