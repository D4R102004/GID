# **MASAKI OKU (奥正木) - COMPLETE CHARACTER PROFILE**

ROLE: Arc 3 boss flashback (opens the arc); afterlife as Mauricio's living car
FACTION: None (Kamino). Kami's secret first chosen successor
STATUS: Dead (~22-25 years before Arc 1); her spirit lives on in her car
AGE_ARC_1: N/A (dead). Very young at death (~21-22, close to Mauricio's age then)

## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Masaki Oku (奥正木)
**Age at death:** very young (author: "jovencita, como en la imagen"), close to Mauricio's age at the time (he is in his 40s in Arc 1)
**Occupation:** **Teacher**: she taught Kami's ideology to her pupils. Also a **God's Flag driver** at a professional level
**Family:** **Mauricio Fangio** (boyfriend). She has a family in Kamino; they play no role in the story
**Nouryoku:** None
**Status:** Dead. Her spirit survives in the car she raced in (see The Living Car)

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- **Gentle, simple-looking** face
- **Short brown hair** with bangs, in an **'80s** style
- **Athletic** build, fit from racing
- **'80s fashion**: with or without racing gear, she dresses in the style of that era
- **Signature:** her **helmet**, the same one Mauricio wears today
- Resembles **Mei** a little (important detail; see MAURICIO_FANGIO_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)
- **Visual reference:** shared by the author (anime girl with short reddish-brown hair, white collared shirt). Inspiration only: the character is "based on" the reference, not a copy of it

---

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
- **Gentle and kind-hearted**
- **Naive and insecure**: clueless around important people, including boys like her own boyfriend. She doesn't realize people might get the wrong idea about her
- **Proud**: above all as a racer. Behind the wheel she is **passionate**; she knows the race deeply but tends to come across as **distant**
- The contrast is her core: naive and insecure off the track, proud and passionate on it

### **Faith:**
- Believes in **all** of Kami's ideology: the idea of a **better world** and of **justice**
- Her faith is that of the **average citizen of Kamino**, only lived more intensely

### **What Drives Her:**
- Her **faith in Kami**
- To spend her life **making the world better**

### **What Makes Her Happy:**
- **Fulfilling her dream**
- Being with **her loved ones**
- Feeling that she **contributes to the better world**

### **What Terrifies Her:**
- The fears of ordinary people
- **Losing Mauricio**
- That her life could **fall apart with Kami's death**

### **What Enrages Her:**
- **The world** as it is. In this she is very much like Kami

### **Fatal Flaws:**
- **Pride**
- **Naivety**: she doesn't see how her closeness to Kami looks to others, Mauricio included

---

## **VOICE & DIALOGUE**

- Speaks **like a teacher**: clear, articulate, an intelligent person. **No voseo** (unlike Mauricio)
- **Warm** in conversation, with her **insecurity** underneath
- Behind the wheel: **passion**
- Calls Kami **"Kami-sama"**
- Verbal tics: none for now

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Teacher and Racer:**
- A Kamino girl who **taught Kami's ideology** to children; her pupils loved her
- A very talented God's Flag driver, at a professional level; **second to Kami** in the race
- At first **she was the driver**; Mauricio didn't race. He was her **cheerleader**
- She had other rivals among the drivers; none matter to the plot
- Her car: **an ordinary, old car**, not a race car

### **Kami's First Successor:**
- **Kami chose her as his successor as leader of Pristania**, before Angel
- They met **in secret**; he gave her special lessons in his ideology. At Kami's request she **told Mauricio nothing**
- She was **in love with him in her heart** (pure admiration, maybe something more), but loved Mauricio and stayed loyal
- Kami recognized she had a boyfriend and **did not try to love her** (his attraction is left ambiguous on purpose: MAURICIO profile)

### **Death:**
- Kami told her she was **ready**: she should bring her boyfriend, and they would explain everything together. The plan: **reassure Mauricio about the meetings** and tell him Masaki would be Kami's successor as leader of Pristania
- **On the way home, driving**, she was **hit by an unknown person from Kamino**. **An accident**
- It happened **far from her home**; **Mauricio arrived afterwards**
- She died without Mauricio ever learning why she met with Kami
- Kami, in shock, searched again and later found **Angel** (ANGEL_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

## **RACING (in place of Powers & Combat)**

- **No Nouryoku**: the God's Flag forbids them, and she has none
- **Professional-level driver**; **second only to Kami**
- Deep knowledge of the race; **passion** behind the wheel
- Her edge outlives her: the living car races with Mauricio for decades

---

## **THE LIVING CAR**

- Her **strong will** let her spirit **attach to her car** (spirits with a strong will can adhere to objects after death; no Nouryoku needed. Same rule as Gloria, Arc 5)
- What binds her: **it's her car**, and her **passion for racing**
- Mauricio kept the car after her death and discovered it was **alive**
- **A will without clear consciousness**: like a mute character in a series, except she is a car
- **She knows she is a car. She does not know she was Masaki**, or anything of her past life
- **Does not speak**, but **acts on her own**; she and Mauricio understand each other without words
- Learns **scents** (tracks Ichiro by his scent: ARC_3_SUMMARY beats 11 and 14)

---

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Mauricio Fangio (boyfriend):**
- Met in Kamino, the ordinary way. **Young love** on both sides; a close couple with a normal life together
- He was **her cheerleader** before he ever raced
- She **unintentionally irritates** him by talking about Kami constantly. Mauricio loves Kami too, but in his head she is "too much". He never learns the reason: Kami was instructing her
- After her death he races in her car, wears her helmet, and becomes mayor **to honor her**. His **last words are to Masaki** (Arc 3)

### **Kami (mentor):**
- With her he was as he was with Angel: **understanding, a teacher, a divine presence; kind and paternal**. He was preparing his successor
- She adored him ("Kami-sama"), loved him in her heart, and was above all **happy to be mentored** by him
- **Loyalty** is one of Kami's values: the ideal that drew her to him is what kept her faithful to Mauricio

### **Her pupils:**
- **They loved her.** No plans for them to appear in the present

### **Her family:**
- Exists in Kamino; no role in the story

### **Angel:**
- **Never met.** Angel comes after all of this

### **Other drivers:**
- She had rivals besides Kami; not important to the plot

### **The car and Mauricio (after death):**
- Partners who understand each other without words. Neither knows the car is Masaki. **The car mourns Mauricio** when he dies

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 3 - "5 Souls to the Rescue":**
- **Opening flashback (arc boss flashback):** Masaki, the race against Kami, the secret lessons, "you're ready", the accident
- **Not explained on page:** her spirit in the car; Kami searching again and finding Angel
- **As the car:** Mauricio's partner in the God's Flag; exposes Ichiro at Mauricio's house and learns his scent; Mauricio lends it to the twins; it tracks Ichiro to the clinic and carries the five out of Kamino
- Mauricio's **last words are to Masaki** (the car is far away with the heroes)

### **Arc 4 transition:**
- In Vista Alegre the car sees the news of Mauricio's execution and **races back to Kamino like lightning** (it mourns him; it leaves the heroes)

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- **Kami's ideal lived by an ordinary believer**: a teacher with the average Kamino citizen's faith who wants to make the world better, and who is angered by the world as Kami was
- *Editor's readings (not author canon; use lightly):*
  - Mirror of Angel: both Kami's chosen successors, both trained in secret, both treated with the same kindness (the treatment is author canon; the mirror framing is the editor's)
  - Kami's choice reads as natural: she already passed his ideology on to children
  - Her naivety is what made the secret fragile; Mauricio's jealousy had a cause he never learns
  - She feared her life would fall apart with Kami's death, and died first; Mauricio lives that fear twice (losing her, then Kami)
  - Kami's succession fails twice: Masaki dies before being presented; Kami dies three days before presenting Angel
