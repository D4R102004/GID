## **BASIC INFO**

**Full Name:** Mauricio Fangio
**Age:** 40s (Arc 1), about the age of Free's old Elite (Terror, Elysia, Uragaeshi)
**Occupation:** **Mayor of Kamino** and reigning **champion of the God's Flag**, both since **Kami's death** (~18 years before Arc 1)
**Family:** No one now. He had a girlfriend, **Masaki Oku**, who died (~22-25 years before Arc 1)
**Nouryoku:** None
**Status:** Arc 3 boss

---

## **PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**

- An older man; gives off **veteran, wise** vibes
- Short **black hair**
- **Ordinary features**: he looks like any ordinary person
- **Signature:** the **helmet that belonged to Masaki**, which he likes to wear
- **Visual reference:** an old-style racing driver: leather helmet, pilot goggles hanging at the neck, suit jacket over simple clothes, weathered face, serious distant gaze. Inspiration only; the character is "based on" the reference, not a copy of it

---

## **PERSONALITY CORE**

### **Defining Traits:**
- **Embodies Kami's philosophy:** no to revenge, no to hatred; justice must prevail in the world. Similar to **Shigeru** in this respect
- **Reasonable, not blind faith:** when Ichiro breaks into his house, he lets him go instead of punishing him
- **Plays by the rules**; strong sportsmanship. Sees worthy opponents as worthy opponents, whoever they are
- **Very gentlemanly**, especially with women
- **A good leader**, with a gift for **oratory**, like Kami

### **Faith:**
- A **serene believer**. Kami gave him a homeland; he believes in him and does not question him
- Trusts that a better world is possible and works for it
- **Resents Free**, but the God's Flag winner's wish is Kami's tradition: if the winners are from Free, then they are the winners. Granting it is Kami's justice in action

### **Why He Races:**
- At first he **didn't race**: the driver was **Masaki**
- He races now **to honor her**

- **Folksy, warm** (campechano)

## **BACKSTORY**

### **Masaki Oku (奥正木)**
- A Kamino girl, a **very talented God's Flag driver** and Mauricio's girlfriend. She once **finished second to Kami**
- She was a **fervent follower of Kami**
- **Kami chose her as his successor, before Angel.** He met her in secret and gave her special lessons in his ideology. At Kami's request, she **never told Mauricio**
- She **resembles Mei** a little (important detail)
- Kami recognized she had a boyfriend and **did not try to love her**; he may have felt attraction at some point (left ambiguous on purpose)
- Kami told her she was ready: she should show her boyfriend, and they would explain everything. **On the way home she was run over and killed**
- Kami was left in shock and searched again; he found **Angel** (see ANGEL_CHARACTER_PROFILE.md)

### **The Living Car**
- Masaki's **spirit survived** and **attached itself to Mauricio's car** (spirits with a **strong will** can adhere to objects after death; no Nouryoku needed. Same phenomenon as **Gloria**, who becomes vengeful; Masaki becomes a car)
- The car became a kind of **walking Nouryoku**. Masaki **does not know** she is a car
- Mauricio discovered his car was **alive** right after losing his girlfriend. They **communicate** and get along. He **does not know** it is Masaki
- He sees nothing wrong in racing with it: the God's Flag bans the drivers' Nouryoku, and the car is his partner, not his power

### **Champion and Mayor**
- After mourning, he kept the car and kept racing
- **After Kami's death** (Kami had been Kamino's mayor), Mauricio **won his first God's Flag** and asked for the **mayoralty** as his wish, **to honor Masaki**
- He has been champion ever since. Being mayor and being champion are separate: one can be mayor without being champion

## **KEY RELATIONSHIPS**

### **Masaki Oku (dead girlfriend):**
- The reason he races and the reason he became mayor. Wears her helmet
- Unknowingly races with her spirit every time

### **Kami:**
- No personal relationship: a young man of Kamino who **idolized** him like everyone in town
- Never raced against him

### **Glayne Byronstrike:**
- Rivals for **about 10 years**; Mauricio always wins
- **Strong mutual respect**. Glayne is somewhat **resentful** at never winning
- Glayne races without anyone knowing he is from Pristania
- **Glayne kills him** (Arc 3)

### **Arjan & Lopney:**
- Sees them as **worthy rivals** (sportsmanship)

### **Ichiro:**
- Ichiro breaks into his house; Mauricio lets him go (scene detail: ARC_3_SUMMARY)

### **The people of Kamino:**
- **Well loved**; to many he is a kind of **idol**, since everyone in Kamino likes the race even if they don't watch it
- In the end, **his own town condemns him** as a traitor

## **ARC-BY-ARC ROLE**

### **Arc 3 - "5 Souls to the Rescue":**
- **Arc boss.** The arc opens with the flashback of **Masaki and Kami**
- Mayor of Kamino and God's Flag champion: the protagonists need to **win the race** to have their wish (a ride to Vista Alegre) granted
- The twins win. **Faithful to Kami, he grants their wish**
- His town declares him a **traitor for helping Free** and **jails** him
- **Glayne kills him** as punishment for helping Free
- **His death marks a new stage for Kamino**, now tied to Pristania and its bloody will

## **THEMATIC ROLE**

- What Kami's ideals look like **without Pristania**: a town governing by Kami's teachings out of custom, with justice, sportsmanship and no hatred
- He honors Kami's tradition even when it favors his enemies, and dies for it at the hands of Kami's own organization
